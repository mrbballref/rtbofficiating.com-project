About Our Partnership

Professional support built around the event.

The UAPB Celebrity Basketball Game combines entertainment, school pride, community engagement, and competitive basketball in one high-energy event. RTBO delivers professional officiating and event support that allows participants and organizers to focus on the experience.

Service Requested

Celebrity-game officiating, crew coordination, game-flow management, and event support.

  • Special-event officiating crews
  • Clear pregame coordination
  • Professional game management
  • Community-focused event support

Services Provided

Elite Officiating

Qualified officials selected to match the competition level, event format, and expectations.

Scheduling & Assigning

Organized crew assignments, game coverage planning, and schedule communication.

On-Site Supervision

Leadership support that promotes consistency, professionalism, and efficient event operations.

Official Development

Feedback and accountability that help officials improve while supporting the event.

Game Reporting

Clear incident, conduct, and event reporting when requested by event leadership.
