HOW STORAGE WORKS

Local-First Film Storage

This HTML/CSS/JS build stores uploaded video blobs in your browser using IndexedDB. It does not upload to a remote server.

  • Video remains on this device/browser profile.
  • Deleting browser site data deletes locally stored film.
  • Use Downloads to export source files and metadata.
  • Large video capacity depends on browser/device storage.
