# 1. PRINCIPAL ARCHITECTURE DECISION

The RefShop must be engineered as a:

**HEADLESS + API-FIRST + EVENT-DRIVEN + MULTI-TENANT + DOMAIN-ORIENTED COMMERCE OPERATING SYSTEM**

Do not architect the platform as a conventional monolithic online store.

The platform must begin with bounded enterprise domains and evolve toward independently scalable infrastructure as transaction volume and organizational complexity increase.

The architectural principle is:

**Start with bounded enterprise services; scale into independently isolated regional cells as load, geography, resilience requirements, and organizational complexity justify them.**

The system must not require a complete rewrite when the business grows.
