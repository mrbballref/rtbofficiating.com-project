ABOUT THE FOUNDER

MONTREL SIMMONS

Founder, Raising The Bar Officiating

“We Will Serve, And Will Be Of Service To The Game!”

From Crossett, Arkansas, Montrel Simmons began officiating in June 2008 and founded Raising The Bar Officiating that same year with a mission to give back and elevate the standard of officiating. With over 16 years of dedication, he has committed his life to leadership, education, service, and developing officials at every level.

Key Highlights

  • Officiated at all levels from youth to Semi-Pro including High School, JUCO, NAIA, NCAA DIII, DII, DI, ProAm, and Semi-Pro.
  • Currently works in 2 semi-pro leagues, 3 NCAA DI conferences, 3 NCAA DII conferences, 3 NCAA DIII conferences, 2 NAIA conferences, 2 JUCO conferences, and 6 high school conferences.
  • Officiated 4 JUCO national tournaments, 4 NAIA national tournaments, 1 NCAA DII Regional, 3 NCAA DIII Regionals, and 1 NCAA DIII National Final Four.
  • Coordinator role includes She Got Game League Little Rock ProAm, Arkansas 4A-8 Conference, Conway JV/JH, and an independent NAIA College in Little Rock, AR.
  • Clinician/trainer for Sooner Athletic Conference, Southern Athletic Association, College Conferences of the South, Great American Conference, and the Mid-American Intercollegiate Athletic Association.
  • Served in the United States Marine Corps from 1988 to 1996 including Operation Desert Shield/Desert Storm 1990–1991.
