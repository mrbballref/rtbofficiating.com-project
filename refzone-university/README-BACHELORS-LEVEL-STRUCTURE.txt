REFZONE UNIVERSITY — BACHELOR'S-LEVEL PROFESSIONAL PATHWAY STRUCTURE

This release corrects the prior playlist/module presentation.

Each active Foundations pathway is now presented as a four-year recommended professional degree-level curriculum containing:
- 8 academic terms
- 24 prerequisite-based courses
- 120 RefZone credit-equivalent learning units
- 1,800 total engagement hours
- 2 applied practicum stages
- Integrated capstone and oral defense
- Degree-audit requirement categories
- Academic standing and progression gates
- Course-level workloads, graded deliverables, and assignment requirements
- Rules, mechanics, communication, film, field application, and assessment work

The Coursera-inspired classroom and RefZone iPad video player remain in place. The classroom now displays the academic course code, year, term, unit value, course category, assignments, workload, and grading weights.

IMPORTANT CREDENTIAL SAFEGUARD
This is labeled a “Bachelor’s-Level Professional Foundations Pathway” and “professional degree-level pathway.” The 120 units are RefZone credit-equivalent learning units, not accredited college credits. The program must not be represented as an accredited bachelor’s degree unless legal degree-granting authority, accreditation, or an authorized university partnership is documented and approved in the CMS.
