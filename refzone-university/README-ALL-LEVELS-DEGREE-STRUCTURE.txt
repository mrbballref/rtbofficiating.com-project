REFZONE UNIVERSITY — THREE DEGREE STRUCTURES AT EVERY COMPETITION LEVEL
=======================================================================

This release structures every one of the 20 competition levels as three separate academic programs:

1. Bachelor’s-Level Professional Foundations Program
   - 4 recommended years
   - 8 academic terms
   - 24 required courses
   - 120 RefZone credit-equivalent units
   - 1,800 engagement hours
   - Two practicums, senior capstone, comprehensive examination, oral defense, and portfolio

2. Master’s-Level Advanced Officiating Program
   - 2 recommended years
   - 4 academic terms
   - 12 required graduate courses
   - 36 RefZone graduate credit-equivalent units
   - 540 engagement hours
   - Advanced practicum, comprehensive examination, master’s capstone, oral defense, and portfolio

3. PhD-Level Research, Leadership, and Elite Officiating Program
   - 4 recommended years
   - 8 academic terms
   - 20 required doctoral courses
   - 60 RefZone doctoral credit-equivalent units
   - 900 engagement hours
   - Candidacy examination, dissertation proposal, original research project, final oral defense, and professional contribution portfolio

All 20 competition levels now display all three structures, creating 60 independently addressable program routes.

Use the Program Catalog to open any of the 20 competition levels and choose its Bachelor’s, Master’s, or PhD structure.

CREDENTIAL SAFEGUARD
--------------------
The interface identifies these as degree-structured professional pathways and credit-equivalent learning units. It does not claim accredited or transferable academic degrees or credits unless documented degree-granting authority, accreditation, or an authorized university partnership is verified in the production CMS.
