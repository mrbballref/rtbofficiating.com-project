REFZONE UNIVERSITY COURSE CLASSROOM THEME UPDATE

The Coursera-inspired course workflow has been restyled to match the approved RefZone University website theme.

Updated across every Bachelor’s, Master’s, and PhD classroom:
- Black and charcoal classroom background
- RefZone orange active, progress, button, and focus states
- Metallic-silver iPad frame
- Dark course outline and lesson navigation
- Dark transcript, notes, resources, assignments, syllabus, and video-script panels
- Orange-accented progress, bookmarks, active lessons, quizzes, and source references
- Responsive mobile and tablet presentation
- Accessible contrast and visible keyboard focus states
- Print-friendly lesson output retained

The learning structure, course data, routes, progress tracking, notes, bookmarks, assessments, and video player behavior were not removed or replaced.
