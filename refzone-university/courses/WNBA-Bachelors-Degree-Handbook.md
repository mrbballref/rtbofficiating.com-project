# RefZone University — WNBA Bachelors Program Handbook

**Program:** WNBA Basketball Officiating Bachelor’s-Level Professional Foundations Program
**Credential status:** Professional degree-level pathway; not represented as an accredited academic degree, transferable college credit, WNBA licence, assignment qualification, employment credential, or advancement guarantee
**Season/source status:** 2026 WNBA Official Rule Book current-effective · 2027 centralized Replay Center future-effective
**Structure:** Four-year recommended sequence · eight terms · 24 courses · 120 RefZone credit-equivalent units
**Total engagement hours:** 1800

A complete professional bachelor’s-level professional foundations pathway for WNBA officiating, covering the 2026 WNBA Rules 1-14, Comments on the Rules, mechanics, judgment, replay, Coach’s Challenge, communication, crew-chief leadership, arena operations, film, evaluation, research, and professional women’s basketball practice.

## Program gates
- Correct 2026 WNBA rule, section, exception, penalty, and Comments on the Rules.
- Current-season, effective-date, and assignment-directive control.
- Safety, ethics, integrity, confidentiality, safeguarding, and professional conduct.
- Complete penalty, clock, shot-clock, possession, replay, challenge, and resumption administration.
- Original work, qualified human review, copyright compliance, and accessible delivery.
- No promise of assignment, employment, advancement, licence, certification, accredited degree, or transferable credit.
