# NAIA Men’s Basketball Officiating Master’s-Level Advanced Officiating Program

**Structure:** Two years · four academic terms · 12 graduate courses · 36 RefZone graduate credit-equivalent units · 540 engagement hours  
**Rules implementation cycle:** 2026-27  
**Credential status:** Professional graduate degree-level pathway; not represented as an accredited academic degree or transferable graduate credit  
**Release status:** Complete graduate instructional production draft pending required qualified reviews  

## Graduate admission

- Completed NAIA Men’s Bachelor’s-Level Professional Foundations pathway or verified equivalent competency in current NCAA men’s rules, NAIA procedures, three-person mechanics, contact judgment, timing, replay, communication, and applied officiating.
- Passing graduate placement gates in rules interpretation, penalty administration, mechanics, normal-speed film judgment, clock and shot-clock administration, and professional conduct.
- Verified experience or an approved high-fidelity simulation plan sufficient for graduate practicum work.
- Membership access remains separate from academic admission.

## Graduate program outcomes

- Interpret current NCAA men’s rules through definitions, exceptions, related provisions, approved rulings, corrections, effective dates, replay limits, and verified NAIA administrative authority.
- Resolve complex multi-event plays, penalty sequences, correctable errors, fighting events, timing issues, and atypical situations.
- Demonstrate advanced contact judgment, disciplined correct no-calls, restricted-area understanding, and defensible normal-speed film analysis.
- Perform advanced three-person mechanics, transition, press, timing, shot-clock, media-timeout, replay, challenge, and end-game responsibilities.
- Lead crews, manage conflict, communicate with supervisors, and complete professional incident documentation.
- Evaluate, observe, instruct, use anchored rubrics, and design remediation based on observable evidence.
- Complete graduate practicum, comprehensive examinations, complete-game film defense, capstone, oral defense, and professional contribution portfolio.

## Graduate completion gates

- Complete all 12 graduate courses and 36 RefZone graduate credit-equivalent units.
- Earn at least 85 percent in each course and pass all mandatory rules, replay, clock, shot-clock, safety, ethics, source-control, and professional-conduct competencies.
- Pass advanced interpretation, complex penalty, contact judgment, mechanics, timing, challenge, communication, leadership, evaluator, and film-performance gates.
- Complete the advanced applied officiating practicum, evaluator conference, remediation, and re-demonstration.
- Pass the comprehensive examination, complete-game film defense, master’s capstone, oral defense, and professional contribution review.

## Publication safeguards

- Every lesson, scenario, assessment, diagram brief, narration, dialogue, and video script is original RefZone University material.
- Official NCAA and NAIA publications control accuracy but are not reproduced word-for-word.
- Conference, Continental Athletic Conference, tournament, championship, media, replay, and assigning-authority procedures are taught only after the applicable current policy is attached and validated.
- Course completion does not guarantee certification, assignments, employment, postseason selection, or advancement.
