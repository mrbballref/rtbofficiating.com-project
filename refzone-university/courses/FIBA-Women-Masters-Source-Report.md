# RefZone University — FIBA Women Masters Source Report

1. Current-effective FIBA Official Basketball Rules.
2. Current-effective FIBA Official Basketball Rules Interpretations.
3. Official Basketball Equipment provisions.
4. Current official FIBA rules changes and effective-date notices.
5. Official FIBA Refereeing Material Library and authorized iRef resources.

7. Women’s competition-, event-, Regional Office-, National Federation-, tournament-, and assignment-specific regulations and directives.
8. Official FIBA Women in Basketball and Women in Officiating materials for leadership, inclusion, development, and career-support context.
9. Original RefZone University instruction approved by qualified reviewers.

## Current-effective playing authority
- **FIBA Official Basketball Rules 2024** and **Official Basketball Rules Interpretations 2024** remain the current controlling code until superseded.
- The playing code is generally common to men’s and women’s FIBA 5-on-5 basketball. This women’s pathway does not invent gender-based rule differences. It independently teaches women’s competition administration, event procedures, officiating development, safeguarding, inclusion, and leadership.

## Future-effective authority
- **FIBA Official Basketball Rules 2026** and the official changes document become effective October 1, 2026. They are transition sources before that date and must not be taught as current early.
- The corresponding updated interpretations must be validated upon official publication before affected lessons are released as current.

## Women’s officiating and professional-development context
- FIBA Women in Basketball strategy and Women in Officiating initiatives.
- FIBA Referee Maternity Plan as a career-inclusion framework, subject to National Federation and assignment-specific implementation.
- FIBA Women’s Basketball World Cup 2026 referee preparation communication, including the long-list and pre-competition video-review and communication program, as development context only.
- Completion of a RefZone pathway does not grant a FIBA licence, event nomination, assignment, employment, or advancement.

## Assignment-specific authority
Every assignment requires the current competition regulations, technical-meeting instructions, commissioner directions, table and timing procedures, IRS/challenge procedure, security plan, emergency action plan, broadcast rundown, travel information, and local organizing instructions. General course defaults never override an authorized event directive.

## Copyright and originality
Official publications are reference authorities only. The program uses original RefZone explanations, scenarios, diagrams, assessments, narration, dialogue, research activities, and video scripts. Protected rulebook text, official diagrams, footage, logos, and restricted materials are not reproduced without authorization.
