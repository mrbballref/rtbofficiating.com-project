# RefZone University — WNBA Masters Program Handbook

**Program:** WNBA Basketball Officiating Master’s-Level Advanced Officiating Program
**Credential status:** Professional degree-level pathway; not represented as an accredited academic degree, transferable college credit, WNBA licence, assignment qualification, employment credential, or advancement guarantee
**Season/source status:** 2026 WNBA Official Rule Book current-effective · 2027 centralized Replay Center future-effective
**Structure:** Two-year recommended sequence · four terms · 12 courses · 36 RefZone graduate credit-equivalent units
**Total engagement hours:** 540

A complete professional master’s-level advanced officiating pathway for WNBA officiating, covering the 2026 WNBA Rules 1-14, Comments on the Rules, mechanics, judgment, replay, Coach’s Challenge, communication, crew-chief leadership, arena operations, film, evaluation, research, and professional women’s basketball practice.
