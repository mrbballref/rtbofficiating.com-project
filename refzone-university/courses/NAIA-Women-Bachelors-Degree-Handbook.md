# NAIA Women’s Basketball Officiating Bachelor’s-Level Professional Foundations Program

**Structure:** Four years · eight terms · 24 courses · 120 RefZone credit-equivalent units · 1,800 engagement hours  
**Rules basis:** 2025-26 NCAA Women’s Rules Book and Casebook, current official corrections and interpretations, current-cycle rules changes and points of emphasis, and 2026-27 NAIA administrative procedures  
**Credential status:** Professional degree-level pathway; not an accredited academic degree or transferable college credit

## Completion gates

- Complete all 24 courses and 120 RefZone credit-equivalent units.
- Pass rules, definitions, penalty, clock, shot-clock, replay, mechanics, safety, ethics, and source-control gates.
- Complete both applied practicums, evaluator conference, integrated examinations, complete-game film defense, capstone, oral defense, and professional portfolio.
- Correct all rules-code, penalty, replay, source, and safety errors through documented remediation and re-demonstration.

## Publication safeguards

- Official sources control accuracy; RefZone course language is original.
- NAIA, region, conference, tournament, championship, and assignor procedures must be validated annually and displayed for the learner’s actual assignment.
- Completion does not guarantee certification, assignments, employment, postseason selection, or advancement.
