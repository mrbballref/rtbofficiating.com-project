# NJCAA Women Bachelor’s-Level Review Package

## Required approvals

- NCAA women’s rules reviewer
- NJCAA women’s procedures reviewer
- CCA women’s mechanics reviewer
- Curriculum and assessment reviewer
- Copyright and licensing reviewer
- Accessibility reviewer
- Video-realism reviewer
- Program administrator

## Release gates

- Every Rule 1-11 section and related approved ruling is represented or deliberately mapped to a lesson.
- Current rules changes, points of emphasis, case-book rulings, replay, and coaches’ appeal procedures are validated.
- The CCA mechanics framework and any applicable conference modifications are validated.
- Region, conference, tournament, championship, and assignor policies are attached for the deployment context.
- All student-facing text, cases, diagrams, assessments, dialogue, and video scripts are original.
- All videos meet court, quarter, game-clock, shot-clock, mechanics, continuity, caption, transcript, audio-description, and licensing requirements.
- No program is represented as accredited, transferable, or a guarantee of assignments or employment.
