## Lesson 1: Cylinder principle

- Lesson ID: `fibm-f133-l01-cylinder-principle`
- Video title: Cylinder principle
- Target audience: Developing FIBA men’s officials
- Estimated duration: 10:45
- Rule/source references: FIBA Official Basketball Rules — fouls and general principles; FIBA Refereeing Material Library and authorized iRef instructional resources — post play, block/charge, unsportsmanlike, act-of-violence, and game-management guidelines; Current FIBA rule updates and IRS authority

### Learning objectives
- Explain cylinder principle in original RefZone University language and identify the controlling FIBA or authorized event authority.
- Identify the FIBA season, internal regulations, Official Basketball Rules and authorized competition regulations, source version, effective date, and assignment directive before applying the concept.
- Distinguish a supported legal outcome, an illegal or unsupported outcome, a borderline or unresolved outcome, and a disciplined no-call, no-correction, or withhold-conclusion outcome.
- Apply the correct mechanics, evidence standard, penalty, clock, shot clock, possession, replay, resumption, research, or policy process when applicable.
- Communicate the conclusion briefly, calmly, accurately, neutrally, and without importing terminology from another ruleset.
- Review the work on film or through source evidence and record a measurable next-game, teaching, leadership, or research application.

**Camera angle:** Slow push-in to source timeline and course map

**Action:** The screen identifies Cylinder principle, the applicable event type, and the difference between current-effective, future-effective, and event-specific authority.

**Narration:** This lesson examines Cylinder principle. Begin by identifying the FIBA season, controlling internal regulations, Official Basketball Rules and authorized competition regulations, officiating manual, official interpretation or technical clarification, and assignment directive.
