### Scene 3 · 03:00-05:10
**Learning purpose:** Compare legal, illegal, borderline, and disciplined no-action outcomes  
**Location/camera:** Same court with four original play variations · Four-panel normal-speed comparison with no slow motion initially  
**Action:** Four original examples isolate one decisive fact for Rotation initiation, response, completion, and abort: a legal outcome, an illegal outcome, a borderline view, and a correct no-call or no-correction.  
**Narration:** Compare the plays at normal speed. Do not react only to the final result. For Rotation initiation, response, completion, and abort, locate the first relevant action, identify legal status before it, and decide whether the evidence completes every required element. A dramatic result does not replace rule evidence.  
**On-screen text:** See the first relevant fact  
**Dialogue:** Partner: “My angle does not complete the missing element. Stay with the ruling unless authorized evidence changes it.”  
**Decision pause:** What single fact separates the examples?; Is the available angle complete enough to rule?  
**Ruling reveal:** The ruling follows the complete legal-status and evidence sequence, not appearance, reputation, crowd reaction, or expected outcome.  

### Scene 4 · 05:10-07:30
**Learning purpose:** Apply mechanics, coverage, and administration  
**Location/camera:** Court, scorer’s table, and replay station when applicable · Primary POV, secondary POV, overhead diagram, freeze at status, freeze at decisive fact  
**Action:** The play runs at normal speed, freezes only to verify facts, returns to normal speed, and completes reporting and resumption for Rotation initiation, response, completion, and abort.  
**Narration:** Positioning is part of the decision. For Rotation initiation, response, completion, and abort, establish the matchup, maintain an open look, observe the complete action, and avoid reaching from a secondary position. After the whistle or administrative decision, confirm player, ball, clock, shot-clock, team-foul, possession, replay, and resumption status.  
**On-screen text:** Angle → Evidence → Classification → Administration  
**Dialogue:** Calling official: “I have the complete action. Confirm the clock and restart.”  
**Decision pause:** Who owns the play?; What help is factual and permitted?; What is the complete administration?  
**Ruling reveal:** Use the authorized signal, report accurately, restore every required game condition, and resume without unnecessary delay.
