## Lesson 1: Comprehensive FIBA and FIBA competition-specific-rules examination

- Lesson ID: `fibm-f173-l01-comprehensive-fiba-and-FIBA-adapted-rules-examinatio`
- Video title: Comprehensive FIBA and FIBA competition-specific-rules examination
- Target audience: Developing FIBA men’s officials
- Estimated duration: 10:45
- Rule/source references: FIBA Refereeing Material Library and authorized iRef instructional resources — Official Basketball Rules and authorized competition regulations, Official Interpretations, guidelines, and protocols; FIBA Internal Regulations and authorized event competition regulations — technical rules, competition regulations, disciplinary code, and IRS appendices; FIBA Official Basketball Rules 2024 and future-effective 2026 transition materials

### Learning objectives
- Explain comprehensive fiba and FIBA competition-specific-rules examination in original RefZone University language and identify the controlling FIBA or authorized event authority.
- Identify the FIBA season, internal regulations, Official Basketball Rules and authorized competition regulations, source version, effective date, and assignment directive before applying the concept.
- Distinguish a supported legal outcome, an illegal or unsupported outcome, a borderline or unresolved outcome, and a disciplined no-call, no-correction, or withhold-conclusion outcome.
- Apply the correct mechanics, evidence standard, penalty, clock, shot clock, possession, replay, resumption, research, or policy process when applicable.
- Communicate the conclusion briefly, calmly, accurately, neutrally, and without importing terminology from another ruleset.
- Review the work on film or through source evidence and record a measurable next-game, teaching, leadership, or research application.

**Narration:** This lesson examines Comprehensive FIBA and FIBA competition-specific-rules examination. Begin by identifying the FIBA season, controlling internal regulations, Official Basketball Rules and authorized competition regulations, officiating manual, official interpretation or technical clarification, and assignment directive. Never apply a future-effective FIBA change or a domestic-league procedure without confirmed FIBA adoption.
