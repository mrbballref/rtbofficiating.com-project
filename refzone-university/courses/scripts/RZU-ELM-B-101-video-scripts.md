## Euroleague Basketball competition structure and officiating department

**Estimated duration:** 10:45  
**Target audience:** Developing EuroLeague men’s officials  
**Rule/source map:** FIBA Official Basketball Rules — court and equipment provisions; 2025-26 EuroLeague Bylaws and Unified Scorers Manual

### Scene 1 — 00:00-01:20
**Learning purpose:** Frame the authority, effective date, and learning question  
**Location:** RefZone University digital classroom and source-control desk  
**Camera:** Slow push-in to source timeline and course map  
**Action:** The screen identifies Euroleague Basketball competition structure and officiating department, the applicable event type, and the difference between current-effective, future-effective, and event-specific authority.  
**Narration:** This lesson examines Euroleague Basketball competition structure and officiating department. Begin by identifying the EuroLeague season, controlling bylaws, adapted rules, officiating manual, technical clarification, and assignment directive. FIBA supplies the underlying playing code, but Euroleague Basketball adaptations and competition procedures control when they differ. Never apply a future-effective FIBA change or a domestic-league procedure without confirmed EuroLeague adoption.  
**Dialogue:** Instructor: “Name the controlling event document before you analyze the play.”  
**On-screen text:** Competition → Source → Effective date → Ruling  
**Overlays:** Euroleague Basketball assignment type, Current-effective source, Future-effective source, Human-review gate  
**Decision pause:** Which competition is being officiated? | Which rule code is expressly adopted? | Is the source current effective or future effective?
