## Lesson 1: FIBA Official Basketball Rules 2024 as the controlling playing code

- Lesson ID: `fibw-f102-l01-fiba-rules-as-the-underlying-playing-code`
- Video title: FIBA Official Basketball Rules 2024 as the controlling playing code
- Target audience: Developing FIBA women’s officials
- Estimated duration: 10:45
- Rule/source references: FIBA Refereeing Material Library and authorized iRef instructional resources — Official Basketball Rules and authorized competition regulations, Official Interpretations, guidelines, and protocols; FIBA Internal Regulations and authorized event competition regulations — technical rules, competition regulations, disciplinary code, and IRS appendices; FIBA Official Basketball Rules 2024 and future-effective 2026 transition materials

### Learning objectives
- Explain fiba rules as the underlying playing code in original RefZone University language and identify the controlling FIBA or authorized event authority.
- Identify the FIBA season, internal regulations, Official Basketball Rules and authorized competition regulations, source version, effective date, and assignment directive before applying the concept.
- Distinguish a supported legal outcome, an illegal or unsupported outcome, a borderline or unresolved outcome, and a disciplined no-call, no-correction, or withhold-conclusion outcome.
- Apply the correct mechanics, evidence standard, penalty, clock, shot clock, possession, replay, resumption, research, or policy process when applicable.
- Communicate the conclusion briefly, calmly, accurately, neutrally, and without importing terminology from another ruleset.
- Review the work on film or through source evidence and record a measurable next-game, teaching, leadership, or research application.

### Scene 1 — 00:00-01:20
**Learning purpose:** Frame the authority, effective date, and learning question
**Location:** RefZone University digital classroom and source-control desk
**Court configuration:** Split view of the iPad player, source timeline, and assignment card
**Players:** None
**Officials:** Learner, instructor, and qualified reviewer represented without real-person likenesses
**Official positions:** Not applicable
**Camera angle:** Slow push-in to source timeline and course map

**Action:** The screen identifies FIBA Official Basketball Rules 2024 as the controlling playing code, the applicable event type, and the difference between current-effective, future-effective, and event-specific authority.

**Narration:** This lesson examines FIBA Official Basketball Rules 2024 as the controlling playing code. Begin by identifying the FIBA season, controlling internal regulations, Official Basketball Rules and authorized competition regulations, officiating manual, official interpretation or technical clarification, and assignment directive. FIBA supplies the underlying playing code, but FIBA competition implementations and competition procedures control when they differ. Never apply a future-effective FIBA change or a domestic-league procedure without confirmed FIBA adoption.

**On-screen text:** Competition → Source → Effective date → Ruling

**Dialogue:** Instructor: “Name the controlling event document before you analyze the play.”

**Visual overlays:**

- Current-effective source
- Future-effective source
- Human-review gate

**Decision pause:**

- Which rule code is expressly adopted?
- Is the source current effective or future effective?

**Narration:** Compare the plays at normal speed. Do not react only to the final result. For FIBA Official Basketball Rules 2024 as the controlling playing code, locate the first relevant action, identify legal status before it, and decide whether the evidence completes every required element. A dramatic result does not replace rule evidence.

**On-screen text:** See the first relevant fact

**Dialogue:** Partner: “My angle does not complete the missing element. Stay with the ruling unless authorized evidence changes it.”

**Visual overlays:**
- LEGAL
- ILLEGAL
- BORDERLINE
- NO-CALL / NO-CORRECTION

**Decision pause:**
- What single fact separates the examples?
- Is the available angle complete enough to rule?

**Ruling reveal:** The ruling follows the complete legal-status and evidence sequence, not appearance, reputation, crowd reaction, or expected outcome.

After the whistle or administrative decision, confirm player, ball, clock, shot-clock, team-foul, possession, replay, and resumption status.
