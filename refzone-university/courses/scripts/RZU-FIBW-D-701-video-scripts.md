## Lesson 1: Officiating as an interdisciplinary field

- Lesson ID: `fibw-e701-l01-officiating-as-an-interdisciplinary-field`
- Video title: Officiating as an interdisciplinary field
- Target audience: Doctoral learners, supervisors, researchers, instructors, and policy leaders
- Estimated duration: 10:45
- Rule/source references: FIBA Refereeing Material Library and authorized iRef instructional resources; FIBA Official Basketball Rules and current interpretations; FIBA Internal Regulations and authorized event competition regulations and assignment-specific directives

### Learning objectives
- Explain officiating as an interdisciplinary field in original RefZone University language and identify the controlling FIBA or authorized event authority.
- Identify the FIBA season, internal regulations, Official Basketball Rules and authorized competition regulations, source version, effective date, and assignment directive before applying the concept.
- Distinguish supported, unsupported, unresolved, and ethically withheld conclusions using a defensible evidence standard.
- Apply the correct mechanics, evidence standard, penalty, clock, shot clock, possession, replay, resumption, research, or policy process when applicable.
- Communicate the conclusion briefly, calmly, accurately, neutrally, and without importing terminology from another ruleset.
- Review the work on film or through source evidence and record a measurable next-game, teaching, leadership, or research application.

### Scene 1 — 00:00-01:20
**Learning purpose:** Frame the authority, effective date, and learning question
**Location:** RefZone University digital classroom and source-control desk
**Court configuration:** Split view of the iPad player, source timeline, and assignment card
**Players:** None
**Officials:** Learner, instructor, and qualified reviewer represented without real-person likenesses
**Official positions:** Not applicable
**Camera angle:** Slow push-in to source timeline and course map

**Action:** The screen identifies Officiating as an interdisciplinary field, the applicable event type, and the difference between current-effective, future-effective, and event-specific authority.

**Narration:** This lesson examines Officiating as an interdisciplinary field. Begin by identifying the FIBA season, controlling internal regulations, Official Basketball Rules and authorized competition regulations, officiating manual, official interpretation or technical clarification, and assignment directive. FIBA supplies the underlying playing code, but FIBA competition implementations and competition procedures control when they differ. Never apply a future-effective FIBA change or a domestic-league procedure without confirmed FIBA adoption.

**Dialogue:** Instructor: “Name the controlling event document before you analyze the play.”
