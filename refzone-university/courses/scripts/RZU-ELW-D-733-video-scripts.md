## Competency framework design

**Estimated duration:** 10:45  
**Target audience:** Doctoral learners, supervisors, researchers, instructors, and policy leaders  
**Rule/source map:** 2025-26 FIBA Europe FIBA mechanics and game-official guidance — coaching, evaluation, and report protocols; Authorized FIBA Europe Refereeing and Competitions administration clinic and development materials

### Scene 1 — 00:00-01:20
**Learning purpose:** Frame the authority, effective date, and learning question  
**Location:** RefZone University digital classroom and source-control desk  
**Camera:** Slow push-in to source timeline and course map  
**Action:** The screen identifies Competency framework design, the applicable event type, and the difference between current-effective, future-effective, and event-specific authority.  
**Narration:** This lesson examines Competency framework design. Begin by identifying the EuroLeague season, controlling bylaws, adapted rules, authorized FIBA mechanics guidance, technical and assignment clarification, and assignment directive. FIBA supplies the underlying playing code, but EuroLeague Women competition regulations and authorized event modifications and competition procedures control when they differ. Never apply a future-effective FIBA change or a domestic-league procedure without confirmed EuroLeague adoption.  
**Dialogue:** Instructor: “Name the controlling event document before you analyze the play.”  
**On-screen text:** Competition → Source → Effective date → Ruling  
**Overlays:** FIBA Europe assignment type, Current-effective source, Future-effective source, Human-review gate  
**Decision pause:** Which competition is being officiated? | Which rule code is expressly adopted? | Is the source current effective or future effective?

### Scene 3 — 03:00-05:10
**Learning purpose:** Compare legal, illegal, borderline, and disciplined no-action outcomes  
**Location:** Same court with four original play variations  
**Camera:** Four-panel normal-speed comparison with no slow motion initially  
**Action:** Four original examples isolate one decisive fact for Competency framework design: a legal outcome, an illegal outcome, a borderline view, and a correct no-call or no-correction.  
**Narration:** Compare the plays at normal speed. Do not react only to the final result. For Competency framework design, locate the first relevant action, identify legal status before it, and decide whether the evidence completes every required element. A dramatic result does not replace rule evidence.  
**Dialogue:** Partner: “My angle does not complete the missing element. Stay with the ruling unless authorized evidence changes it.”  
**On-screen text:** Supported · Unsupported · Unresolved · Withhold conclusion

**Ruling reveal:** The ruling follows the complete legal-status and evidence sequence, not appearance, reputation, crowd reaction, or expected outcome.

### Scene 4 — 05:10-07:30
**Learning purpose:** Apply mechanics, coverage, and administration  
**Location:** Court, scorer’s table, and replay station when applicable  
**Camera:** Primary POV, secondary POV, overhead diagram, freeze at status, freeze at decisive fact  
**Action:** The play runs at normal speed, freezes only to verify facts, returns to normal speed, and completes reporting and resumption for Competency framework design.  
**Narration:** Positioning is part of the decision. For Competency framework design, establish the matchup, maintain an open look, observe the complete action, and avoid reaching from a secondary position. After the whistle or administrative decision, confirm player, ball, clock, shot-clock, team-foul, possession, replay, and resumption status.  
**Dialogue:** Calling official: “I have the complete action. Confirm the clock and restart.”  
**On-screen text:** Angle → Evidence → Classification → Administration  
**Overlays:** Primary coverage, Secondary help, Open angle, Clock and shot clock, Penalty and restart  
**Decision pause:** Who owns the play? | What help is factual and permitted? | What is the complete administration?  
**Ruling reveal:** Use the authorized signal, report accurately, restore every required game condition, and resume without unnecessary delay.
