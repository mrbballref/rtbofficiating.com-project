# RefZone University — WNBA PhD Program Handbook

**Program:** WNBA Basketball Officiating PhD-Level Research, Leadership, and Elite Officiating Program
**Credential status:** Professional degree-level pathway; not represented as an accredited academic degree, transferable college credit, WNBA licence, assignment qualification, employment credential, or advancement guarantee
**Season/source status:** 2026 WNBA Official Rule Book current-effective · 2027 centralized Replay Center future-effective
**Structure:** Four-year recommended sequence · eight terms · 20 courses · 60 RefZone doctoral credit-equivalent units
**Total engagement hours:** 900

A complete professional phd-level research, leadership, and elite officiating pathway for WNBA officiating, covering the 2026 WNBA Rules 1-14, Comments on the Rules, mechanics, judgment, replay, Coach’s Challenge, communication, crew-chief leadership, arena operations, film, evaluation, research, and professional women’s basketball practice.
