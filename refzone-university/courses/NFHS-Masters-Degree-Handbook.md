# NFHS Basketball Officiating Master’s-Level Advanced Officiating Program

**Structure:** Two years · four academic terms · 12 required graduate courses · 36 RefZone graduate credit-equivalent units · 540 engagement hours  
**Rules season:** 2026–27  
**Credential status:** Professional graduate degree-level pathway; not represented as an accredited academic degree or transferable graduate credit  
**Release status:** Complete graduate instructional production draft pending required human reviews  

## Program admission

- Completed NFHS Bachelor’s-Level Professional Foundations pathway or verified equivalent competency in current rules, mechanics, judgment, communication, and applied officiating.
- Passing graduate placement gates in rules, penalty administration, three-person mechanics, film judgment, and professional conduct.
- Verified experience or approved simulation plan sufficient for advanced practicum work.
- Membership access is separate from academic admission.

## Graduate program outcomes

- Interpret current NFHS rules through definitions, exceptions, related provisions, cases, mechanics, effective dates, and local authority.
- Resolve complex multi-event plays, penalty sequences, correctable errors, and atypical situations.
- Demonstrate advanced contact judgment, disciplined correct no-calls, and defensible normal-speed film analysis.
- Perform advanced three-person mechanics, transition, press, timing, end-game, and crew-coverage responsibilities.
- Lead crews, manage conflict, communicate with supervisors, and complete professional incident documentation.
- Evaluate, observe, instruct, use anchored rubrics, and design remediation based on observable evidence.
- Complete graduate practicum, comprehensive examinations, film defense, capstone, oral defense, and professional contribution portfolio.

## Graduate completion gates

- Complete all 12 graduate courses and 36 RefZone graduate credit-equivalent units.
- Earn at least 85 percent in each course and pass all mandatory rules, safety, ethics, source-control, and professional-conduct competencies.
- Pass advanced rules interpretation, complex penalty, contact judgment, mechanics, timing, communication, leadership, and evaluator performance gates.
- Complete the advanced applied officiating practicum, remediation, and verified portfolio.
- Pass the comprehensive examination, complete-game film defense, master’s capstone, oral defense, and professional contribution review.

## Publication safeguards

- Every lesson, scenario, assessment, diagram, narration, and script is original RefZone University material.
- Official NFHS publications control accuracy but are not reproduced word-for-word.
- Shot-clock, replay, challenge, tournament, state, and assigning-authority procedures are taught only when the applicable documented policy is attached and validated.
- No course completion guarantees certification, assignments, employment, postseason selection, or advancement.
