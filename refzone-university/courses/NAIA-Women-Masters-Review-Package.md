# NAIA Women’s Master’s-Level Review Package

## Required approvals

- NCAA women’s rules reviewer
- NAIA procedures reviewer
- Collegiate three-person mechanics reviewer
- Replay and coach’s challenge reviewer
- Graduate curriculum and assessment reviewer
- Crew-chief, evaluator, and practicum reviewer
- Copyright and licensing reviewer
- Accessibility reviewer
- Video-realism and production reviewer
- Program administrator

## Release gates

- Every graduate lesson is mapped to current source authority, an assessment, an applied artifact, and a complete video package.
- Current corrections, interpretations, approved rulings, points of emphasis, clock, shot-clock, replay, challenge, and table procedures are validated.
- Conference, Continental Athletic Conference, tournament, championship, venue, media, and assigning-authority policies are attached for the deployment context.
- All student-facing explanations, scenarios, case plays, assessments, dialogue, diagram briefs, and video scripts are original.
- All videos meet collegiate court, shot-clock, mechanics, continuity, caption, transcript, audio-description, accessibility, and licensing requirements.
- Mandatory source, rules, safety, ethics, replay, administration, practicum, remediation, comprehensive-examination, and oral-defense gates are functional.
- The program is not represented as accredited, transferable, or a guarantee of certification, assignments, employment, postseason selection, or advancement.
