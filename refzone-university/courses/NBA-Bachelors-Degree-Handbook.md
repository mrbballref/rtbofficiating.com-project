# RefZone University — NBA Bachelors Program Handbook

**Program:** NBA Basketball Officiating Bachelor’s-Level Professional Foundations Program
**Credential status:** Professional degree-level pathway; not represented as an accredited academic degree, transferable college credit, NBA licence, assignment qualification, employment credential, or advancement guarantee
**Season/source status:** 2025-26 NBA Official Playing Rules current published authority · 2026-27 playing rules and operations pending official publication
**Structure:** Four-year recommended sequence · eight terms · 24 courses · 120 RefZone credit-equivalent units
**Total engagement hours:** 1800

A complete professional bachelor’s-level professional foundations pathway for NBA officiating, covering the 2025-26 NBA Rules 1-14, Comments on the Rules, mechanics, judgment, replay, Coach’s Challenge, communication, crew-chief leadership, arena operations, film, evaluation, research, and professional NBA basketball practice.

## Program gates
- Correct 2025-26 NBA rule, section, exception, penalty, and Comments on the Rules.
- Current-season, effective-date, and assignment-directive control.
- Safety, ethics, integrity, confidentiality, safeguarding, and professional conduct.
- Complete penalty, clock, shot-clock, possession, replay, challenge, and resumption administration.
- Original work, qualified human review, copyright compliance, and accessible delivery.
- No promise of assignment, employment, advancement, licence, certification, accredited degree, or transferable credit.
