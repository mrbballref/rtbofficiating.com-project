## Effective-date control

- FIBA Official Basketball Rules 2024 remain current through September 30, 2026.
- FIBA Official Basketball Rules 2026 v1.1 become effective October 1, 2026.
- The EuroLeague Women 2026-27 regular season begins after October 1, so the 2026 rules are expected to control the competition unless FIBA Europe issues an authorized contrary directive.
- The platform must keep 2024 and 2026 content separately versioned and activate the 2026 material only on its effective date.
- New or revised interpretations must be validated before publication or assessment use.

## EuroLeague Women competition controls included

- The competition is governed and administered by FIBA Europe.
- The 2026-27 Regular Season contains 16 teams divided into four First Round groups of four.
- The top three teams from each First Round group advance to two six-team Second Round groups, carrying First Round results.
- The top four teams in each Second Round group reach the best-of-three Play-Ins.
- Six teams reach the Final Six, with two teams receiving byes into the semifinals through the Semi-Final Play-Ins.
- EuroLeague Women games use three referees and neutral commissioners under the competition regulations.
- Event-specific eligibility, official-ball, court-layout, media, roster, accreditation, and game-day requirements are maintained as separate administrative content rather than being confused with playing rules.

## Women in officiating and leadership

The curriculum includes women-in-officiating leadership, mentoring, inclusion, professional development, bias awareness, language and cultural competence, and institutional-development topics. These subjects supplement, but never replace, the controlling playing rules, regulations, mechanics, and appointment directives.

Student-facing explanations, scenarios, assessments, scripts, and diagrams are original RefZone University material. No official publication is reproduced word-for-word. Public release requires qualified rules, mechanics, competition, copyright, accessibility, and program-administrator approval.
