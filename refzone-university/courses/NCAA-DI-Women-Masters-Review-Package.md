# NCAA Division I Women’s Master’s-Level Program — Review Package

## Rules review
- [ ] Every lesson cites the correct current NCAA women’s source version.
- [ ] Rule conditions, exceptions, penalties, resumption, clock, shot clock, possession, replay, and challenge consequences are verified.
- [ ] Women’s and men’s rules terminology is not mixed.
- [ ] Division I administrative procedures are not misrepresented as universal playing rules.

## Mechanics review
- [ ] Lead, trail, center, rotations, transition, press, free throws, throw-ins, reporting, and crew communication are checked against authorized current mechanics authority.
- [ ] Conference or assignor mechanics are labeled by jurisdiction.
- [ ] Video positioning and signals are accurate.

## Assessment review
- [ ] All 180 lesson assessments have one defensible answer and an accurate rationale.
- [ ] Comprehensive examinations sample rules, penalty administration, mechanics, replay, communication, leadership, and film judgment.
- [ ] Practicum and oral-defense rubrics use observable anchored criteria.

## Video and realism review
- [ ] All 180 lesson scripts use regulation court geometry and stable game-state data.
- [ ] Player movement, ball movement, contact, official positioning, signals, reporting, clock, shot clock, possession, and resumption remain consistent.
- [ ] Every major play shows normal speed, primary angle, secondary angle, overhead analysis, decisive freeze, slow-motion fact review, and return to normal speed.

## Copyright and academic-integrity review
- [ ] Explanations, cases, questions, diagrams, dialogue, narration, and overlays are original RefZone University work.
- [ ] No protected rulebook, case-book, mechanics, broadcast, logo, likeness, or music asset is used without authorization.
- [ ] Official fact, RefZone analysis, inference, and unresolved questions are clearly distinguished.

## Accessibility review
- [ ] WCAG 2.2 AA keyboard, focus, semantic, contrast, scalable-text, and error requirements are met.
- [ ] Every video has captions, searchable transcript, audio description, and a non-video alternative.
- [ ] Diagrams and assessments do not rely on color alone.

## Final publication gate

- [ ] Division I administration reviewer approved.
- [ ] Mechanics and replay reviewers approved.
- [ ] Curriculum and assessment reviewers approved.
- [ ] Copyright and accessibility reviewers approved.
- [ ] Video reviewer approved.
- [ ] Program administrator approved.
