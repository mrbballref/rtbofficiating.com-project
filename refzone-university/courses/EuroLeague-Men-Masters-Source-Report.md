# EuroLeague Men Master’s-Level Source Report

**Rules cycle:** 2026-27 transition cycle
**Status:** Complete instructional production draft pending qualified review

## Source hierarchy

1. Current EuroLeague bylaws, adapted rules, technical rules clarification, IRS appendix, officiating manual, and assignment directives.
2. Current-effective official playing-rules code and official interpretations.
3. Current Euroleague Basketball Officiating Department mechanics, coaching, evaluation, and game-management materials when authorized.
4. Current arena, unified scorers, digital scoresheet, TV, security, and game-operations directives.
5. Original RefZone University instruction approved by qualified reviewers.

## Effective-date control

- FIBA Official Basketball Rules 2024 remain current through September 30, 2026 unless superseded.
- FIBA Official Basketball Rules 2026 become effective October 1, 2026.
- The 2026 interpretations must be validated when published.
- Future-effective rules are taught as transition material, not as current before their effective date.
