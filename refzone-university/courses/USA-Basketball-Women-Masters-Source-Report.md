# USA Basketball Women Master’s-Level Source Report

**Rules cycle:** 2026-27 transition cycle
**Status:** Complete instructional production draft pending qualified review

## Source hierarchy

1. Event-specific USA Basketball or FIBA competition regulations, technical-meeting materials, and assignment directives.
2. Current-effective official playing-rules code and official interpretations.
3. Current official mechanics and iRef development materials when authorized.
4. USA Basketball Youth Guidelines and domestic-event rules when the assignment is a USA Basketball youth event.
5. Original RefZone University instruction approved by qualified reviewers.

## Effective-date control

- FIBA Official Basketball Rules 2024 remain current through September 30, 2026 unless superseded.
- FIBA Official Basketball Rules 2026 become effective October 1, 2026.
- The 2026 interpretations must be validated when published.
- Future-effective rules are taught as transition material, not as current before their effective date.

## Validated source registry

### FIBA Official Basketball Rules 2024 v1.0
- Publisher: International Basketball Federation
- Type: Current-effective international playing-rules code
- Season/effective status: Effective October 1, 2024 through September 30, 2026 unless superseded
- Location: Uploaded licensed/reference copy: FIBA Rulebook.pdf; official FIBA Resource Hub
- Status: Validated 105-page reference. Controls FIBA-based USA national-team assignments until the 2026 rules become effective.

### FIBA Official Basketball Rules Interpretations 2024 v1.0
- Publisher: International Basketball Federation
- Type: Current-effective official interpretations
- Season/effective status: Current until replaced by the 2026 interpretations
- Location: https://about.fiba.basketball/en/services/resource-hub/downloads
- Status: Required interpretation authority for FIBA-based assignments. The 2026 interpretations were announced for September 2026 and must be validated before use.

### FIBA Official Basketball Rules 2026 v1.1
- Publisher: International Basketball Federation
- Type: Future-effective international playing-rules code
- Season/effective status: 2026
- Location: https://about.fiba.basketball/en/services/resource-hub/downloads
- Status: Future effective on October 1, 2026. It must not be taught as the current rule before that date. Transition lessons identify the effective-date gate.

### FIBA Official Basketball Rules 2026 Changes v1.1
- Publisher: International Basketball Federation
- Type: Official future-effective rules-change summary
- Season/effective status: 2026
- Location: https://about.fiba.basketball/en/services/resource-hub/downloads
- Status: Includes new IRS permissions, two technical-foul categories, Disruption and Flagrant foul terminology replacing Unsportsmanlike-foul terminology, uniform changes, and court-marking flexibility. Human rules review remains required.

### USA Basketball Youth Basketball Guidelines and Rules & Standards
- Publisher: USA Basketball and National Basketball Association
- Type: Official development guidelines and event standards
- Season/effective status: Current 2026 web publication
- Location: https://www.usab.com/play/youth-basketball-guidelines
- Status: The guidelines recommend age-appropriate standards, identify FIBA rules for the grades 9-12 segment when no specific standard is stated, and require event-specific verification.

### 2026 USA Basketball U.S. Open Basketball Championships Event Information
- Publisher: USA Basketball
- Type: Official domestic-event rules and administration reference
- Season/effective status: 2026
- Location: https://www.usab.com/play/national-events/usa-basketball-youth-tournaments
- Status: Official event materials state that the 2026 U.S. Open uses USA Basketball-modified NFHS regulations, Youth Guidelines, four-quarter play, and a 30-second shot clock. Exact event bulletins and age-division modifications control the assignment.

### FIBA Game Officials Licensing 2025-2027 Guidelines
- Publisher: International Basketball Federation
- Type: Official game-official licensing and national-federation guidance
- Season/effective status: Licensed period September 1, 2025-August 31, 2027
- Location: https://assets.fiba.basketball/image/upload/v1728899485/jY5cKBC8z2BMWvpu.pdf
- Status: Used to teach the licensing framework, national-federation responsibilities, license categories, testing, and documentation. The course does not promise or confer a license.

### FIBA Referees’ Basic Fitness Test: Set Up & Procedure v5.0
- Publisher: International Basketball Federation
- Type: Official fitness-testing reference
- Season/effective status: GOL 2025-2027
- Location: https://assets.fiba.basketball/image/upload/v1728662544/TYG0xsQJlDkAoCFR.pdf
- Status: Used for fitness-test awareness and professional preparation. Medical clearance, federation administration, and current test instructions are required.

### USA Basketball governance, Safe Sport, forms, and national-team event resources
- Publisher: USA Basketball
- Type: Official national-governing-body administration and safeguarding resources
- Season/effective status: Current effective publication or event cycle
- Location: https://www.usab.com/about
- Status: USA Basketball states that it oversees certification of FIBA and USA Basketball officials and assignments to international competition. Safeguarding, event, credential, eligibility, and reporting materials must be verified for each assignment.

### USA Basketball Women’s National Team and women’s competition resources
- Publisher: USA Basketball
- Type: Official women’s national-team, event, and competition-context resources
- Season/effective status: Current event cycle
- Location: https://www.usab.com/teams and authorized USA Basketball women’s event pages
- Status: Used to identify the women’s national-team environment, event calendar, participant and host context, and assignment-specific materials. These pages do not replace the controlling rulebook, interpretations, or event bulletin.

### FIBA Women’s Basketball World Cup 2026 competition and referee-preparation resources
- Publisher: International Basketball Federation
- Type: Official women’s championship and officiating-preparation context
- Season/effective status: 2026
- Location: https://www.fiba.basketball/en/events/fiba-womens-basketball-world-cup-2026 and https://refereeing.fiba.basketball
- Status: Used for women’s elite-event structure, technical preparation, and pre-competition officiating context. The competition regulations, technical meeting, and assignment directives control event administration.

### FIBA Women in Basketball and Women in Officiating resources
- Publisher: International Basketball Federation
- Type: Official inclusion, leadership, and officiating-development guidance
- Season/effective status: Current strategic cycle
- Location: https://about.fiba.basketball/en/organization/strategy/women-in-basketball and https://about.fiba.basketball/en/programs/refereeing-initiatives
- Status: Used in leadership, equity, career-development, and institutional-policy courses, including awareness of female-referee inclusion and the FIBA Referee Maternity Plan. It is not a playing-rules authority.

### FIBA iRef Academy and Refereeing Material Library
- Publisher: International Basketball Federation
- Type: Official mechanics, individual officiating techniques, video, and development resources
- Season/effective status: Current authorized library content
- Location: https://refereeing.fiba.basketball/en/material-library
- Status: Primary mechanics-development source when authorized. Exact three-person mechanics, individual officiating techniques, pregame, table, IRS, and event modifications remain subject to qualified review.

### Applicable FIBA competition regulations and USA Basketball assignment directives
- Publisher: FIBA, FIBA Americas, USA Basketball, host event, or other authorized body
- Type: Event-specific competition and assignment controls
- Season/effective status: Assignment specific
- Location: Event bulletin, competition regulations, technical meeting materials, and assignment communication
- Status: These materials determine the actual rules code, age category, ball, timing, IRS, head coach’s challenge, table configuration, eligibility, arrival, uniform, reporting, and security procedures. They override general guidance when authorized.

## Missing or assignment-specific materials

A universal USA Basketball women’s rulebook or universal officials manual does not control every USA Basketball assignment. Event bulletins, competition regulations, age-category modifications, host procedures, mechanics directives, credential requirements, replay availability, and reporting instructions must be obtained and approved for the specific assignment.
