# RefZone University — NBA G League PhD Program Handbook

**Program:** NBA G League Basketball Officiating PhD-Level Research, Leadership, and Elite Officiating Program
**Credential status:** Professional degree-level pathway; not represented as an accredited academic degree, NBA or NBA G League employment credential, assignment qualification, or transferable college credit
**Season/source status:** 2025-26 published playing authority · 2026-27 league operations current · 2026-27 playing-rule modifications pending official publication
**Structure:** Four-year recommended sequence · eight terms · 20 courses · 60 RefZone doctoral credit-equivalent units
**Total engagement hours:** 900

A complete professional phd-level research, leadership, and elite officiating pathway for NBA G League officiating, covering NBA Rules 1-14, current G League experimental rules, mechanics, judgment, replay, challenges, communication, crew-chief leadership, game operations, film, evaluation, research, and development-league professional practice.
