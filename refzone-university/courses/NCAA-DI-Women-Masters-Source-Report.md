- NCAA women’s playing rules use a 2025-26 and 2026-27 rule-book cycle.
- The current cycle includes the coach’s-challenge system, a 20-second frontcourt reset after specified dead-ball awards, the legal jump-stop clarification, revised one-hand contact provisions, and updated team-technical administration.

## Copyright and originality controls

- Official publications are reference authorities only. RefZone lessons, explanations, case plays, diagrams, assessments, dialogue, and video scripts are original.
- No protected rulebook or casebook text, mechanics diagrams, broadcast footage, school marks, conference marks, NCAA marks, player likenesses, or copyrighted audio may be republished without authorization.
- Rule citations may identify the controlling provision, but extended protected text is not reproduced.

## Known limitations and publication gates

- The supplied official rules, casebook, and CCA mechanics publications require qualified rules and mechanics review before public release.
- 2025-26 Division I championship materials remain prior-season references until the NCAA publishes or confirms 2026-27 replacements.
- Conference and assigning-authority policies are not universal and must be attached to the source registry for the learner’s assignment.
- All 768 lessons and assessments must be rechecked when a source is superseded, disputed, or replaced.
