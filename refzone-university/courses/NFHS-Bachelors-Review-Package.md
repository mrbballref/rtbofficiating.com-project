# NFHS Bachelor’s-Level Review and Publication Package

Each item must record reviewer name, qualifications, date, corrections, source version, approval decision, and next review date.

## Rules review
- Confirm every Rule 1–10 concept represented in the curriculum.
- Validate definitions, conditions, exceptions, penalties, timing, possession, and resumption.
- Validate 2026–27 changes, comments, points of emphasis, and state modifications.
- Reject any imported NCAA, professional, or international terminology not explicitly compared.

## Mechanics review
- Validate two-person and three-person positions, coverage, movement, rotations, transitions, signals, reporting, and resumption.
- Validate every court diagram and every video-script official position.

## Curriculum and assessment review
- Confirm prerequisites, sequencing, learning outcomes, workload, assessments, rubrics, remediation, and capstone alignment.
- Verify each assessment has one defensible answer and an accurate rationale.

## Copyright and licensing review
- Confirm all explanations, scenarios, assessments, diagrams, narration, dialogue, and overlays are original.
- Confirm no protected rulebook text, case language, mechanics diagram, footage, logo, likeness, broadcast audio, or music is used without authorization.

## Accessibility review
- Test WCAG 2.2 AA keyboard, focus, semantics, contrast, text scaling, reduced motion, captions, transcript, audio description, diagrams, forms, and assessments.

## Video-realism review
- Confirm regulation geometry, court markings, official count and position, uniforms, player numbers, direction, score, clocks, signals, contact, and restart.
- Reject distorted bodies, hands, balls, lines, impossible motion, incorrect mechanics, or inconsistent game state.

## Final publication decision
- Rules reviewer approved
- Mechanics reviewer approved
- Curriculum reviewer approved
- Assessment reviewer approved
- Copyright reviewer approved
- Accessibility reviewer approved
- Video reviewer approved
- Program administrator approved
- No missing controlling source
- Effective and next-review dates recorded
