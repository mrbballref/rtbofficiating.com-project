# USA Basketball Men Master’s-Level Degree Handbook

**Program:** USA Basketball Men’s Basketball Officiating Master’s-Level Advanced Officiating Program
**Program code:** RZU-USAM-M
**Rules cycle:** 2026-27 transition cycle
**Courses:** 12
**Lessons:** 180
**Engagement hours:** 540

## Credential status

Professional degree-level pathway; not represented as an accredited academic degree or transferable college credit

## Program purpose

A complete 12-course professional master’s-level USA Basketball men’s officiating program covering event-specific source selection, current and future-effective FIBA rules, USA Basketball domestic modifications, mechanics, judgment, replay, communication, leadership, film, research, and professional practice.

## Source-control requirement

Every assignment begins with competition identification, governing code, source version, effective date, and event modification. FIBA 2026 rules are future effective until October 1, 2026. Domestic USA Basketball events may adopt modified NFHS rules, FIBA rules, or another authorized code. No universal code is assumed.

## Completion requirements

- Complete every required course and assigned prerequisite.
- Pass rules-code, source-version, effective-date, safety, ethics, timing, replay, and administration gates.
- Complete required film, mechanics, event, communication, leadership, research, or practicum work.
- Pass the capstone, comprehensive examination, candidacy, dissertation, or oral defense requirements applicable to the pathway.
- Complete all remediation and required qualified-review corrections before credential release.
