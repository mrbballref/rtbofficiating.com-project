# RefZone University — FIBA Women Masters Degree-Structured Program Handbook

**Program:** FIBA Women’s Basketball Officiating Master’s-Level Advanced Officiating Program

**Credential status:** Professional degree-level pathway; not represented as an accredited academic degree, FIBA licence, assignment credential, or transferable college credit

**Rules cycle:** 2024 current / 2026 future-effective transition cycle

## Program identity
This is an independently managed FIBA Women pathway. The common FIBA 5-on-5 playing code is taught accurately without inventing gender-based rule differences. The curriculum adds women’s competition administration, event operations, Women in Officiating development, safeguarding, inclusion, leadership, film study, assessment, research, and assignment-specific source control.

## Academic structure
- Courses: 12
- Lessons: 180
- Credit-equivalent units: 36
- Engagement hours: 540
- Every lesson includes an assessment and a complete platform-agnostic six-scene video-production script.

## Completion safeguards
Completion does not confer an accredited academic degree, transferable university credit, a FIBA licence, event nomination, assignment, employment, or advancement. Human review and source approval remain required before public publication.

- RZU-FIBW-M 501 — Advanced FIBA Rules Interpretation, OBRI, and Source-Control Seminar
- RZU-FIBW-M 502 — Complex FIBA Case Plays, Penalty Sequencing, and Correctable Errors
- RZU-FIBW-M 503 — Advanced Contact Judgment and Correct No-Call Philosophy
- RZU-FIBW-M 511 — Advanced FIBA Three-Person Mechanics and Coverage Extensions
- RZU-FIBW-M 512 — Advanced Shot Clock, End-of-Game, Broadcast Timing, and Table Administration
- RZU-FIBW-M 513 — Advanced IRS, Head Coach’s Challenge Where Adopted, Protest, and Atypical Plays
- RZU-FIBW-M 521 — Advanced Game Management, Coach Communication, and Women’s International Conflict Resolution
- RZU-FIBW-M 522 — FIBA Crew-Chief Leadership and Crew Performance
- RZU-FIBW-M 523 — Referee Instructor, Evaluator, Observer, Mentor, and Women in Officiating Development
- RZU-FIBW-M 531 — Graduate Film Analysis and Performance Analytics
