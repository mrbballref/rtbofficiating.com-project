# NCAA Division I Women — Complete Bachelor’s, Master’s, and PhD-Level Program Summary

## Program inventory

- **Bachelor’s-Level Professional Foundations:** 24 courses, 120 RefZone credit-equivalent units, 1,800 engagement hours, 288 lessons, 288 assessments, and 288 complete lesson-video scripts.
- **Master’s-Level Advanced Officiating:** 12 courses, 36 graduate credit-equivalent units, 540 engagement hours, 180 lessons, 180 assessments, and 180 complete lesson-video scripts.
- **PhD-Level Research, Leadership, and Elite Officiating:** 20 courses, 60 doctoral credit-equivalent units, 900 engagement hours, 300 lessons, 300 assessments, and 300 complete lesson-video scripts.
- **Combined:** 56 academic courses, 216 RefZone credit-equivalent units, 3,240 engagement hours, 768 lessons, 768 assessments, 768 lesson-video scripts, and 4,608 scripted scenes.

## Division I women’s emphasis

The curriculum integrates NCAA women’s playing rules, CCA three-person mechanics, Division I conference and national officiating expectations, coach’s challenges, replay, national media operations, First Four and early-round administration, regional and Final Four environments, crew-chief leadership, film analysis, research, policy, and professional contribution.

## Degree-level safeguard

These are professional degree-level pathways. They are not represented as accredited academic degrees, transferable college credit, NCAA certification, conference certification, assignment guarantees, employment guarantees, or postseason-selection guarantees.

- Authorized CCA women’s mechanics reviewer
- Replay and coach’s-challenge reviewer
- Curriculum and assessment reviewer
- Copyright and licensing reviewer
- Accessibility and video-realism reviewers
- Program administrator
- Research, ethics, legal, statistics, and dissertation reviewers for the doctoral pathway
