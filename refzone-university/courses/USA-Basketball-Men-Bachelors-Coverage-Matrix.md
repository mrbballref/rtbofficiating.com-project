## Effective-date publication gate

- Apply FIBA 2024 as current authority through September 30, 2026 unless an authorized competition states otherwise.
- Apply FIBA 2026 only to competitions on or after October 1, 2026, subject to the event regulations and current official interpretations.
- Do not use the new Disruption/Flagrant or technical-category framework before its effective date.
- Revalidate all USA Basketball event modifications, especially domestic youth and U.S. Open assignments.
