# EuroLeague Women Bachelor’s-Level Degree Handbook

**Program:** EuroLeague Women’s Basketball Officiating Bachelor’s-Level Professional Foundations Program
**Program code:** RZU-ELW-B
**Rules cycle:** 2026-27 transition cycle
**Courses:** 24
**Lessons:** 288
**Engagement hours:** 1800

## Credential status

Professional degree-level pathway; not represented as an accredited academic degree or transferable college credit

## Program purpose

A complete 24-course professional bachelor’s-level EuroLeague Women’s officiating program covering EuroLeague Women competition regulations, bylaws, current and future-effective FIBA rules, casebook, mechanics, judgment, replay, communication, leadership, film, research, and professional practice.

## Source-control requirement

Every assignment begins with the EuroLeague season, bylaws, adapted rules, authorized FIBA mechanics guidance, technical and assignment clarification, assignment directive, source version, and effective date. FIBA 2026 rules are future effective until October 1, 2026 and require confirmed EuroLeague implementation. Domestic-league or other competition procedures never override EuroLeague authority.
