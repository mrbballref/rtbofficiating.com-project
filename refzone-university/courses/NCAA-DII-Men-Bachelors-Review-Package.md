# NCAA Division II Men Bachelor’s-Level Review Package

## Required approvals

- NCAA men’s rules reviewer
- NCAA Division II men’s championship/procedures reviewer
- Authorized NCAA men’s or applicable conference/consortium mechanics reviewer
- Curriculum and assessment reviewer
- Copyright and licensing reviewer
- Accessibility reviewer
- Video-realism reviewer
- Program administrator

## Release gates

- Every NCAA Men’s Rule 1-11 section and related approved ruling is represented or deliberately mapped to a lesson.
- Current rules changes, points of emphasis, case-book rulings, replay, and coach’s-challenge procedures are validated.
- The authorized men’s mechanics framework and all applicable conference or consortium modifications are attached and validated.
- Division II conference, tournament, championship, media-timeout, arrival, safety, and assigning procedures are attached for the deployment context.
- Prior-season Division II championship manuals are visibly labeled and replaced or revalidated when current manuals are issued.
- All student-facing text, cases, diagrams, assessments, dialogue, and video scripts are original.
- All videos meet court, half, game-clock, shot-clock, mechanics, continuity, caption, transcript, audio-description, and licensing requirements.
- No program is represented as accredited, transferable, or a guarantee of assignments, certification, postseason selection, or employment.
