# NFHS Basketball Officiating Bachelor’s-Level Professional Foundations Program

**Structure:** Four years · eight academic terms · 24 required courses · 120 RefZone credit-equivalent units · 1,800 engagement hours  
**Rules season:** 2026–27  
**Credential status:** Professional degree-level pathway; not represented as an accredited academic degree or transferable college credit  
**Release status:** Complete instructional production draft pending required human reviews  

## Program outcomes

- Apply the complete current NFHS basketball rule system, definitions, penalties, and administrative procedures.
- Perform two-person and three-person mechanics with disciplined primary coverage, angles, transitions, rotations, and reporting.
- Judge violations and contact through legal status, complete visual evidence, prohibited effect, correct classification, and correct no-call philosophy.
- Communicate with players, coaches, partners, table personnel, administrators, assignors, supervisors, and evaluators professionally.
- Prepare, arrive, conduct pregames, inspect the game environment, manage unusual situations, complete reports, and review performance.
- Use normal-speed film study, source control, applied practicums, integrated examinations, a senior capstone, and oral defense to demonstrate competency.

## Program completion gates

- Complete all 24 required courses and 120 RefZone credit-equivalent units.
- Pass source-control, rules, penalty, mechanics, safety, ethics, communication, clock, and game-administration competency gates.
- Complete both applied officiating practicums and required remediation.
- Submit the complete-game film analysis and professional self-evaluation.
- Pass the integrated rules and mechanics seminar, comprehensive examination, senior capstone, oral defense, and professional portfolio.

## Publication safeguards

- Every lesson is original RefZone University instruction and does not reproduce protected NFHS publication text.
- Official sources, case references, mechanics requirements, signals, penalties, and current-season changes require qualified human validation before public publication.
- State-association and assigning-authority modifications must be attached and displayed for the learner’s jurisdiction.
- Membership access remains separate from academic admission, and completion does not guarantee certification, assignments, employment, or advancement.
