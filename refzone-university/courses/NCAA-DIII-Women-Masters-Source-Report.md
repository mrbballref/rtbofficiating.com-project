# NCAA Division III Women’s Master’s-Level Program — Source Report

## Source-control status
This program is a complete graduate instructional production draft. It may not be published as rules-authoritative until the controlling sources and required human reviews are approved.

## Controlling playing-rules sources
1. Current effective NCAA Women’s Basketball Rules Book for the 2025–26 and 2026–27 rules cycle.
2. Current official NCAA Women’s Basketball Case Book and approved rulings.
3. Current NCAA women’s rules changes, interpretations, comments, corrections, and rules-differences materials.
4. Current NCAA women’s replay, coaches’ appeals and challenges, table-crew, court-diagram, uniform, and timing resources.
5. Current authorized NCAA women’s basketball mechanics or CCA manual and assignment-specific mechanics directives.

## NCAA Division III administrative sources
1. Current NCAA Division III Women’s Basketball prechampionships manual.
2. Current host-operations manual.
3. Current site-representative manual.
4. Current media-coordination and timeout protocols.
5. Applicable conference, consortium, tournament, venue, replay, security, emergency, and assigning-authority directives.

## Source hierarchy
- NCAA women’s playing rules control game rulings.
- Official case-book rulings and interpretations explain application.
- Authorized mechanics publications control positioning and crew procedures.
- Division III and assignment-specific manuals control championship and administrative procedures only within their stated jurisdiction.
- A conference or tournament modification must not be represented as universal NCAA women’s playing law.

## Current-cycle control notes
The NCAA’s official women’s basketball rules page identifies the rulebook as covering the 2025–26 and 2026–27 rules cycle and lists the 2025–26 case book and related rules resources. The current coaches’ challenge system and other cycle changes must be taught from the effective official language and implementation guidance, not from summaries alone.

## Known publication limitations
- Division III championship manuals currently available in the source set may be 2025–26 editions and require replacement or revalidation for 2026–27.
- Conference, consortium, venue, media, replay, and assignor requirements can vary and must be verified for each assignment.
- The exact edition, effective date, and authorization status of any supplied CCA mechanics manual must be confirmed before it is treated as current controlling mechanics authority.

## Copyright and licensing
Official publications are used as reference authorities. RefZone lessons must paraphrase accurately, cite rule numbers and source versions, and use original scenarios, diagrams, assessments, narration, and video. No protected rulebook text, case-book text, mechanics diagrams, broadcast footage, logos, or real-person likenesses may be reproduced without authorization.

## Publication gate
Required approvals: NCAA women’s rules reviewer; Division III administration reviewer; authorized mechanics reviewer; replay reviewer; curriculum reviewer; assessment reviewer; copyright reviewer; accessibility reviewer; video reviewer; and program administrator.
