# USA Basketball Women PhD-Level Degree Handbook

**Program:** USA Basketball Women’s Officiating PhD-Level Research, Leadership, and Elite Officiating Program
**Program code:** RZU-USAW-D
**Rules cycle:** 2026-27 transition cycle
**Courses:** 20
**Lessons:** 300
**Engagement hours:** 900

## Credential status

Professional degree-level pathway; not represented as an accredited academic degree or transferable college credit

## Program purpose

A complete 20-course professional doctoral-level USA Basketball women’s officiating program covering event-specific source selection, current and future-effective FIBA rules, USA Basketball domestic modifications, mechanics, judgment, replay, communication, leadership, film, research, and professional practice.

## Source-control requirement

Every assignment begins with competition identification, governing code, source version, effective date, and event modification. FIBA 2026 rules are future effective until October 1, 2026. Domestic USA Basketball events may adopt modified NFHS rules, FIBA rules, or another authorized code. No universal code is assumed.
