# FIBA Men PhD-Level Degree Handbook

**Program:** FIBA Men’s Basketball Officiating PhD-Level Research, Leadership, and Elite Officiating Program
**Program code:** RZU-FIBM-D
**Rules cycle:** 2024 current / 2026 future-effective transition cycle
**Courses:** 20
**Lessons:** 300
**Engagement hours:** 900

## Credential status

Professional degree-level pathway; not represented as an accredited academic degree or transferable college credit

## Program purpose

A complete 20-course professional doctoral-level FIBA men’s officiating program covering FIBA Official Basketball Rules and Official Interpretations, internal regulations, current and future-effective FIBA rules, Official Interpretations, mechanics, judgment, replay, communication, leadership, film, research, and professional practice.

## Source-control requirement

Every assignment begins with the FIBA season, internal regulations, Official Basketball Rules and authorized competition regulations, officiating manual, official interpretation or technical clarification, assignment directive, source version, and effective date. FIBA 2026 rules are future effective until October 1, 2026 and require confirmed FIBA implementation. Domestic-league or other competition procedures never override FIBA authority.
