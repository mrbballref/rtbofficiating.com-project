# EuroLeague Men Master’s-Level Review Package

## Required approvals

- Euroleague Basketball adapted-rules and competition-regulations reviewer
- FIBA rules and official-interpretations reviewer
- FIBA/iRef mechanics reviewer
- EuroLeague bylaws, technical rules, unified scorers, and arena-operations reviewer
- Replay and head coach’s challenge reviewer
- Integrity, ethics, disciplinary, labor, and legal reviewer
- Curriculum and assessment reviewer
- Copyright and licensing reviewer
- Accessibility reviewer
- Video-realism reviewer
- Program administrator

## Publication checklist

- [ ] Every lesson identifies the actual competition and controlling authority.
- [ ] Current-effective and future-effective rules are separated.
- [ ] EuroLeague adapted rules and technical clarifications are verified against the current manual and bylaws.
- [ ] No FIBA, Euroleague Basketball, club, athlete, coach, referee, or broadcast content is copied without authorization.
- [ ] Every assessment has a current source and rationale.
- [ ] Every video script has accurate court geometry, clocks, mechanics, signals, administration, captions, transcript, and audio description.
- [ ] All rule, mechanics, safety, replay, licensing, and source conflicts are resolved or blocked from publication.
- [ ] Accreditation and employment disclaimers remain visible.
