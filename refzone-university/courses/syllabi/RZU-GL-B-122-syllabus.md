A 5-unit bachelor’s-level professional foundations pathway course focused on applied nba g league officiating practicum i, combining original RefZone instruction, official-source study, film or evidence analysis, communication, assessment, and applied professional performance.

## Learning outcomes
- Select and cite the controlling NBA G League-specific authority, internal regulations, officiating manual, official interpretation or technical clarification, assignment directive, and underlying NBA G League provision.
- Apply the course concepts using complete legal-status or evidence sequences.
- Create and solve original legal, illegal, borderline, atypical, and disciplined no-action scenarios.
- Use accurate mechanics, administration, communication, research, or policy methods.
- Defend conclusions at normal speed or against complete evidence and respond to qualified review.
- Complete the course performance task and remediation requirements.
