# RZU-NFHS-B 172 — Integrated Rules and Mechanics Seminar

**Program:** NFHS Basketball Officiating Bachelor’s-Level Professional Foundations Program  
**Academic period:** Year 4 · Term 8  
**Term theme:** Senior Capstone, Oral Defense, and Professional Portfolio  
**Learning volume:** 5 RefZone credit-equivalent units · 75 engagement hours  
**Prerequisite:** Successful completion of RZU-NFHS-B 171 and all applicable competency gates  
**Credential status:** Professional degree-level pathway; not represented as an accredited degree or transferable college credit  
**Publication status:** Complete instructional draft pending qualified rules, mechanics, curriculum, copyright, accessibility, video, and program review  

A five-unit academic course in the NFHS Bachelor's-Level Professional Foundations pathway focused on integrated rules and mechanics seminar. The course combines original RefZone instruction, authorized-source study, case-play analysis, film review, communication practice, and applied performance.

## Learning outcomes

- Explain and apply the complete competency domain of integrated rules and mechanics seminar using current NFHS authority.
- Identify legal status, primary coverage, required visual evidence, exceptions, and administration before making a ruling.
- Create and solve original legal, illegal, borderline, atypical, and correct no-call or no-correction scenarios.
- Use correct mechanics, signals, reporting, communication, clock, possession, penalty, and resumption procedures.
- Analyze performance at normal speed, use slow motion for facts, and defend conclusions with source and film evidence.
- Complete the course performance task and remediation requirements at the approved competency standard.
- Present a formal written and oral defense supported by current sources and authentic performance evidence.

### 1. Rules 1–3 Integration: Court, Officials, Players, and Equipment

This lesson develops rules 1–3 integration: court, officials, players, and equipment within Integrated Rules and Mechanics Seminar. Students identify the controlling status, observe the complete action, apply the current NFHS rule or mechanics requirement, administer the result, communicate professionally, and convert the play into a repeatable next-game decision process.

**Required evidence**

- Read the assigned current source section and record the rule number, defined terms, conditions, exceptions, penalty, and effective date without copying protected text.
- Create a one-page decision tree for rules 1–3 integration: court, officials, players, and equipment.
- Write one original legal, illegal, borderline, and correct no-call or no-correction example.
- Complete the normal-speed recognition drill and then use slow motion only to identify factual details.

- RZU-NFHS-B 172 source and terminology notebook

- Legal/illegal/borderline/no-call comparison assignment

- Signal, reporting, and communication demonstration
- Midterm integrated examination
- Integrated Rules and Mechanics Seminar final performance project and oral defense

## Completion requirements

- Earn at least 80 percent for the academic course.
- Pass every mandatory rules, penalty, safety, ethics, and source-control competency assigned to the course.
- Complete all required case, film, communication, and practical submissions.
- Correct any superseded-source or administration error identified during review.
- Pass the final performance project and oral defense or complete assigned remediation and re-demonstration.

## Source, copyright, and academic-integrity standard

Official NFHS publications and current state, league, tournament, and assigning-authority policies control accuracy. The course uses original RefZone University explanations, scenarios, assessments, diagrams, dialogue, and video scripts. Protected publication text, case language, mechanics diagrams, footage, logos, and likenesses are not reproduced without authorization.
