# RZU-WNB-B 116 — Flagrant, Punching, Away-From-the-Play, Transition Take, Double, Offensive, and Loose-Ball Fouls

**Program:** WNBA Basketball Officiating Bachelor’s-Level Professional Foundations Program
**Units:** 5 · **Hours:** 75
**Prerequisite:** RZU-WNB-B 115
**Status:** Complete instructional production draft. Qualified WNBA rules, mechanics, replay, challenge, game operations, referee development, curriculum, copyright, accessibility, video, assessment, research, legal, safeguarding, and administrator review remain required before public publication.

A 5-unit bachelor’s-level professional foundations pathway course focused on flagrant, punching, away-from-the-play, transition take, double, offensive, and loose-ball fouls, combining original RefZone instruction, official WNBA source study, normal-speed film or evidence analysis, communication, assessment, and applied professional performance.

## Learning outcomes
- Select and cite the controlling WNBA-specific authority, internal regulations, officiating manual, official interpretation or technical clarification, assignment directive, and underlying WNBA provision.
- Apply the course concepts using complete legal-status or evidence sequences.
- Create and solve original legal, illegal, borderline, atypical, and disciplined no-action scenarios.
- Use accurate mechanics, administration, communication, research, or policy methods.
- Defend conclusions at normal speed or against complete evidence and respond to qualified review.
- Complete the course performance task and remediation requirements.

## Lessons
1. Rule 12B flagrant foul criteria, unnecessary and excessive contact
2. Punching fouls, attempts, hostile acts, retaliation, and safety
3. Away-from-the-play foul elements, timing, penalty, selected shooter, and possession
4. Transition take foul elements, play opportunity, exceptions, penalty, and restart
5. Double fouls, offensive fouls, loose-ball fouls, and team-control consequences
6. Upgrade and downgrade analysis, replay evidence, communication, and reporting
7. Source Map, Definitions, and Competency Targets: Flagrant, Punching, Away-From-the-Play, Transition Take, Double, Offensive, and Loose-Ball Fouls
8. Legal Status and Required Conditions: Flagrant, Punching, Away-From-the-Play, Transition Take, Double, Offensive, and Loose-Ball Fouls
9. Legal, Illegal, Borderline, and Correct No-Call Laboratory: Flagrant, Punching, Away-From-the-Play, Transition Take, Double, Offensive, and Loose-Ball Fouls
10. Mechanics, Primary Coverage, and Visual Evidence: Flagrant, Punching, Away-From-the-Play, Transition Take, Double, Offensive, and Loose-Ball Fouls
11. Penalty, Clock, Shot Clock, Possession, Replay, and Resumption: Flagrant, Punching, Away-From-the-Play, Transition Take, Double, Offensive, and Loose-Ball Fouls
12. Communication, Reporting, Film Review, and Course Assessment: Flagrant, Punching, Away-From-the-Play, Transition Take, Double, Offensive, and Loose-Ball Fouls
