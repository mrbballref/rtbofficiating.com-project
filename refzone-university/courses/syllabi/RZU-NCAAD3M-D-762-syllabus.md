# RZU-NCAAD3M-D 762 — Dissertation Proposal and Research Defense

**Program:** NCAA Division III Men’s Basketball Officiating PhD-Level Research, Leadership, and Elite Officiating Pathway  
**Academic placement:** Year 4, Term 7  
**Units:** 3 RefZone doctoral credit-equivalent units  
**Engagement:** 45 hours  
**Prerequisite:** Successful completion of RZU-NCAAD3M-D 761 and approval of the doctoral research area  
**Credential status:** Professional doctoral degree-level pathway; not an accredited or transferable academic degree  
**Publication status:** Complete instructional production draft; required human review remains pending  

## Course Description

A three-unit doctoral course in the NCAA Division III Men’s PhD-Level Research, Leadership, and Elite Officiating pathway focused on dissertation proposal and research defense. The course integrates current NCAA men’s playing-rules authority and NCAA Division III administrative authority, doctoral scholarship, original research or policy work, ethics, equity, professional application, peer review, and oral defense.

## Learning Outcomes

- Critically synthesize the doctoral domain of dissertation proposal and research defense using current NCAA men’s playing-rules authority and NCAA Division III administrative authority and validated research evidence.
- Design and defend an appropriate research, policy, analytic, leadership, curriculum, technology, or evaluation method.
- Evaluate alternative explanations, uncertainty, ethics, equity, privacy, copyright, governance, and implementation risk.
- Produce original doctoral writing, evidence visualizations, peer review, revision, and oral defense.
- Translate findings into a responsible professional or institutional contribution.
- Maintain complete source versioning, research records, human-review gates, and academic integrity.

## Grading

- Problem, purpose, framework, and literature review: 25%
- Research design, measures, sampling, and analysis plan: 25%
- Ethics, feasibility, data governance, and risk plan: 20%
- Written dissertation proposal: 15%
- Proposal oral defense and revision: 15%

## Required Submissions

- Complete dissertation proposal
- Critical literature review
- Approved methods and analysis plan
- Ethics and data-governance plan
- Proposal defense presentation
- Committee revision memorandum

## Lesson Sequence

1. Selecting a Significant and Feasible Dissertation Problem
2. Stakeholder Need, Scholarly Gap, and Professional Contribution
3. Proposal Title, Abstract, and Problem Statement
4. Purpose, Questions, Hypotheses, and Scope
5. Conceptual or Theoretical Framework
6. Critical Literature Review
7. Research Design and Methodological Alignment
8. Population, Sampling, Access, and Recruitment
9. Measures, Instruments, Protocols, and Pilot Testing
10. Data Collection, Management, Security, and Retention
11. Analysis Plan and Integration Strategy
12. Ethics, Consent, Confidentiality, and Approval Plan
13. Feasibility, Timeline, Budget, Risks, and Contingencies
14. Written Dissertation Proposal Submission
15. Proposal Oral Defense and Committee Revision

## Completion Requirements

- Earn at least 85 percent for the doctoral course unless a higher candidacy, proposal, dissertation, or defense standard applies.
- Pass every mandatory ethics, integrity, source-control, privacy, safety, equity, and professional-conduct competency.
- Complete all required doctoral writing, research, policy, analytics, leadership, curriculum, technology, peer-review, or defense submissions.
- Correct any source-version, methodological, evidence, attribution, confidentiality, or overclaiming issue identified during review.
- Pass the final course defense, committee review, examination, proposal, dissertation milestone, or assigned remediation.

## Source and Copyright Controls

- Official NCAA men’s and NCAA Division III publications and current official updates are reference authorities; student-facing instruction is original RefZone University language.
- No protected rulebook text, casebook language, mechanics diagrams, game footage, logos, or identifiable participant data may be reproduced without authorization.
- Research literature, legal and policy sources, technology claims, and institutional procedures require source-by-source validation.
- Any uncertainty, source conflict, inaccessible authority, ethics concern, or jurisdiction-specific requirement remains blocked for human review.
