# RZU-NFHS-B 161 — Pregame, Arrival, Crew Protocol, and Professional Preparation

**Program:** NFHS Basketball Officiating Bachelor’s-Level Professional Foundations Program  
**Academic period:** Year 4 · Term 7  
**Term theme:** Advanced Practicum and Film Analysis  
**Learning volume:** 5 RefZone credit-equivalent units · 75 engagement hours  
**Prerequisite:** Successful completion of RZU-NFHS-B 153 and all applicable competency gates  
**Credential status:** Professional degree-level pathway; not represented as an accredited degree or transferable college credit  
**Publication status:** Complete instructional draft pending qualified rules, mechanics, curriculum, copyright, accessibility, video, and program review  

A five-unit academic course in the NFHS Bachelor's-Level Professional Foundations pathway focused on pregame, arrival, crew protocol, and professional preparation. The course combines original RefZone instruction, authorized-source study, case-play analysis, film review, communication practice, and applied performance.

## Learning outcomes

- Explain and apply the complete competency domain of pregame, arrival, crew protocol, and professional preparation using current NFHS authority.
- Identify legal status, primary coverage, required visual evidence, exceptions, and administration before making a ruling.
- Create and solve original legal, illegal, borderline, atypical, and correct no-call or no-correction scenarios.
- Use correct mechanics, signals, reporting, communication, clock, possession, penalty, and resumption procedures.
- Analyze performance at normal speed, use slow motion for facts, and defend conclusions with source and film evidence.
- Complete the course performance task and remediation requirements at the approved competency standard.

### 1. Assignment Acceptance, Conflicts, and Reliability

This lesson develops assignment acceptance, conflicts, and reliability within Pregame, Arrival, Crew Protocol, and Professional Preparation. Students identify the controlling status, observe the complete action, apply the current NFHS rule or mechanics requirement, administer the result, communicate professionally, and convert the play into a repeatable next-game decision process.

**Required evidence**

- Create a one-page decision tree for assignment acceptance, conflicts, and reliability.
- Write one original legal, illegal, borderline, and correct no-call or no-correction example.

## Source, copyright, and academic-integrity standard

The course uses original RefZone University explanations, scenarios, assessments, diagrams, dialogue, and video scripts.
