# RZU-USAM-B 123 — Traveling, Pivoting, Dribbling, Carrying, and Footwork

**Program:** USA Basketball Men’s Basketball Officiating Bachelor’s-Level Professional Foundations Program  
**Year/Term:** Year 2, Term 3  
**Units:** 5 RefZone credit-equivalent units  
**Engagement hours:** 75  
**Credential status:** Professional degree-level pathway; not accredited or transferable academic credit  
**Publication status:** Complete instructional draft pending qualified review

## Course description

A 5-unit undergraduate-style course focused on traveling, pivoting, dribbling, carrying, and footwork. It combines original RefZone instruction, official-source study, event-specific rules-code selection, film or evidence analysis, communication, assessment, and applied performance.

## Learning outcomes

- Select and cite the controlling USA Basketball, FIBA, or expressly adopted domestic authority.
- Apply the course concepts using complete legal-status or evidence sequences.
- Create and solve original legal, illegal, borderline, atypical, and disciplined no-action scenarios.
- Use accurate mechanics, administration, communication, research, or policy methods.
- Defend conclusions at normal speed or against complete evidence and respond to qualified review.
- Complete the course performance task and remediation requirements.

## Required lessons

1. Establishing the pivot foot
2. Starting and ending the dribble
3. Gather concepts and FIBA footwork language
4. Jump stops, spin moves, and step-throughs
5. Fumble, interrupted dribble, and double dribble
6. Carrying or palming and illegal dribble
7. Source Map, Definitions, and Competency Targets: Traveling, Pivoting, Dribbling, Carrying, and Footwork
8. Legal Status and Required Conditions: Traveling, Pivoting, Dribbling, Carrying, and Footwork
9. Legal, Illegal, Borderline, and No-Call Laboratory: Traveling, Pivoting, Dribbling, Carrying, and Footwork
10. Mechanics, Primary Coverage, and Visual Evidence: Traveling, Pivoting, Dribbling, Carrying, and Footwork
11. Penalty, Clock, Shot-Clock, Possession, and Resumption: Traveling, Pivoting, Dribbling, Carrying, and Footwork
12. Communication, Reporting, Film Review, and Course Assessment: Traveling, Pivoting, Dribbling, Carrying, and Footwork

## Source-control rule
