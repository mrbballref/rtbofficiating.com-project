# RZU-NFHS-B 163 — Applied Officiating Practicum I

**Program:** NFHS Basketball Officiating Bachelor’s-Level Professional Foundations Program  
**Academic period:** Year 4 · Term 7  
**Term theme:** Advanced Practicum and Film Analysis  
**Learning volume:** 5 RefZone credit-equivalent units · 75 engagement hours  
**Prerequisite:** Successful completion of RZU-NFHS-B 162 and all applicable competency gates  
**Credential status:** Professional degree-level pathway; not represented as an accredited degree or transferable college credit  
**Publication status:** Complete instructional draft pending qualified rules, mechanics, curriculum, copyright, accessibility, video, and program review  

A five-unit academic course in the NFHS Bachelor's-Level Professional Foundations pathway focused on applied officiating practicum i. The course combines original RefZone instruction, authorized-source study, case-play analysis, film review, communication practice, and applied performance.

## Learning outcomes

- Explain and apply the complete competency domain of applied officiating practicum i using current NFHS authority.
- Identify legal status, primary coverage, required visual evidence, exceptions, and administration before making a ruling.
- Create and solve original legal, illegal, borderline, atypical, and correct no-call or no-correction scenarios.
- Use correct mechanics, signals, reporting, communication, clock, possession, penalty, and resumption procedures.
- Analyze performance at normal speed, use slow motion for facts, and defend conclusions with source and film evidence.
- Complete the course performance task and remediation requirements at the approved competency standard.
- Demonstrate the required floor behavior through an anchored practical or film-defense rubric.

- **Week 1:** Practicum Orientation, Safety, and the Anchored Rubric

- **Week 10:** Contact Judgment Performance

- **Week 12:** Violation Judgment and Count Performance

- **Week 14:** Player, Coach, and Crew Communication Performance · final project, practical, or oral defense

## Lessons

### 1. Practicum Orientation, Safety, and the Anchored Rubric

This lesson develops practicum orientation, safety, and the anchored rubric within Applied Officiating Practicum I. Students identify the controlling status, observe the complete action, apply the current NFHS rule or mechanics requirement, administer the result, communicate professionally, and convert the play into a repeatable next-game decision process.

**Required evidence**

- Read the assigned current source section and record the rule number, defined terms, conditions, exceptions, penalty, and effective date without copying protected text.
- Create a one-page decision tree for practicum orientation, safety, and the anchored rubric.
- Write one original legal, illegal, borderline, and correct no-call or no-correction example.
- Complete the normal-speed recognition drill and then use slow motion only to identify factual details.
