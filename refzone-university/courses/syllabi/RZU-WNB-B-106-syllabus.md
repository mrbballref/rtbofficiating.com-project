# RZU-WNB-B 106 — Putting the Ball in Play, Live and Dead Ball, Jump Balls, Throw-Ins, and Late-Game Advancement

**Program:** WNBA Basketball Officiating Bachelor’s-Level Professional Foundations Program
**Units:** 5 · **Hours:** 75
**Prerequisite:** RZU-WNB-B 105
**Status:** Complete instructional production draft. Qualified WNBA rules, mechanics, replay, challenge, game operations, referee development, curriculum, copyright, accessibility, video, assessment, research, legal, safeguarding, and administrator review remain required before public publication.

A 5-unit bachelor’s-level professional foundations pathway course focused on putting the ball in play, live and dead ball, jump balls, throw-ins, and late-game advancement, combining original RefZone instruction, official WNBA source study, normal-speed film or evidence analysis, communication, assessment, and applied professional performance.

## Learning outcomes
- Select and cite the controlling WNBA-specific authority, internal regulations, officiating manual, official interpretation or technical clarification, assignment directive, and underlying WNBA provision.
- Apply the course concepts using complete legal-status or evidence sequences.
- Create and solve original legal, illegal, borderline, atypical, and disciplined no-action scenarios.
- Use accurate mechanics, administration, communication, research, or policy methods.
- Defend conclusions at normal speed or against complete evidence and respond to qualified review.
- Complete the course performance task and remediation requirements.

1. Rule 6 start of game, periods, overtime, and possession to begin later periods
2. Live ball, ball alive, dead ball, whistle, successful goal, and suspended play
3. Center-circle jump balls, other jump balls, involved players, and restrictions
4. Rule 8 player and ball out of bounds and responsibility for the throw-in
5. Designated spot, thrower restrictions, defender restrictions, counts, and interference
6. Last-two-minutes timeout advancement option, location, eight-second count, and clocks
7. Source Map, Definitions, and Competency Targets: Putting the Ball in Play, Live and Dead Ball, Jump Balls, Throw-Ins, and Late-Game Advancement
8. Legal Status and Required Conditions: Putting the Ball in Play, Live and Dead Ball, Jump Balls, Throw-Ins, and Late-Game Advancement
9. Legal, Illegal, Borderline, and Correct No-Call Laboratory: Putting the Ball in Play, Live and Dead Ball, Jump Balls, Throw-Ins, and Late-Game Advancement
10. Mechanics, Primary Coverage, and Visual Evidence: Putting the Ball in Play, Live and Dead Ball, Jump Balls, Throw-Ins, and Late-Game Advancement
11. Penalty, Clock, Shot Clock, Possession, Replay, and Resumption: Putting the Ball in Play, Live and Dead Ball, Jump Balls, Throw-Ins, and Late-Game Advancement
