# RZU-GL-B 110 — Basket Interference, Goaltending, Rim and Cylinder Plays, and Scoring Corrections

**Program:** NBA G League Basketball Officiating Bachelor’s-Level Professional Foundations Program
**Units:** 5 · **Hours:** 75
**Prerequisite:** RZU-GL-B 109
**Status:** Complete instructional production draft. Qualified NBA/G League rules, experimental rules, mechanics, replay, game operations, referee development, curriculum, copyright, accessibility, video, assessment, research, legal, and administrator review remain required before public publication.

A 5-unit bachelor’s-level professional foundations pathway course focused on basket interference, goaltending, rim and cylinder plays, and scoring corrections, combining original RefZone instruction, official-source study, film or evidence analysis, communication, assessment, and applied professional performance.

## Learning outcomes
- Select and cite the controlling NBA G League-specific authority, internal regulations, officiating manual, official interpretation or technical clarification, assignment directive, and underlying NBA G League provision.
- Apply the course concepts using complete legal-status or evidence sequences.
- Create and solve original legal, illegal, borderline, atypical, and disciplined no-action scenarios.
- Use accurate mechanics, administration, communication, research, or policy methods.
- Defend conclusions at normal speed or against complete evidence and respond to qualified review.
- Complete the course performance task and remediation requirements.

7. Source Map, Definitions, and Competency Targets: Basket Interference, Goaltending, Rim and Cylinder Plays, and Scoring Corrections
8. Legal Status and Required Conditions: Basket Interference, Goaltending, Rim and Cylinder Plays, and Scoring Corrections
9. Legal, Illegal, Borderline, and Correct No-Call Laboratory: Basket Interference, Goaltending, Rim and Cylinder Plays, and Scoring Corrections
10. Mechanics, Primary Coverage, and Visual Evidence: Basket Interference, Goaltending, Rim and Cylinder Plays, and Scoring Corrections
11. Penalty, Clock, Shot-Clock, Possession, and Resumption: Basket Interference, Goaltending, Rim and Cylinder Plays, and Scoring Corrections
12. Communication, Reporting, Film Review, and Course Assessment: Basket Interference, Goaltending, Rim and Cylinder Plays, and Scoring Corrections
