# RZU-D3M-B 132 — Common, Intentional, Technical, Flagrant, Fighting, and Disqualification Rules

**Program:** NCAA Division III Men’s Basketball Officiating Bachelor’s-Level Professional Foundations Program  
**Academic period:** Year 2 · Term 4  
**Term theme:** Replay, Mechanics, and Collegiate Game Management  
**Learning volume:** 5 RefZone credit-equivalent units · 75 engagement hours  
**Prerequisite:** Successful completion of RZU-D3M-B 131 and all applicable competency gates  
**Credential status:** Professional degree-level pathway; not an accredited degree or transferable college credit  
**Rules basis:** 2025-26 NCAA Men’s Rules Book and Casebook, current approved rulings, current-cycle rules changes and points of emphasis, and verified NCAA Division III procedures and modifications  

A five-unit academic course in the NCAA Division III Men Bachelor’s-Level Professional Foundations pathway focused on common, intentional, technical, flagrant, fighting, and disqualification rules. The course combines original RefZone instruction, official-source study, approved-ruling analysis, film review, communication practice, and applied performance.

## Learning outcomes

- Explain and apply common, intentional, technical, flagrant, fighting, and disqualification rules using current NCAA men’s authority and verified NCAA Division III procedures and modifications.
- Identify legal status, primary coverage, required visual evidence, exceptions, and administration before ruling.
- Create and solve original legal, illegal, borderline, atypical, and correct no-call scenarios.
- Use correct mechanics, signals, reporting, communication, clock, shot-clock, replay, possession, penalty, and resumption procedures.
- Analyze performance at normal speed, use slow motion only for facts, and defend conclusions with source and film evidence.
- Complete the performance task and remediation requirements at the approved competency standard.

## Lessons

### 1. Administrative and Team Technical Fouls, Warnings, and Delay Administration

This lesson develops administrative and team technical fouls, warnings, and delay administration within Common, Intentional, Technical, Flagrant, Fighting, and Disqualification Rules. Students establish the controlling status, observe the complete action, apply current NCAA men’s rules and verified NCAA Division III modifications and procedures, administer the full result, communicate professionally, and convert the play into a repeatable next-game process.

**Required evidence**

- Read the assigned current rule and related approved rulings; record definitions, conditions, exceptions, penalty, and effective date without copying protected text.
- Write a one-page decision tree for the lesson.
- Create one original legal, illegal, borderline, and correct no-call or no-correction example.
- Complete a normal-speed recognition drill and use slow motion only to identify facts.
