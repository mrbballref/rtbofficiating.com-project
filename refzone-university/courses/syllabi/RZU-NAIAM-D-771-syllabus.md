# RZU-NAIAM-D 771 — Applied Dissertation or Institutional Research Project

**Program:** NAIA Men’s Basketball Officiating PhD-Level Research, Leadership, and Elite Officiating Pathway  
**Academic placement:** Year 4, Term 8  
**Units:** 3 RefZone doctoral credit-equivalent units  
**Engagement:** 45 hours  
**Prerequisite:** Successful dissertation-proposal defense, required ethics or institutional approval, and committee authorization to begin research  
**Credential status:** Professional doctoral degree-level pathway; not an accredited or transferable academic degree  
**Publication status:** Complete instructional production draft; required human review remains pending  

## Course Description

A three-unit doctoral course in the NAIA Men’s PhD-Level Research, Leadership, and Elite Officiating pathway focused on applied dissertation or institutional research project. The course integrates current NCAA men’s playing-rules authority and NAIA administrative authority, doctoral scholarship, original research or policy work, ethics, equity, professional application, peer review, and oral defense.

## Learning Outcomes

- Critically synthesize the doctoral domain of applied dissertation or institutional research project using current NCAA men’s playing-rules authority and NAIA administrative authority and validated research evidence.
- Design and defend an appropriate research, policy, analytic, leadership, curriculum, technology, or evaluation method.
- Evaluate alternative explanations, uncertainty, ethics, equity, privacy, copyright, governance, and implementation risk.
- Produce original doctoral writing, evidence visualizations, peer review, revision, and oral defense.
- Translate findings into a responsible professional or institutional contribution.
- Maintain complete source versioning, research records, human-review gates, and academic integrity.

## Grading

- Protocol compliance, data governance, and audit trail: 15%
- Data quality and analysis: 25%
- Findings, interpretation, and limitations: 20%
- Applied professional contribution: 15%
- Complete committee-approved manuscript: 25%

## Required Submissions

- Approved protocol and audit trail
- Research dataset or documented institutional evidence
- Analysis files and decision log
- Complete dissertation or institutional research manuscript
- Applied implementation artifact
- Committee pre-defense approval

## Lesson Sequence

1. Research Launch and Approved Protocol Verification
2. Site Access, Participant Communication, and Data Governance
3. Pilot Study and Instrument Refinement
4. Data Collection Cycle I
5. Data Quality Review and Audit Trail
6. Data Collection Cycle II
7. Quantitative, Qualitative, or Integrated Analysis I
8. Analysis II and Robustness or Trustworthiness Checks
9. Findings Development and Evidence Displays
10. Interpretation Against Literature and Framework
11. Limitations, Rival Explanations, and Boundary Conditions
12. Professional Recommendations and Implementation Artifact
13. Complete Dissertation or Institutional Report Draft
14. Committee Review, Revision, and Pre-Defense Examination
15. Final Manuscript Approval for Defense

## Completion Requirements

- Earn at least 85 percent for the doctoral course unless a higher candidacy, proposal, dissertation, or defense standard applies.
- Pass every mandatory ethics, integrity, source-control, privacy, safety, equity, and professional-conduct competency.
- Complete all required doctoral writing, research, policy, analytics, leadership, curriculum, technology, peer-review, or defense submissions.
- Correct any source-version, methodological, evidence, attribution, confidentiality, or overclaiming issue identified during review.
- Pass the final course defense, committee review, examination, proposal, dissertation milestone, or assigned remediation.

## Source and Copyright Controls

- Official NCAA men’s and NAIA publications and current official updates are reference authorities; student-facing instruction is original RefZone University language.
- No protected rulebook text, casebook language, mechanics diagrams, game footage, logos, or identifiable participant data may be reproduced without authorization.
- Research literature, legal and policy sources, technology claims, and institutional procedures require source-by-source validation.
- Any uncertainty, source conflict, inaccessible authority, ethics concern, or jurisdiction-specific requirement remains blocked for human review.
