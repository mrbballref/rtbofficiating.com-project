# RZU-NFHS-M 522 — Replay, Correctable Errors, and Atypical Plays

**Program:** NFHS Basketball Officiating Master’s-Level Advanced Officiating Program  
**Academic period:** Year 1 · Term 2  
**Term theme:** Elite Mechanics, Timing, Replay Policy, and Atypical Administration  
**Learning volume:** 3 RefZone graduate credit-equivalent units · 45 engagement hours  
**Prerequisite:** Successful completion of RZU-NFHS-M 521 and all applicable graduate competency gates  
**Credential status:** Professional graduate degree-level pathway; not represented as an accredited degree or transferable graduate credit  
**Publication status:** Complete graduate instructional draft pending qualified rules, mechanics, curriculum, copyright, accessibility, assessment, video, and program review  

A three-unit graduate course in the NFHS Master’s-Level Advanced Officiating pathway focused on replay, correctable errors, and atypical plays. The course combines current-source seminars, original complex cases, advanced film and mechanics analysis, professional communication, applied performance, and oral defense.

## Graduate learning outcomes

- Analyze and defend the advanced competency domain of replay, correctable errors, and atypical plays using current NFHS authority.
- Resolve complex fact patterns through legal status, chronology, definitions, exceptions, mechanics ownership, and complete administration.
- Distinguish legal, illegal, borderline, and correct no-call or no-correction outcomes under normal-speed conditions.
- Produce graduate-level written analysis, film evidence, professional communication, and oral defense.
- Demonstrate advanced leadership, evaluation, or applied officiating behavior appropriate to the course domain.
- Identify source limitations, local-policy requirements, copyright restrictions, and human-review gates.

## Fifteen-week graduate sequence

- **Week 1:** NFHS Replay Reality: Standard Rules Versus Authorized Local Policy
- **Week 2:** Reviewable and Non-Reviewable Elements When Replay Is Authorized
- **Week 3:** Evidence Standards and the Ruling on the Floor
- **Week 4:** Coach-Initiated Review or Challenge Policies Where Authorized
- **Week 5:** Replay Crew Communication and Game Resumption
- **Week 6:** Correctable-Error Categories and Correction Windows
- **Week 7:** Clock and Score Errors With Definite Information · midterm rules memorandum workshop
- **Week 8:** Wrong Shooter, Wrong Basket, and Incorrect Award · written and oral midterm examination
- **Week 9:** Extra Player, Illegal Entry, and Substitution Irregularities
- **Week 10:** Broken Equipment, Horn Failure, Power Failure, and Suspended Games
- **Week 11:** Blood, Injury, Concussion, and Medical Administration
- **Week 12:** Ball Lodged, Overhead Obstruction, and Unusual Boundary Plays
- **Week 13:** Security Threats, Unauthorized Court Entry, and Emergency Action
- **Week 14:** Atypical Plays Laboratory: Chronology to Resumption · final project presentation
- **Week 15:** Replay, Correctable Error, and Rare-Play Comprehensive Exam · oral defense and remediation conference
