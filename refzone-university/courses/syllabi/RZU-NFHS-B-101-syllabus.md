# RZU-NFHS-B 101 — Professional Identity, Ethics, and the Role of the Official

**Program:** NFHS Basketball Officiating Bachelor’s-Level Professional Foundations Program  
**Academic period:** Year 1 · Term 1  
**Term theme:** Professional Identity, Source Literacy, and Officiating Foundations  
**Learning volume:** 5 RefZone credit-equivalent units · 75 engagement hours  
**Prerequisite:** NFHS Bachelor's-Level pathway admission or approved placement  
**Credential status:** Professional degree-level pathway; not represented as an accredited degree or transferable college credit  
**Publication status:** Complete instructional draft pending qualified rules, mechanics, curriculum, copyright, accessibility, video, and program review  

The first academic course in the NFHS Bachelor's-Level Professional Foundations pathway. Students develop the professional identity, ethical judgment, preparation habits, communication standards, and reflective practice required of a complete basketball official.

## Learning outcomes

- Explain the contextual difference between the terms referee and official without treating either title as inferior.
- Describe the education-based mission of NFHS basketball and the official's responsibility to fairness, safety, order, and sportsmanship.
- Apply ethical standards to conflicts, confidentiality, gambling restrictions, social media, bias, reporting, and professional boundaries.
- Use a repeatable assignment-preparation process covering conflicts, crew contact, travel, arrival, uniform, equipment, fitness, and site readiness.
- Communicate rulings and factual information briefly, calmly, neutrally, and professionally.
- Support partners, correct factual errors through proper crew procedure, and receive feedback without defensiveness.
- Create accurate incident documentation that separates observed facts from assumptions and emotion.
- Use film and a learning journal to convert experience into measurable next-game objectives.
