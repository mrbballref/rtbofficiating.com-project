# RZU-NBA-B 113 — Ball-Handler, Screening, Post Play, Cutters, Hand-Offs, Rebounding, and Loose-Ball Judgment

**Program:** NBA Basketball Officiating Bachelor’s-Level Professional Foundations Program
**Units:** 5 · **Hours:** 75
**Prerequisite:** RZU-NBA-B 112
**Status:** Complete instructional production draft. Qualified NBA rules, mechanics, replay, challenge, game operations, referee development, curriculum, copyright, accessibility, video, assessment, research, legal, safeguarding, and administrator review remain required before public publication.

A 5-unit bachelor’s-level professional foundations pathway course focused on ball-handler, screening, post play, cutters, hand-offs, rebounding, and loose-ball judgment, combining original RefZone instruction, official NBA source study, normal-speed film or evidence analysis, communication, assessment, and applied professional performance.

## Learning outcomes
- Select and cite the controlling NBA-specific authority, internal regulations, officiating manual, official interpretation or technical clarification, assignment directive, and underlying NBA provision.
- Apply the course concepts using complete legal-status or evidence sequences.
- Create and solve original legal, illegal, borderline, atypical, and disciplined no-action scenarios.
- Use accurate mechanics, administration, communication, research, or policy methods.
- Defend conclusions at normal speed or against complete evidence and respond to qualified review.
- Complete the course performance task and remediation requirements.

## Lessons
1. Ball-handler freedom of movement, hands, forearms, arm bars, holds, and rerouting
2. Push-offs, hooks, ward-offs, shoulder and hip displacement, and initiated contact
3. Legal and illegal screens, time and distance, movement, width, and blind screens
4. Post play, fronting, arm bars, clamping, backing down, seals, and dislodging
5. Cutters, hand-offs, denial, bumps, grabs, freedom of movement, and help defense
6. Rebounding, legal boxing out, holding, pushing, undercutting, and loose-ball fouls
7. Source Map, Definitions, and Competency Targets: Ball-Handler, Screening, Post Play, Cutters, Hand-Offs, Rebounding, and Loose-Ball Judgment
8. Legal Status and Required Conditions: Ball-Handler, Screening, Post Play, Cutters, Hand-Offs, Rebounding, and Loose-Ball Judgment
9. Legal, Illegal, Borderline, and Correct No-Call Laboratory: Ball-Handler, Screening, Post Play, Cutters, Hand-Offs, Rebounding, and Loose-Ball Judgment
10. Mechanics, Primary Coverage, and Visual Evidence: Ball-Handler, Screening, Post Play, Cutters, Hand-Offs, Rebounding, and Loose-Ball Judgment
11. Penalty, Clock, Shot Clock, Possession, Replay, and Resumption: Ball-Handler, Screening, Post Play, Cutters, Hand-Offs, Rebounding, and Loose-Ball Judgment
12. Communication, Reporting, Film Review, and Course Assessment: Ball-Handler, Screening, Post Play, Cutters, Hand-Offs, Rebounding, and Loose-Ball Judgment
