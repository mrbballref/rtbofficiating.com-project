**Program:** NCAA Division I Women’s Basketball Officiating PhD-Level Research, Leadership, and Elite Officiating Pathway

**Units:** 3 RefZone doctoral credit-equivalent units

## Course Description

A three-unit doctoral course in the NCAA Division I Women’s PhD-Level Research, Leadership, and Elite Officiating pathway focused on rules-change impact analysis and institutional policy research. The course integrates current NCAA women’s playing-rules authority and NCAA Division I administrative authority, doctoral scholarship, original research or policy work, ethics, equity, professional application, peer review, and oral defense.

## Learning Outcomes

- Critically synthesize the doctoral domain of rules-change impact analysis and institutional policy research using current NCAA women’s playing-rules authority and NCAA Division I administrative authority and validated research evidence.
- Design and defend an appropriate research, policy, analytic, leadership, curriculum, technology, or evaluation method.
- Evaluate alternative explanations, uncertainty, ethics, equity, privacy, copyright, governance, and implementation risk.
- Produce original doctoral writing, evidence visualizations, peer review, revision, and oral defense.
- Translate findings into a responsible professional or institutional contribution.
- Maintain complete source versioning, research records, human-review gates, and academic integrity.

## Lesson Sequence

1. The Life Cycle of a Rules Change
2. Problem Definition and Policy Rationale
3. Stakeholder Mapping and Consultation
4. Historical Baselines and Pre-Change Evidence
5. Implementation Fidelity and Training Readiness
6. Officials, Coaches, Players, Table, and Technology Impacts
7. Safety, Fairness, Freedom of Movement, and Game-Flow Outcomes
8. Unintended Consequences and Ruleset Interaction
9. Pilot Programs, Natural Experiments, and Phased Adoption
10. Monitoring Indicators and Post-Implementation Review
11. State Modifications and Cross-Jurisdiction Comparison
12. Writing Comments, Guidance, and Implementation Materials
13. Policy Recommendation and Decision Memo
14. Rules-Change Impact Study Design
15. Institutional Policy Research Defense

## Source and Copyright Controls

- Official NCAA women’s and NCAA Division I publications and current official updates are reference authorities; student-facing instruction is original RefZone University language.
- No protected rulebook text, casebook language, mechanics diagrams, game footage, logos, or identifiable participant data may be reproduced without authorization.
- Research literature, legal and policy sources, technology claims, and institutional procedures require source-by-source validation.
- Any uncertainty, source conflict, inaccessible authority, ethics concern, or jurisdiction-specific requirement remains blocked for human review.
