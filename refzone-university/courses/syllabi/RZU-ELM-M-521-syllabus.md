# RZU-ELM-M 521 — Advanced Game Management, Coach Communication, and In-Arena Conflict

**Program:** EuroLeague Men’s Basketball Officiating Master’s-Level Advanced Officiating Program  
**Year/Term:** Year 2, Term 3  
**Units:** 3 RefZone credit-equivalent units  
**Engagement hours:** 45  
**Credential status:** Professional degree-level pathway; not accredited or transferable academic credit  
**Publication status:** Complete instructional draft pending qualified review

## Course description

A 3-unit graduate-style course focused on advanced game management, coach communication, and in-arena conflict. It combines original RefZone instruction, official-source study, event-specific rules-code selection, film or evidence analysis, communication, assessment, and applied performance.

## Learning outcomes

- Select and cite the controlling Euroleague Basketball adapted authority, bylaws, officiating manual, technical clarification, assignment directive, and underlying FIBA provision.
- Apply the course concepts using complete legal-status or evidence sequences.
- Create and solve original legal, illegal, borderline, atypical, and disciplined no-action scenarios.
- Use accurate mechanics, administration, communication, research, or policy methods.
- Defend conclusions at normal speed or against complete evidence and respond to qualified review.
- Complete the course performance task and remediation requirements.
