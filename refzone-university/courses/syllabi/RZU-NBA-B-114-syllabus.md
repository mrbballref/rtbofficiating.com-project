# RZU-NBA-B 114 — Shooting Fouls, Gather and Continuation, Airborne Shooters, Closeouts, Landing Space, and High-Five Contact

**Program:** NBA Basketball Officiating Bachelor’s-Level Professional Foundations Program
**Units:** 5 · **Hours:** 75
**Prerequisite:** RZU-NBA-B 113
**Status:** Complete instructional production draft. Qualified NBA rules, mechanics, replay, challenge, game operations, referee development, curriculum, copyright, accessibility, video, assessment, research, legal, safeguarding, and administrator review remain required before public publication.

A 5-unit bachelor’s-level professional foundations pathway course focused on shooting fouls, gather and continuation, airborne shooters, closeouts, landing space, and high-five contact, combining original RefZone instruction, official NBA source study, normal-speed film or evidence analysis, communication, assessment, and applied professional performance.

## Learning outcomes
- Select and cite the controlling NBA-specific authority, internal regulations, officiating manual, official interpretation or technical clarification, assignment directive, and underlying NBA provision.
- Apply the course concepts using complete legal-status or evidence sequences.
- Create and solve original legal, illegal, borderline, atypical, and disciplined no-action scenarios.
- Use accurate mechanics, administration, communication, research, or policy methods.
- Defend conclusions at normal speed or against complete evidence and respond to qualified review.
- Complete the course performance task and remediation requirements.

## Lessons
1. Rule 4 gather, field-goal attempt, shooting motion, continuation, and release
2. Airborne-shooter status, landing rights, legal contest, and verticality
3. Arm, wrist, hand, body, hip, and lower-body contact on shots
4. Closeouts, walk-under, undercut, landing-space contact, and injury potential
5. High-five contact, blocked shots, contact after release, and marginal contact
6. Offensive leg extension, kick-out, ward-off, natural motion, and correct ruling
7. Source Map, Definitions, and Competency Targets: Shooting Fouls, Gather and Continuation, Airborne Shooters, Closeouts, Landing Space, and High-Five Contact
8. Legal Status and Required Conditions: Shooting Fouls, Gather and Continuation, Airborne Shooters, Closeouts, Landing Space, and High-Five Contact
9. Legal, Illegal, Borderline, and Correct No-Call Laboratory: Shooting Fouls, Gather and Continuation, Airborne Shooters, Closeouts, Landing Space, and High-Five Contact
10. Mechanics, Primary Coverage, and Visual Evidence: Shooting Fouls, Gather and Continuation, Airborne Shooters, Closeouts, Landing Space, and High-Five Contact
11. Penalty, Clock, Shot Clock, Possession, Replay, and Resumption: Shooting Fouls, Gather and Continuation, Airborne Shooters, Closeouts, Landing Space, and High-Five Contact
12. Communication, Reporting, Film Review, and Course Assessment: Shooting Fouls, Gather and Continuation, Airborne Shooters, Closeouts, Landing Space, and High-Five Contact
