# RZU-USAM-D 771 — Applied Dissertation or Institutional Research Project

**Program:** USA Basketball Men’s Basketball Officiating PhD-Level Research, Leadership, and Elite Officiating Program  
**Year/Term:** Year 4, Term 8  
**Units:** 3 RefZone credit-equivalent units  
**Engagement hours:** 45  
**Credential status:** Professional degree-level pathway; not accredited or transferable academic credit  
**Publication status:** Complete instructional draft pending qualified review

## Course description

A 3-unit doctoral-style course focused on applied dissertation or institutional research project. It combines original RefZone instruction, official-source study, event-specific rules-code selection, film or evidence analysis, communication, assessment, and applied performance.

## Learning outcomes

- Select and cite the controlling USA Basketball, FIBA, or expressly adopted domestic authority.
- Apply the course concepts using complete legal-status or evidence sequences.
- Create and solve original legal, illegal, borderline, atypical, and disciplined no-action scenarios.
- Use accurate mechanics, administration, communication, research, or policy methods.
- Defend conclusions at normal speed or against complete evidence and respond to qualified review.
- Complete the course performance task and remediation requirements.

## Source-control rule

The actual competition, event bulletin, rules code, source version, and effective date must be identified before any ruling or policy conclusion. FIBA 2026 rules are future effective until October 1, 2026. Official publications are reference authorities and are not reproduced word-for-word.
