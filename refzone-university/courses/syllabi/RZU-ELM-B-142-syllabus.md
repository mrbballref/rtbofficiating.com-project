## Course description

A 5-unit undergraduate-style course focused on double fouls, special situations, penalty sequencing, and point of interruption. It combines original RefZone instruction, official-source study, event-specific rules-code selection, film or evidence analysis, communication, assessment, and applied performance.

## Learning outcomes

- Select and cite the controlling Euroleague Basketball adapted authority, bylaws, officiating manual, technical clarification, assignment directive, and underlying FIBA provision.
- Apply the course concepts using complete legal-status or evidence sequences.
- Create and solve original legal, illegal, borderline, atypical, and disciplined no-action scenarios.
- Use accurate mechanics, administration, communication, research, or policy methods.
- Defend conclusions at normal speed or against complete evidence and respond to qualified review.
- Complete the course performance task and remediation requirements.
