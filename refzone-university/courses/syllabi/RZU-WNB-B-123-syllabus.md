# RZU-WNB-B 123 — Applied WNBA Officiating Practicum and Game-Administration Laboratory

**Program:** WNBA Basketball Officiating Bachelor’s-Level Professional Foundations Program
**Units:** 5 · **Hours:** 75
**Prerequisite:** RZU-WNB-B 122
**Status:** Complete instructional production draft. Qualified WNBA rules, mechanics, replay, challenge, game operations, referee development, curriculum, copyright, accessibility, video, assessment, research, legal, safeguarding, and administrator review remain required before public publication.

A 5-unit bachelor’s-level professional foundations pathway course focused on applied wnba officiating practicum and game-administration laboratory, combining original RefZone instruction, official WNBA source study, normal-speed film or evidence analysis, communication, assessment, and applied professional performance.

## Learning outcomes
- Select and cite the controlling WNBA-specific authority, internal regulations, officiating manual, official interpretation or technical clarification, assignment directive, and underlying WNBA provision.
- Apply the course concepts using complete legal-status or evidence sequences.
- Create and solve original legal, illegal, borderline, atypical, and disciplined no-action scenarios.
- Use accurate mechanics, administration, communication, research, or policy methods.
- Defend conclusions at normal speed or against complete evidence and respond to qualified review.
- Complete the course performance task and remediation requirements.

## Lessons
1. Pregame preparation, source verification, and crew conference simulation
2. First-half positioning, primary coverage, whistles, reporting, and communication
3. Second-half adjustment, physicality management, timeout and clock awareness
4. Late-game advancement, shot clock, replay, challenge, and end-of-period simulation
5. Atypical play, injury, security, table error, and incident-report simulation
6. Normal-speed film review, self-evaluation, instructor rubric, and remediation
7. Source Map, Definitions, and Competency Targets: Applied WNBA Officiating Practicum and Game-Administration Laboratory
8. Legal Status and Required Conditions: Applied WNBA Officiating Practicum and Game-Administration Laboratory
9. Legal, Illegal, Borderline, and Correct No-Call Laboratory: Applied WNBA Officiating Practicum and Game-Administration Laboratory
10. Mechanics, Primary Coverage, and Visual Evidence: Applied WNBA Officiating Practicum and Game-Administration Laboratory
11. Penalty, Clock, Shot Clock, Possession, Replay, and Resumption: Applied WNBA Officiating Practicum and Game-Administration Laboratory
12. Communication, Reporting, Film Review, and Course Assessment: Applied WNBA Officiating Practicum and Game-Administration Laboratory
