# RZU-USAW-B 102 — Source Authority, Rules-Code Selection, and Effective-Date Control

**Program:** USA Basketball Women’s Officiating Bachelor’s-Level Professional Foundations Program  
**Year/Term:** Year 1, Term 1  
**Units:** 5 RefZone credit-equivalent units  
**Engagement hours:** 75  
**Credential status:** Professional degree-level pathway; not accredited or transferable academic credit  
**Publication status:** Complete instructional draft pending qualified review

## Course description

A 5-unit undergraduate-style course focused on source authority, rules-code selection, and effective-date control. It combines original RefZone instruction, official-source study, event-specific rules-code selection, film or evidence analysis, communication, assessment, and applied performance.

## Learning outcomes

- Select and cite the controlling USA Basketball, FIBA, or expressly adopted domestic authority.
- Apply the course concepts using complete legal-status or evidence sequences.
- Create and solve original legal, illegal, borderline, atypical, and disciplined no-action scenarios.
- Use accurate mechanics, administration, communication, research, or policy methods.
- Defend conclusions at normal speed or against complete evidence and respond to qualified review.
- Complete the course performance task and remediation requirements.

## Required lessons

1. USA Basketball event bulletins and assignment directives
2. FIBA 2024 current-effective rules
3. FIBA 2026 future-effective rules transition
4. Official interpretations and competition regulations
5. USA Basketball Youth Guidelines versus event-specific modifications
6. NFHS, FIBA, NCAA, NBA, and 3x3 source separation
7. Source Map, Definitions, and Competency Targets: Source Authority, Rules-Code Selection, and Effective-Date Control
8. Legal Status and Required Conditions: Source Authority, Rules-Code Selection, and Effective-Date Control
9. Legal, Illegal, Borderline, and No-Call Laboratory: Source Authority, Rules-Code Selection, and Effective-Date Control
10. Mechanics, Primary Coverage, and Visual Evidence: Source Authority, Rules-Code Selection, and Effective-Date Control
11. Penalty, Clock, Shot-Clock, Possession, and Resumption: Source Authority, Rules-Code Selection, and Effective-Date Control
12. Communication, Reporting, Film Review, and Course Assessment: Source Authority, Rules-Code Selection, and Effective-Date Control

## Source-control rule

The actual competition, event bulletin, rules code, source version, and effective date must be identified before any ruling or policy conclusion. FIBA 2026 rules are future effective until October 1, 2026. Official publications are reference authorities and are not reproduced word-for-word.
