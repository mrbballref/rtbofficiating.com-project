# RZU-NFHS-B 102 — Governing Bodies, Source Authority, and Rulebook Literacy

**Program:** NFHS Basketball Officiating Bachelor’s-Level Professional Foundations Program  
**Academic period:** Year 1 · Term 1  
**Term theme:** Professional Identity, Source Literacy, and Officiating Foundations  
**Learning volume:** 5 RefZone credit-equivalent units · 75 engagement hours  
**Prerequisite:** Successful completion of RZU-NFHS-B 101 and all applicable competency gates  
**Credential status:** Professional degree-level pathway; not represented as an accredited degree or transferable college credit  
**Publication status:** Complete instructional draft pending qualified rules, mechanics, curriculum, copyright, accessibility, video, and program review  

A five-unit academic course in the NFHS Bachelor's-Level Professional Foundations pathway focused on governing bodies, source authority, and rulebook literacy. The course combines original RefZone instruction, authorized-source study, case-play analysis, film review, communication practice, and applied performance.

## Learning outcomes

- Explain and apply the complete competency domain of governing bodies, source authority, and rulebook literacy using current NFHS authority.
- Identify legal status, primary coverage, required visual evidence, exceptions, and administration before making a ruling.
- Create and solve original legal, illegal, borderline, atypical, and correct no-call or no-correction scenarios.
- Use correct mechanics, signals, reporting, communication, clock, possession, penalty, and resumption procedures.
- Analyze performance at normal speed, use slow motion for facts, and defend conclusions with source and film evidence.
- Complete the course performance task and remediation requirements at the approved competency standard.
- Maintain a versioned source record and resolve source conflicts without treating unofficial summaries as authority.

## Source, copyright, and academic-integrity standard

Official NFHS publications and current state, league, tournament, and assigning-authority policies control accuracy. The course uses original RefZone University explanations, scenarios, assessments, diagrams, dialogue, and video scripts. Protected publication text, case language, mechanics diagrams, footage, logos, and likenesses are not reproduced without authorization.
