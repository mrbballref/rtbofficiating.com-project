# RZU-WNB-B 104 — Players, Substitutes, Coaches, Team Personnel, Uniforms, and Core Definitions

**Program:** WNBA Basketball Officiating Bachelor’s-Level Professional Foundations Program
**Units:** 5 · **Hours:** 75
**Prerequisite:** RZU-WNB-B 103
**Status:** Complete instructional production draft. Qualified WNBA rules, mechanics, replay, challenge, game operations, referee development, curriculum, copyright, accessibility, video, assessment, research, legal, safeguarding, and administrator review remain required before public publication.

A 5-unit bachelor’s-level professional foundations pathway course focused on players, substitutes, coaches, team personnel, uniforms, and core definitions, combining original RefZone instruction, official WNBA source study, normal-speed film or evidence analysis, communication, assessment, and applied professional performance.

## Learning outcomes
- Select and cite the controlling WNBA-specific authority, internal regulations, officiating manual, official interpretation or technical clarification, assignment directive, and underlying WNBA provision.
- Apply the course concepts using complete legal-status or evidence sequences.
- Create and solve original legal, illegal, borderline, atypical, and disciplined no-action scenarios.
- Use accurate mechanics, administration, communication, research, or policy methods.
- Defend conclusions at normal speed or against complete evidence and respond to qualified review.
- Complete the course performance task and remediation requirements.

5. Pivot, traveling, screen, field-goal attempt, throw-in, last two minutes, and suspension
6. Point of interruption, team control, team possession, fumble, and status combinations
7. Source Map, Definitions, and Competency Targets: Players, Substitutes, Coaches, Team Personnel, Uniforms, and Core Definitions
8. Legal Status and Required Conditions: Players, Substitutes, Coaches, Team Personnel, Uniforms, and Core Definitions
9. Legal, Illegal, Borderline, and Correct No-Call Laboratory: Players, Substitutes, Coaches, Team Personnel, Uniforms, and Core Definitions
10. Mechanics, Primary Coverage, and Visual Evidence: Players, Substitutes, Coaches, Team Personnel, Uniforms, and Core Definitions
11. Penalty, Clock, Shot Clock, Possession, Replay, and Resumption: Players, Substitutes, Coaches, Team Personnel, Uniforms, and Core Definitions
