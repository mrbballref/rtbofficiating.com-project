**Program:** NJCAA Men’s Basketball Officiating PhD-Level Research, Leadership, and Elite Officiating Pathway  
**Academic placement:** Year 2, Term 3  
**Units:** 3 RefZone doctoral credit-equivalent units

**Publication status:** Complete instructional production draft; required human review remains pending  

## Course Description

A three-unit doctoral course in the NJCAA Men’s PhD-Level Research, Leadership, and Elite Officiating pathway focused on quantitative research methods for officiating. The course integrates current NCAA men’s playing-rules authority and NJCAA administrative authority, doctoral scholarship, original research or policy work, ethics, equity, professional application, peer review, and oral defense.

## Learning Outcomes

- Critically synthesize the doctoral domain of quantitative research methods for officiating using current NCAA men’s playing-rules authority and NJCAA administrative authority and validated research evidence.
- Design and defend an appropriate research, policy, analytic, leadership, curriculum, technology, or evaluation method.
- Evaluate alternative explanations, uncertainty, ethics, equity, privacy, copyright, governance, and implementation risk.
- Produce original doctoral writing, evidence visualizations, peer review, revision, and oral defense.
- Translate findings into a responsible professional or institutional contribution.
- Maintain complete source versioning, research records, human-review gates, and academic integrity.

## Lesson Sequence

1. Quantitative Questions in Officiating Research
2. Variables, Operational Definitions, and Measurement Scales
3. Populations, Samples, Frames, and Selection Bias
4. Descriptive Statistics for Officiating Data
5. Probability, Uncertainty, and Sampling Distributions
6. Confidence Intervals and Practical Significance
7. Hypothesis Testing and Error Control
8. Correlation, Association, and Causation
9. Regression Models for Performance and Decision Data
10. Repeated Measures, Nested Crews, and Longitudinal Designs
11. Experimental and Quasi-Experimental Training Studies
12. Power, Sample Size, and Feasibility
13. Data Cleaning, Missing Data, and Reproducibility
14. Quantitative Results Tables and Visualizations
15. Quantitative Methods Examination and Analysis Plan

## Source and Copyright Controls

- Official NCAA men’s and NJCAA publications and current official updates are reference authorities; student-facing instruction is original RefZone University language.
- No protected rulebook text, casebook language, mechanics diagrams, game footage, logos, or identifiable participant data may be reproduced without authorization.
- Research literature, legal and policy sources, technology claims, and institutional procedures require source-by-source validation.
- Any uncertainty, source conflict, inaccessible authority, ethics concern, or jurisdiction-specific requirement remains blocked for human review.
