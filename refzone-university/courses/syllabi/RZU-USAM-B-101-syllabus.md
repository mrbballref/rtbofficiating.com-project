# RZU-USAM-B 101 — Professional Identity, Ethics, and the USA Basketball Officiating Environment

**Program:** USA Basketball Men’s Basketball Officiating Bachelor’s-Level Professional Foundations Program  
**Year/Term:** Year 1, Term 1  
**Units:** 5 RefZone credit-equivalent units  
**Engagement hours:** 75  
**Credential status:** Professional degree-level pathway; not accredited or transferable academic credit  
**Publication status:** Complete instructional draft pending qualified review

## Course description

A 5-unit undergraduate-style course focused on professional identity, ethics, and the usa basketball officiating environment. It combines original RefZone instruction, official-source study, event-specific rules-code selection, film or evidence analysis, communication, assessment, and applied performance.

## Learning outcomes

- Select and cite the controlling USA Basketball, FIBA, or expressly adopted domestic authority.
- Apply the course concepts using complete legal-status or evidence sequences.
- Create and solve original legal, illegal, borderline, atypical, and disciplined no-action scenarios.
- Use accurate mechanics, administration, communication, research, or policy methods.
- Defend conclusions at normal speed or against complete evidence and respond to qualified review.
- Complete the course performance task and remediation requirements.

## Required lessons

1. USA Basketball as the national governing body
2. The difference between referee and complete official
3. Service, impartiality, and national-team responsibility
4. Ethics, integrity, gambling restrictions, and confidentiality
5. Safe Sport awareness and mandatory reporting boundaries
6. Assignment acceptance, conflicts, reliability, and travel readiness
7. Source Map, Definitions, and Competency Targets: Professional Identity, Ethics, and the USA Basketball Officiating Environment
8. Legal Status and Required Conditions: Professional Identity, Ethics, and the USA Basketball Officiating Environment
9. Legal, Illegal, Borderline, and No-Call Laboratory: Professional Identity, Ethics, and the USA Basketball Officiating Environment
10. Mechanics, Primary Coverage, and Visual Evidence: Professional Identity, Ethics, and the USA Basketball Officiating Environment
11. Penalty, Clock, Shot-Clock, Possession, and Resumption: Professional Identity, Ethics, and the USA Basketball Officiating Environment
12. Communication, Reporting, Film Review, and Course Assessment: Professional Identity, Ethics, and the USA Basketball Officiating Environment

## Source-control rule

The actual competition, event bulletin, rules code, source version, and effective date must be identified before any ruling or policy conclusion. FIBA 2026 rules are future effective until October 1, 2026. Official publications are reference authorities and are not reproduced word-for-word.
