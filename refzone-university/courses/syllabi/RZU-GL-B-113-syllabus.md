A 5-unit bachelor’s-level professional foundations pathway course focused on shooting fouls, continuation, airborne shooters, closeouts, landing space, and high-five contact, combining original RefZone instruction, official-source study, film or evidence analysis, communication, assessment, and applied professional performance.

## Learning outcomes
- Select and cite the controlling NBA G League-specific authority, internal regulations, officiating manual, official interpretation or technical clarification, assignment directive, and underlying NBA G League provision.
- Apply the course concepts using complete legal-status or evidence sequences.
- Create and solve original legal, illegal, borderline, atypical, and disciplined no-action scenarios.
- Use accurate mechanics, administration, communication, research, or policy methods.
- Defend conclusions at normal speed or against complete evidence and respond to qualified review.
- Complete the course performance task and remediation requirements.

## Lessons
1. Act of shooting, gather, continuation, and release
2. Airborne-shooter status and landing rights
3. Legal closeout versus undercut or walk-under contact
4. Contact to shooting arm, hand, wrist, body, hip, and legs
5. High-five contact and 2025-26 landing-space points of emphasis
6. Offensive leg extension, unnatural motion, and created contact
7. Source Map, Definitions, and Competency Targets: Shooting Fouls, Continuation, Airborne Shooters, Closeouts, Landing Space, and High-Five Contact
8. Legal Status and Required Conditions: Shooting Fouls, Continuation, Airborne Shooters, Closeouts, Landing Space, and High-Five Contact
9. Legal, Illegal, Borderline, and Correct No-Call Laboratory: Shooting Fouls, Continuation, Airborne Shooters, Closeouts, Landing Space, and High-Five Contact
10. Mechanics, Primary Coverage, and Visual Evidence: Shooting Fouls, Continuation, Airborne Shooters, Closeouts, Landing Space, and High-Five Contact
11. Penalty, Clock, Shot-Clock, Possession, and Resumption: Shooting Fouls, Continuation, Airborne Shooters, Closeouts, Landing Space, and High-Five Contact
12. Communication, Reporting, Film Review, and Course Assessment: Shooting Fouls, Continuation, Airborne Shooters, Closeouts, Landing Space, and High-Five Contact
