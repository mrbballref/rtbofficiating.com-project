# RZU-NBA-B 109 — Violations I: Out of Bounds, Dribbling, Traveling, Pivoting, Throw-Ins, and Striking the Ball

**Program:** NBA Basketball Officiating Bachelor’s-Level Professional Foundations Program
**Units:** 5 · **Hours:** 75
**Prerequisite:** RZU-NBA-B 108
**Status:** Complete instructional production draft. Qualified NBA rules, mechanics, replay, challenge, game operations, referee development, curriculum, copyright, accessibility, video, assessment, research, legal, safeguarding, and administrator review remain required before public publication.

A 5-unit bachelor’s-level professional foundations pathway course focused on violations i: out of bounds, dribbling, traveling, pivoting, throw-ins, and striking the ball, combining original RefZone instruction, official NBA source study, normal-speed film or evidence analysis, communication, assessment, and applied professional performance.

## Learning outcomes
- Select and cite the controlling NBA-specific authority, internal regulations, officiating manual, official interpretation or technical clarification, assignment directive, and underlying NBA provision.
- Apply the course concepts using complete legal-status or evidence sequences.
- Create and solve original legal, illegal, borderline, atypical, and disciplined no-action scenarios.
- Use accurate mechanics, administration, communication, research, or policy methods.
- Defend conclusions at normal speed or against complete evidence and respond to qualified review.
- Complete the course performance task and remediation requirements.

3. Traveling, gather, pivot foot, starts, stops, jump stops, and step-throughs
4. Thrower-in restrictions, plane violations, counts, delay, and illegal assistance
5. Kicking, punching, striking, and intentionally using the leg or fist
6. Whistle, signal, throw-in location, clock, shot clock, direction, and myths
7. Source Map, Definitions, and Competency Targets: Violations I: Out of Bounds, Dribbling, Traveling, Pivoting, Throw-Ins, and Striking the Ball
8. Legal Status and Required Conditions: Violations I: Out of Bounds, Dribbling, Traveling, Pivoting, Throw-Ins, and Striking the Ball
9. Legal, Illegal, Borderline, and Correct No-Call Laboratory: Violations I: Out of Bounds, Dribbling, Traveling, Pivoting, Throw-Ins, and Striking the Ball
10. Mechanics, Primary Coverage, and Visual Evidence: Violations I: Out of Bounds, Dribbling, Traveling, Pivoting, Throw-Ins, and Striking the Ball
11. Penalty, Clock, Shot Clock, Possession, Replay, and Resumption: Violations I: Out of Bounds, Dribbling, Traveling, Pivoting, Throw-Ins, and Striking the Ball
12. Communication, Reporting, Film Review, and Course Assessment: Violations I: Out of Bounds, Dribbling, Traveling, Pivoting, Throw-Ins, and Striking the Ball
