# RZU-NJCW-B 132 — Team, Player/Substitute, Bench, Flagrant, Fighting, and Disqualification Rules

**Program:** NJCAA Women’s Basketball Officiating Bachelor’s-Level Professional Foundations Program  
**Academic period:** Year 2 · Term 4  
**Term theme:** Replay, Mechanics, and Collegiate Game Management  
**Learning volume:** 5 RefZone credit-equivalent units · 75 engagement hours  
**Prerequisite:** Successful completion of RZU-NJCW-B 131 and all applicable competency gates  
**Credential status:** Professional degree-level pathway; not an accredited degree or transferable college credit  
**Rules basis:** 2025-26 NCAA Women’s Rules Book and Casebook, current approved rulings, current-cycle rules changes and points of emphasis, and verified NJCAA procedures  

A five-unit academic course in the NJCAA Women Bachelor’s-Level Professional Foundations pathway focused on team, player/substitute, bench, flagrant, fighting, and disqualification rules. The course combines original RefZone instruction, official-source study, approved-ruling analysis, film review, communication practice, and applied performance.

## Learning outcomes

- Explain and apply team, player/substitute, bench, flagrant, fighting, and disqualification rules using current NCAA women’s authority and verified NJCAA procedures.
- Identify legal status, primary coverage, required visual evidence, exceptions, and administration before ruling.
- Create and solve original legal, illegal, borderline, atypical, and correct no-call scenarios.
- Use correct mechanics, signals, reporting, communication, clock, shot-clock, replay, possession, penalty, and resumption procedures.
- Analyze performance at normal speed, use slow motion only for facts, and defend conclusions with source and film evidence.
- Complete the performance task and remediation requirements at the approved competency standard.

## Lessons

### 1. Team Technical Fouls, Warnings, and Delay Administration

This lesson develops team technical fouls, warnings, and delay administration within Team, Player/Substitute, Bench, Flagrant, Fighting, and Disqualification Rules. Students establish the controlling status, observe the complete action, apply current NCAA women’s rules and verified NJCAA procedures, administer the full result, communicate professionally, and convert the play into a repeatable next-game process.

**Required evidence**

- Read the assigned current rule and related approved rulings; record definitions, conditions, exceptions, penalty, and effective date without copying protected text.
- Write a one-page decision tree for the lesson.
- Create one original legal, illegal, borderline, and correct no-call or no-correction example.
- Complete a normal-speed recognition drill and use slow motion only to identify facts.

- RZU-NJCW-B 132 source and terminology notebook

## Completion requirements

- Earn at least 80 percent for the academic course.
- Pass mandatory rules, penalty, timing, shot-clock, replay, safety, ethics, and source-control competencies assigned to the course.

- Correct any superseded-source, rules-code, or administration error identified during review.
