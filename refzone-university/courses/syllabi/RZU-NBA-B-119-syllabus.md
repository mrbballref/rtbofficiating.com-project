# RZU-NBA-B 119 — Three-Person Mechanics: Crew Chief and Referees, Lead, Trail, Center, Rotations, and Reporting

**Program:** NBA Basketball Officiating Bachelor’s-Level Professional Foundations Program
**Units:** 5 · **Hours:** 75
**Prerequisite:** RZU-NBA-B 118
**Status:** Complete instructional production draft. Qualified NBA rules, mechanics, replay, challenge, game operations, referee development, curriculum, copyright, accessibility, video, assessment, research, legal, safeguarding, and administrator review remain required before public publication.

A 5-unit bachelor’s-level professional foundations pathway course focused on three-person mechanics: crew chief and referees, lead, trail, center, rotations, and reporting, combining original RefZone instruction, official NBA source study, normal-speed film or evidence analysis, communication, assessment, and applied professional performance.

## Learning outcomes
- Select and cite the controlling NBA-specific authority, internal regulations, officiating manual, official interpretation or technical clarification, assignment directive, and underlying NBA provision.
- Apply the course concepts using complete legal-status or evidence sequences.
- Create and solve original legal, illegal, borderline, atypical, and disciplined no-action scenarios.
- Use accurate mechanics, administration, communication, research, or policy methods.
- Defend conclusions at normal speed or against complete evidence and respond to qualified review.
- Complete the course performance task and remediation requirements.

## Lessons
1. Crew designations: crew chief and two referees
2. Lead position, baseline, post, paint, rebounding, and rotation initiation
3. Trail position, ball coverage, shot clock, transition, three-point attempts, and last shot
4. Center position, weak-side action, cutters, drives, rebounding, and help
5. Rotations, switches, transition, press, free throws, throw-ins, timeouts, and intermissions
6. Double whistles, preliminary signals, reporting, new positions, and crew conferences
7. Source Map, Definitions, and Competency Targets: Three-Person Mechanics: Crew Chief and Referees, Lead, Trail, Center, Rotations, and Reporting
8. Legal Status and Required Conditions: Three-Person Mechanics: Crew Chief and Referees, Lead, Trail, Center, Rotations, and Reporting
9. Legal, Illegal, Borderline, and Correct No-Call Laboratory: Three-Person Mechanics: Crew Chief and Referees, Lead, Trail, Center, Rotations, and Reporting
10. Mechanics, Primary Coverage, and Visual Evidence: Three-Person Mechanics: Crew Chief and Referees, Lead, Trail, Center, Rotations, and Reporting
11. Penalty, Clock, Shot Clock, Possession, Replay, and Resumption: Three-Person Mechanics: Crew Chief and Referees, Lead, Trail, Center, Rotations, and Reporting
12. Communication, Reporting, Film Review, and Course Assessment: Three-Person Mechanics: Crew Chief and Referees, Lead, Trail, Center, Rotations, and Reporting
