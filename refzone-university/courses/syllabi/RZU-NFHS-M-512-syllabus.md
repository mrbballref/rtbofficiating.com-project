# RZU-NFHS-M 512 — Advanced Three-Person Mechanics and Coverage Extensions

**Program:** NFHS Basketball Officiating Master’s-Level Advanced Officiating Program  
**Academic period:** Year 1 · Term 2  
**Term theme:** Elite Mechanics, Timing, Replay Policy, and Atypical Administration  
**Learning volume:** 3 RefZone graduate credit-equivalent units · 45 engagement hours  
**Prerequisite:** Successful completion of RZU-NFHS-M 511 and all applicable graduate competency gates  
**Credential status:** Professional graduate degree-level pathway; not represented as an accredited degree or transferable graduate credit  
**Publication status:** Complete graduate instructional draft pending qualified rules, mechanics, curriculum, copyright, accessibility, assessment, video, and program review  

A three-unit graduate course in the NFHS Master’s-Level Advanced Officiating pathway focused on advanced three-person mechanics and coverage extensions. The course combines current-source seminars, original complex cases, advanced film and mechanics analysis, professional communication, applied performance, and oral defense.

## Graduate learning outcomes

- Analyze and defend the advanced competency domain of advanced three-person mechanics and coverage extensions using current NFHS authority.
- Resolve complex fact patterns through legal status, chronology, definitions, exceptions, mechanics ownership, and complete administration.
- Distinguish legal, illegal, borderline, and correct no-call or no-correction outcomes under normal-speed conditions.
- Produce graduate-level written analysis, film evidence, professional communication, and oral defense.
- Demonstrate advanced leadership, evaluation, or applied officiating behavior appropriate to the course domain.
- Identify source limitations, local-policy requirements, copyright restrictions, and human-review gates.

## Fifteen-week graduate sequence

- **Week 1:** Graduate Three-Person Mechanics Philosophy
- **Week 2:** Lead Positioning, Matchup Discipline, and Baseline Adjustments
- **Week 3:** Center Positioning, Weak-Side Awareness, and Drive Coverage
- **Week 4:** Trail Positioning, Perimeter Matchups, and Clock Responsibilities
- **Week 5:** Primary Coverage, Secondary Coverage, and Extended Coverage
- **Week 6:** Lead-Initiated Rotations and Crew Response
- **Week 7:** Late, Aborted, and Emergency Rotations · midterm rules memorandum workshop
- **Week 8:** Drives, Secondary Defenders, and Restricted-Angle Problems · written and oral midterm examination
- **Week 9:** Post Play, Screening, and Off-Ball Coverage Distribution
- **Week 10:** Rebounding, Goaltending Help, and Basket Interference Support
- **Week 11:** Transition, Press, Long Passes, and Fast-Break Coverage
- **Week 12:** Throw-Ins, Free Throws, Time-Outs, and Intermissions
- **Week 13:** Double Whistles, Help Calls, and Factual Crew Conferences
- **Week 14:** End-of-Period Mechanics and Last-Shot Responsibilities · final project presentation
- **Week 15:** Advanced Three-Person Mechanics Film Practical · oral defense and remediation conference

RefZone University course text, scenarios, assessments, diagrams, dialogue, film prompts, and video scripts are original. Protected publication text, case language, mechanics diagrams, footage, logos, and likenesses are not reproduced without authorization.
