# RZU-NBA-B 115 — Technical Fouls, Delay, Conduct, Taunting, Fighting, Fines, Ejections, and Incident Reporting

**Program:** NBA Basketball Officiating Bachelor’s-Level Professional Foundations Program
**Units:** 5 · **Hours:** 75
**Prerequisite:** RZU-NBA-B 114
**Status:** Complete instructional production draft. Qualified NBA rules, mechanics, replay, challenge, game operations, referee development, curriculum, copyright, accessibility, video, assessment, research, legal, safeguarding, and administrator review remain required before public publication.

A 5-unit bachelor’s-level professional foundations pathway course focused on technical fouls, delay, conduct, taunting, fighting, fines, ejections, and incident reporting, combining original RefZone instruction, official NBA source study, normal-speed film or evidence analysis, communication, assessment, and applied professional performance.

## Learning outcomes
- Select and cite the controlling NBA-specific authority, internal regulations, officiating manual, official interpretation or technical clarification, assignment directive, and underlying NBA provision.
- Apply the course concepts using complete legal-status or evidence sequences.
- Create and solve original legal, illegal, borderline, atypical, and disciplined no-action scenarios.
- Use accurate mechanics, administration, communication, research, or policy methods.
- Defend conclusions at normal speed or against complete evidence and respond to qualified review.
- Complete the course performance task and remediation requirements.

## Lessons
1. Rule 12A excessive timeouts, delay of game, number of players, and equipment conduct
2. Player, coach, trainer, bench, profanity, disrespect, taunting, and escalation
3. Non-unsportsmanlike and unsportsmanlike technical classifications and penalties
4. Fighting fouls, leaving the bench, restraining, peacemaking, and escalation
5. Ejection, disqualification, fines, security involvement, and safe separation
6. Incident facts, names, times, locations, reports, supervisor contact, and confidentiality
7. Source Map, Definitions, and Competency Targets: Technical Fouls, Delay, Conduct, Taunting, Fighting, Fines, Ejections, and Incident Reporting
8. Legal Status and Required Conditions: Technical Fouls, Delay, Conduct, Taunting, Fighting, Fines, Ejections, and Incident Reporting
9. Legal, Illegal, Borderline, and Correct No-Call Laboratory: Technical Fouls, Delay, Conduct, Taunting, Fighting, Fines, Ejections, and Incident Reporting
10. Mechanics, Primary Coverage, and Visual Evidence: Technical Fouls, Delay, Conduct, Taunting, Fighting, Fines, Ejections, and Incident Reporting
11. Penalty, Clock, Shot Clock, Possession, Replay, and Resumption: Technical Fouls, Delay, Conduct, Taunting, Fighting, Fines, Ejections, and Incident Reporting
12. Communication, Reporting, Film Review, and Course Assessment: Technical Fouls, Delay, Conduct, Taunting, Fighting, Fines, Ejections, and Incident Reporting
