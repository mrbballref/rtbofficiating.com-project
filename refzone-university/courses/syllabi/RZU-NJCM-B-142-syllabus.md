# RZU-NJCM-B 142 — Three-Person Mechanics II: Rotations, Transition, Press, and Restarts

**Program:** NJCAA Men’s Basketball Officiating Bachelor’s-Level Professional Foundations Program  
**Academic period:** Year 3 · Term 5  
**Term theme:** Advanced Contact Judgment and Three-Person Coverage  
**Learning volume:** 5 RefZone credit-equivalent units · 75 engagement hours  
**Prerequisite:** Successful completion of RZU-NJCM-B 141 and all applicable competency gates  
**Credential status:** Professional degree-level pathway; not an accredited degree or transferable college credit  
**Rules basis:** 2025-26 NCAA Men’s Rules Book and Casebook, current corrections/interpretations, 2026-27 approved rulings and points of emphasis, and verified NJCAA procedures  

A five-unit academic course in the NJCAA Men Bachelor’s-Level Professional Foundations pathway focused on three-person mechanics ii: rotations, transition, press, and restarts. The course combines original RefZone instruction, official-source study, approved-ruling analysis, film review, communication practice, and applied performance.

## Learning outcomes

- Explain and apply three-person mechanics ii: rotations, transition, press, and restarts using current NCAA men’s authority and verified NJCAA procedures.
- Identify legal status, primary coverage, required visual evidence, exceptions, and administration before ruling.
- Create and solve original legal, illegal, borderline, atypical, and correct no-call scenarios.
- Use correct mechanics, signals, reporting, communication, clock, shot-clock, replay, possession, penalty, and resumption procedures.
- Analyze performance at normal speed, use slow motion only for facts, and defend conclusions with source and film evidence.
- Complete the performance task and remediation requirements at the approved competency standard.
