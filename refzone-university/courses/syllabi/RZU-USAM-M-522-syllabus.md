# RZU-USAM-M 522 — Advanced Game Management and Intercultural Communication

**Program:** USA Basketball Men’s Basketball Officiating Master’s-Level Advanced Officiating Program  
**Year/Term:** Year 2, Term 3  
**Units:** 3 RefZone credit-equivalent units  
**Engagement hours:** 45  
**Credential status:** Professional degree-level pathway; not accredited or transferable academic credit  
**Publication status:** Complete instructional draft pending qualified review

## Course description

A 3-unit graduate-style course focused on advanced game management and intercultural communication. It combines original RefZone instruction, official-source study, event-specific rules-code selection, film or evidence analysis, communication, assessment, and applied performance.

## Learning outcomes

- Select and cite the controlling USA Basketball, FIBA, or expressly adopted domestic authority.
- Apply the course concepts using complete legal-status or evidence sequences.
- Create and solve original legal, illegal, borderline, atypical, and disciplined no-action scenarios.
- Use accurate mechanics, administration, communication, research, or policy methods.
- Defend conclusions at normal speed or against complete evidence and respond to qualified review.
- Complete the course performance task and remediation requirements.

## Required lessons

1. Preventive communication
2. Coach disagreement and de-escalation
3. Player emotion, taunting, and conduct
4. Crew unity and partner support
5. Table and commissioner communication
6. Cultural awareness and language differences
7. Crisis, safety, and security communication
8. Postgame supervisor reporting
9. Graduate Source Seminar and Conflict Resolution: Advanced Game Management and Intercultural Communication
10. Advanced Legal-Status Reconstruction: Advanced Game Management and Intercultural Communication
11. Complex Legal, Illegal, Borderline, and Withhold-Action Laboratory: Advanced Game Management and Intercultural Communication
12. Advanced Mechanics and Coverage Defense: Advanced Game Management and Intercultural Communication
13. Penalty, Replay, Timing, and Resumption Sequencing: Advanced Game Management and Intercultural Communication
14. Normal-Speed Film Defense and Performance Analytics: Advanced Game Management and Intercultural Communication
15. Graduate Memorandum, Practical Examination, and Oral Defense: Advanced Game Management and Intercultural Communication

## Source-control rule

The actual competition, event bulletin, rules code, source version, and effective date must be identified before any ruling or policy conclusion. FIBA 2026 rules are future effective until October 1, 2026. Official publications are reference authorities and are not reproduced word-for-word.
