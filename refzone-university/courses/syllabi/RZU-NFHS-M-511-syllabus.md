# RZU-NFHS-M 511 — Advanced Contact Judgment and Correct No-Call Philosophy

**Program:** NFHS Basketball Officiating Master’s-Level Advanced Officiating Program  
**Academic period:** Year 1 · Term 1  
**Term theme:** Advanced Rules Interpretation, Penalty Sequencing, and Contact Judgment  
**Learning volume:** 3 RefZone graduate credit-equivalent units · 45 engagement hours  
**Prerequisite:** Successful completion of RZU-NFHS-M 502 and all applicable graduate competency gates  
**Credential status:** Professional graduate degree-level pathway; not represented as an accredited degree or transferable graduate credit  
**Publication status:** Complete graduate instructional draft pending qualified rules, mechanics, curriculum, copyright, accessibility, assessment, video, and program review  

A three-unit graduate course in the NFHS Master’s-Level Advanced Officiating pathway focused on advanced contact judgment and correct no-call philosophy. The course combines current-source seminars, original complex cases, advanced film and mechanics analysis, professional communication, applied performance, and oral defense.

## Graduate learning outcomes

- Analyze and defend the advanced competency domain of advanced contact judgment and correct no-call philosophy using current NFHS authority.
- Resolve complex fact patterns through legal status, chronology, definitions, exceptions, mechanics ownership, and complete administration.
- Distinguish legal, illegal, borderline, and correct no-call or no-correction outcomes under normal-speed conditions.
- Produce graduate-level written analysis, film evidence, professional communication, and oral defense.
- Demonstrate advanced leadership, evaluation, or applied officiating behavior appropriate to the course domain.
- Identify source limitations, local-policy requirements, copyright restrictions, and human-review gates.

## Fifteen-week graduate sequence

- **Week 1:** Graduate Contact-Judgment Framework
- **Week 2:** Legal Status Before Contact
- **Week 3:** Incidental, Marginal, and Illegal Contact
- **Week 4:** Ball-Handler Freedom of Movement and Defender Legality
- **Week 5:** Push-Offs, Ward-Offs, Hooks, and Offensive Displacement
- **Week 6:** Post Play: Position, Leverage, Holding, and Dislodging
- **Week 7:** Screening: Time, Distance, Movement, and Extended Body Parts · midterm rules memorandum workshop
- **Week 8:** Cutters, Denial, Rerouting, and Off-Ball Freedom of Movement · written and oral midterm examination
- **Week 9:** Rebounding, Boxing Out, Landing Space, and Verticality
- **Week 10:** Shooters, Airborne Shooters, Closeouts, and Body Contact
- **Week 11:** Hard Fouls, Excessive Contact, and Upgrade Considerations
- **Week 12:** Patient Whistles, Secondary Help, and Double Whistles
- **Week 13:** Correct No-Call Defense and Professional Explanation
- **Week 14:** Consistency, Game Flow, and Bias Control · final project presentation
- **Week 15:** Advanced Contact Film Defense Examination · oral defense and remediation conference
