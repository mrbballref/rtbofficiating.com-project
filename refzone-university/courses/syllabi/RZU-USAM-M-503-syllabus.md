# RZU-USAM-M 503 — Advanced Contact Judgment and Correct No-Call Philosophy

**Program:** USA Basketball Men’s Basketball Officiating Master’s-Level Advanced Officiating Program  
**Year/Term:** Year 1, Term 1  
**Units:** 3 RefZone credit-equivalent units  
**Engagement hours:** 45  
**Credential status:** Professional degree-level pathway; not accredited or transferable academic credit  
**Publication status:** Complete instructional draft pending qualified review

## Course description

A 3-unit graduate-style course focused on advanced contact judgment and correct no-call philosophy. It combines original RefZone instruction, official-source study, event-specific rules-code selection, film or evidence analysis, communication, assessment, and applied performance.

## Learning outcomes

- Select and cite the controlling USA Basketball, FIBA, or expressly adopted domestic authority.
- Apply the course concepts using complete legal-status or evidence sequences.
- Create and solve original legal, illegal, borderline, atypical, and disciplined no-action scenarios.
- Use accurate mechanics, administration, communication, research, or policy methods.
- Defend conclusions at normal speed or against complete evidence and respond to qualified review.
- Complete the course performance task and remediation requirements.

## Required lessons

1. Ball-handler freedom of movement
2. Legal guarding position under pressure
3. Block-charge and no-charge semicircle
4. Airborne shooter and landing
5. Screening and rescreening
6. Post play and off-ball holding
7. Rebounding and displacement
8. Marginal contact and patient whistle
9. Graduate Source Seminar and Conflict Resolution: Advanced Contact Judgment and Correct No-Call Philosophy
10. Advanced Legal-Status Reconstruction: Advanced Contact Judgment and Correct No-Call Philosophy
11. Complex Legal, Illegal, Borderline, and Withhold-Action Laboratory: Advanced Contact Judgment and Correct No-Call Philosophy
12. Advanced Mechanics and Coverage Defense: Advanced Contact Judgment and Correct No-Call Philosophy
13. Penalty, Replay, Timing, and Resumption Sequencing: Advanced Contact Judgment and Correct No-Call Philosophy
14. Normal-Speed Film Defense and Performance Analytics: Advanced Contact Judgment and Correct No-Call Philosophy
15. Graduate Memorandum, Practical Examination, and Oral Defense: Advanced Contact Judgment and Correct No-Call Philosophy

## Source-control rule

The actual competition, event bulletin, rules code, source version, and effective date must be identified before any ruling or policy conclusion. FIBA 2026 rules are future effective until October 1, 2026. Official publications are reference authorities and are not reproduced word-for-word.
