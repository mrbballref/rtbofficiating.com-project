# RZU-USAM-M 512 — Transition, Press, End-of-Game, Clock, and Shot-Clock Administration

**Program:** USA Basketball Men’s Basketball Officiating Master’s-Level Advanced Officiating Program  
**Year/Term:** Year 1, Term 2  
**Units:** 3 RefZone credit-equivalent units  
**Engagement hours:** 45  
**Credential status:** Professional degree-level pathway; not accredited or transferable academic credit  
**Publication status:** Complete instructional draft pending qualified review

## Course description

A 3-unit graduate-style course focused on transition, press, end-of-game, clock, and shot-clock administration. It combines original RefZone instruction, official-source study, event-specific rules-code selection, film or evidence analysis, communication, assessment, and applied performance.

## Learning outcomes

- Select and cite the controlling USA Basketball, FIBA, or expressly adopted domestic authority.
- Apply the course concepts using complete legal-status or evidence sequences.
- Create and solve original legal, illegal, borderline, atypical, and disciplined no-action scenarios.
- Use accurate mechanics, administration, communication, research, or policy methods.
- Defend conclusions at normal speed or against complete evidence and respond to qualified review.
- Complete the course performance task and remediation requirements.

## Required lessons

1. Fast-break primary coverage
2. Press, trap, and backcourt coverage
3. Eight-second and 24-second awareness
4. Late-game throw-ins and tactical fouls
5. Clock and shot-clock corrections
6. Last-shot responsibility
7. Timeout and substitution opportunities
8. End-of-game crew communication
9. Graduate Source Seminar and Conflict Resolution: Transition, Press, End-of-Game, Clock, and Shot-Clock Administration
10. Advanced Legal-Status Reconstruction: Transition, Press, End-of-Game, Clock, and Shot-Clock Administration
11. Complex Legal, Illegal, Borderline, and Withhold-Action Laboratory: Transition, Press, End-of-Game, Clock, and Shot-Clock Administration
12. Advanced Mechanics and Coverage Defense: Transition, Press, End-of-Game, Clock, and Shot-Clock Administration
13. Penalty, Replay, Timing, and Resumption Sequencing: Transition, Press, End-of-Game, Clock, and Shot-Clock Administration
14. Normal-Speed Film Defense and Performance Analytics: Transition, Press, End-of-Game, Clock, and Shot-Clock Administration
15. Graduate Memorandum, Practical Examination, and Oral Defense: Transition, Press, End-of-Game, Clock, and Shot-Clock Administration

## Source-control rule

The actual competition, event bulletin, rules code, source version, and effective date must be identified before any ruling or policy conclusion. FIBA 2026 rules are future effective until October 1, 2026. Official publications are reference authorities and are not reproduced word-for-word.
