# RZU-D1W-B 171 — Applied NCAA Division I Women’s Officiating Practicum I

**Program:** NCAA Division I Women’s Basketball Officiating Bachelor’s-Level Professional Foundations Program  
**Academic period:** Year 4 · Term 8  
**Term theme:** Senior Seminar, Capstone, Oral Defense, and Professional Portfolio  
**Learning volume:** 5 RefZone credit-equivalent units · 75 engagement hours  
**Prerequisite:** Successful completion of RZU-D1W-B 163 and all applicable competency gates  
**Credential status:** Professional degree-level pathway; not an accredited degree or transferable college credit  
**Rules basis:** 2025-26 NCAA Women’s Rules Book and Casebook, current approved rulings, current-cycle rules changes and points of emphasis, and verified NCAA Division I procedures  

A five-unit academic course in the NCAA Division I Women Bachelor’s-Level Professional Foundations pathway focused on applied NCAA Division I Women’s officiating practicum i. The course combines original RefZone instruction, official-source study, approved-ruling analysis, film review, communication practice, and applied performance.

## Learning outcomes

- Explain and apply applied NCAA Division I Women’s officiating practicum i using current NCAA women’s authority and verified NCAA Division I procedures.
- Identify legal status, primary coverage, required visual evidence, exceptions, and administration before ruling.
- Create and solve original legal, illegal, borderline, atypical, and correct no-call scenarios.
- Use correct mechanics, signals, reporting, communication, clock, shot-clock, replay, possession, penalty, and resumption procedures.
- Analyze performance at normal speed, use slow motion only for facts, and defend conclusions with source and film evidence.
- Complete the performance task and remediation requirements at the approved competency standard.

## Lessons

### 1. Practicum Planning, Assignment Approval, and Safety Controls

This lesson develops practicum planning, assignment approval, and safety controls within Applied NCAA Division I Women’s Officiating Practicum I. Students establish the controlling status, observe the complete action, apply current NCAA women’s rules and verified NCAA Division I procedures, administer the full result, communicate professionally, and convert the play into a repeatable next-game process.

**Required evidence**

- Read the assigned current rule and related approved rulings; record definitions, conditions, exceptions, penalty, and effective date without copying protected text.
- Write a one-page decision tree for the lesson.
- Create one original legal, illegal, borderline, and correct no-call or no-correction example.

## Grading

- Lesson quizzes and source-study checks: 15%

- Video, mechanics, replay, or decision laboratory: 20%
- Midterm integrated examination: 15%
- Communication or practical performance: 15%
- Final course project and oral defense: 15%

## Required submissions

- RZU-D1W-B 171 source and terminology notebook
- Twelve original case-play analyses
- Legal/illegal/borderline/no-call comparison assignment
- Normal-speed film-analysis package
- Mechanics, signal, reporting, or communication demonstration
- Midterm integrated examination
- Applied NCAA Division I Women’s Officiating Practicum I final project and oral defense

## Completion requirements

- Earn at least 80 percent for the academic course.
- Pass mandatory rules, penalty, timing, shot-clock, replay, safety, ethics, and source-control competencies assigned to the course.
- Complete all required case, film, communication, and practical submissions.
- Correct any superseded-source, rules-code, or administration error identified during review.
- Pass the final project and oral defense or complete assigned remediation and re-demonstration.
