# RZU-USAM-B 121 — Jump Ball, Alternating Possession, and Possession Administration

**Program:** USA Basketball Men’s Basketball Officiating Bachelor’s-Level Professional Foundations Program  
**Year/Term:** Year 2, Term 3  
**Units:** 5 RefZone credit-equivalent units  
**Engagement hours:** 75  
**Credential status:** Professional degree-level pathway; not accredited or transferable academic credit  
**Publication status:** Complete instructional draft pending qualified review

## Course description

A 5-unit undergraduate-style course focused on jump ball, alternating possession, and possession administration. It combines original RefZone instruction, official-source study, event-specific rules-code selection, film or evidence analysis, communication, assessment, and applied performance.

## Learning outcomes

- Select and cite the controlling USA Basketball, FIBA, or expressly adopted domestic authority.
- Apply the course concepts using complete legal-status or evidence sequences.
- Create and solve original legal, illegal, borderline, atypical, and disciplined no-action scenarios.
- Use accurate mechanics, administration, communication, research, or policy methods.
- Defend conclusions at normal speed or against complete evidence and respond to qualified review.
- Complete the course performance task and remediation requirements.

## Required lessons

1. Opening jump-ball procedure
2. Jump-ball restrictions
3. Alternating-possession entitlement
4. Held-ball situations
5. Lodged ball and special possession cases
6. Possession-arrow and table communication
7. Source Map, Definitions, and Competency Targets: Jump Ball, Alternating Possession, and Possession Administration
8. Legal Status and Required Conditions: Jump Ball, Alternating Possession, and Possession Administration
9. Legal, Illegal, Borderline, and No-Call Laboratory: Jump Ball, Alternating Possession, and Possession Administration
10. Mechanics, Primary Coverage, and Visual Evidence: Jump Ball, Alternating Possession, and Possession Administration
11. Penalty, Clock, Shot-Clock, Possession, and Resumption: Jump Ball, Alternating Possession, and Possession Administration
12. Communication, Reporting, Film Review, and Course Assessment: Jump Ball, Alternating Possession, and Possession Administration

## Source-control rule

The actual competition, event bulletin, rules code, source version, and effective date must be identified before any ruling or policy conclusion. FIBA 2026 rules are future effective until October 1, 2026. Official publications are reference authorities and are not reproduced word-for-word.
