# RZU-NAIW-B 151 — Contact Judgment II: Shooters, Airborne Players, Verticality, and Landing

**Program:** NAIA Women’s Basketball Officiating Bachelor’s-Level Professional Foundations Program  
**Academic period:** Year 3 · Term 6  
**Term theme:** Leadership, Communication, Film, and Performance Analysis  
**Learning volume:** 5 RefZone credit-equivalent units · 75 engagement hours  
**Prerequisite:** Successful completion of RZU-NAIW-B 143 and all applicable competency gates  
**Credential status:** Professional degree-level pathway; not an accredited degree or transferable college credit  
**Rules basis:** 2025-26 NCAA Women’s Rules Book and Casebook, current approved rulings, current-cycle rules changes and points of emphasis, and verified NAIA procedure or modifications and modifications  

A five-unit academic course in the NAIA Women Bachelor’s-Level Professional Foundations pathway focused on contact judgment ii: shooters, airborne players, verticality, and landing. The course combines original RefZone instruction, official-source study, approved-ruling analysis, film review, communication practice, and applied performance.

## Learning outcomes

- Explain and apply contact judgment ii: shooters, airborne players, verticality, and landing using current NCAA women’s authority and verified NAIA procedure or modifications and modifications.
- Identify legal status, primary coverage, required visual evidence, exceptions, and administration before ruling.
- Create and solve original legal, illegal, borderline, atypical, and correct no-call scenarios.
- Use correct mechanics, signals, reporting, communication, clock, shot-clock, replay, possession, penalty, and resumption procedures.
- Analyze performance at normal speed, use slow motion only for facts, and defend conclusions with source and film evidence.
- Complete the performance task and remediation requirements at the approved competency standard.
