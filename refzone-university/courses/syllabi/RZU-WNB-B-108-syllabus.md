# RZU-WNB-B 108 — Free Throws, Lane Administration, Team-Foul Penalties, and Penalty Accuracy

**Program:** WNBA Basketball Officiating Bachelor’s-Level Professional Foundations Program
**Units:** 5 · **Hours:** 75
**Prerequisite:** RZU-WNB-B 107
**Status:** Complete instructional production draft. Qualified WNBA rules, mechanics, replay, challenge, game operations, referee development, curriculum, copyright, accessibility, video, assessment, research, legal, safeguarding, and administrator review remain required before public publication.

A 5-unit bachelor’s-level professional foundations pathway course focused on free throws, lane administration, team-foul penalties, and penalty accuracy, combining original RefZone instruction, official WNBA source study, normal-speed film or evidence analysis, communication, assessment, and applied professional performance.

## Learning outcomes
- Select and cite the controlling WNBA-specific authority, internal regulations, officiating manual, official interpretation or technical clarification, assignment directive, and underlying WNBA provision.
- Apply the course concepts using complete legal-status or evidence sequences.
- Create and solve original legal, illegal, borderline, atypical, and disciplined no-action scenarios.
- Use accurate mechanics, administration, communication, research, or policy methods.
- Defend conclusions at normal speed or against complete evidence and respond to qualified review.
- Complete the course performance task and remediation requirements.

## Lessons
1. Rule 9 lane-space alignment, legal positions, and restrictions
2. Free-throw shooter restrictions, release, rim contact, and violations
3. Defensive, offensive, simultaneous, and delayed lane violations
4. Correct shooter, substitution restrictions, technical and flagrant free throws
5. Team-foul penalty situations, number of attempts, possession, and next play
6. Wrong shooter, wrong number, correctable error, clock, table, and crew communication
7. Source Map, Definitions, and Competency Targets: Free Throws, Lane Administration, Team-Foul Penalties, and Penalty Accuracy
8. Legal Status and Required Conditions: Free Throws, Lane Administration, Team-Foul Penalties, and Penalty Accuracy
9. Legal, Illegal, Borderline, and Correct No-Call Laboratory: Free Throws, Lane Administration, Team-Foul Penalties, and Penalty Accuracy
10. Mechanics, Primary Coverage, and Visual Evidence: Free Throws, Lane Administration, Team-Foul Penalties, and Penalty Accuracy
11. Penalty, Clock, Shot Clock, Possession, Replay, and Resumption: Free Throws, Lane Administration, Team-Foul Penalties, and Penalty Accuracy
12. Communication, Reporting, Film Review, and Course Assessment: Free Throws, Lane Administration, Team-Foul Penalties, and Penalty Accuracy
