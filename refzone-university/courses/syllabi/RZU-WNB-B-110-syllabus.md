A 5-unit bachelor’s-level professional foundations pathway course focused on violations ii: three seconds, eight seconds, backcourt, elbows, player out of bounds, and back-to-basket, combining original RefZone instruction, official WNBA source study, normal-speed film or evidence analysis, communication, assessment, and applied professional performance.

## Learning outcomes
- Select and cite the controlling WNBA-specific authority, internal regulations, officiating manual, official interpretation or technical clarification, assignment directive, and underlying WNBA provision.
- Apply the course concepts using complete legal-status or evidence sequences.
- Create and solve original legal, illegal, borderline, atypical, and disciplined no-action scenarios.

## Lessons
1. Offensive three seconds, count suspension, move to score, and enforcement
2. Defensive three seconds, actively guarding, team warnings, penalty, and possession
3. Eight seconds, ball location, team control, and backcourt exceptions
4. Swinging elbows, entering basket from below, and illegal assist in scoring
5. Offensive screen out of bounds and offensive player illegally out of bounds
6. Five-second back-to-the-basket status, count, termination, violation, and signal
7. Source Map, Definitions, and Competency Targets: Violations II: Three Seconds, Eight Seconds, Backcourt, Elbows, Player Out of Bounds, and Back-to-Basket
8. Legal Status and Required Conditions: Violations II: Three Seconds, Eight Seconds, Backcourt, Elbows, Player Out of Bounds, and Back-to-Basket
9. Legal, Illegal, Borderline, and Correct No-Call Laboratory: Violations II: Three Seconds, Eight Seconds, Backcourt, Elbows, Player Out of Bounds, and Back-to-Basket
10. Mechanics, Primary Coverage, and Visual Evidence: Violations II: Three Seconds, Eight Seconds, Backcourt, Elbows, Player Out of Bounds, and Back-to-Basket
11. Penalty, Clock, Shot Clock, Possession, Replay, and Resumption: Violations II: Three Seconds, Eight Seconds, Backcourt, Elbows, Player Out of Bounds, and Back-to-Basket
12. Communication, Reporting, Film Review, and Course Assessment: Violations II: Three Seconds, Eight Seconds, Backcourt, Elbows, Player Out of Bounds, and Back-to-Basket
