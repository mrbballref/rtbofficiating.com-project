## Lessons
1. Four 12-minute periods and game-clock fundamentals
2. Seven regulation timeouts and timeout administration
3. Reset Timeout procedure and immediate resumption
4. Untimed Target Score overtime set seven points above the tie
5. Scoring, successful free throws, and score corrections
6. End-of-period awareness, heaves, horn, and last-shot responsibility
7. Source Map, Definitions, and Competency Targets: Scoring, Four-Quarter Timing, Timeouts, Reset Timeouts, and Target Score Overtime
8. Legal Status and Required Conditions: Scoring, Four-Quarter Timing, Timeouts, Reset Timeouts, and Target Score Overtime
9. Legal, Illegal, Borderline, and Correct No-Call Laboratory: Scoring, Four-Quarter Timing, Timeouts, Reset Timeouts, and Target Score Overtime
10. Mechanics, Primary Coverage, and Visual Evidence: Scoring, Four-Quarter Timing, Timeouts, Reset Timeouts, and Target Score Overtime
11. Penalty, Clock, Shot-Clock, Possession, and Resumption: Scoring, Four-Quarter Timing, Timeouts, Reset Timeouts, and Target Score Overtime
12. Communication, Reporting, Film Review, and Course Assessment: Scoring, Four-Quarter Timing, Timeouts, Reset Timeouts, and Target Score Overtime
