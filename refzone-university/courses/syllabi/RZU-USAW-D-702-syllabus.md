## Course description

A 3-unit doctoral-style course focused on governance, ethics, integrity, safe sport, and sports law. It combines original RefZone instruction, official-source study, event-specific rules-code selection, film or evidence analysis, communication, assessment, and applied performance.

- Select and cite the controlling USA Basketball, FIBA, or expressly adopted domestic authority.
