# RZU-WNB-B 103 — Court, Equipment, Game Officials, Table Crew, Replay Support, and Arena Operations

**Program:** WNBA Basketball Officiating Bachelor’s-Level Professional Foundations Program
**Units:** 5 · **Hours:** 75
**Prerequisite:** RZU-WNB-B 102
**Status:** Complete instructional production draft. Qualified WNBA rules, mechanics, replay, challenge, game operations, referee development, curriculum, copyright, accessibility, video, assessment, research, legal, safeguarding, and administrator review remain required before public publication.

A 5-unit bachelor’s-level professional foundations pathway course focused on court, equipment, game officials, table crew, replay support, and arena operations, combining original RefZone instruction, official WNBA source study, normal-speed film or evidence analysis, communication, assessment, and applied professional performance.

## Learning outcomes
- Select and cite the controlling WNBA-specific authority, internal regulations, officiating manual, official interpretation or technical clarification, assignment directive, and underlying WNBA provision.
- Apply the course concepts using complete legal-status or evidence sequences.
- Create and solve original legal, illegal, borderline, atypical, and disciplined no-action scenarios.
- Use accurate mechanics, administration, communication, research, or policy methods.
- Defend conclusions at normal speed or against complete evidence and respond to qualified review.
- Complete the course performance task and remediation requirements.

## Lessons
1. Rule 1 court dimensions, boundary lines, lane, three-point area, and restricted area
2. Rule 1 WNBA ball, baskets, backboards, nets, lights, clocks, and required equipment
3. Rule 2 crew chief, two referees, scorer, game-clock timer, and shot-clock timer
4. Rule 2 pregame inspection, equipment approval, player equipment, and foreign substances
5. Scorer, timer, shot-clock, replay-support, substitution-box, and communication duties
6. Arena entry, locker room, security, medical, broadcast, emergency, and game-operations checks
7. Source Map, Definitions, and Competency Targets: Court, Equipment, Game Officials, Table Crew, Replay Support, and Arena Operations
8. Legal Status and Required Conditions: Court, Equipment, Game Officials, Table Crew, Replay Support, and Arena Operations
9. Legal, Illegal, Borderline, and Correct No-Call Laboratory: Court, Equipment, Game Officials, Table Crew, Replay Support, and Arena Operations
10. Mechanics, Primary Coverage, and Visual Evidence: Court, Equipment, Game Officials, Table Crew, Replay Support, and Arena Operations
11. Penalty, Clock, Shot Clock, Possession, Replay, and Resumption: Court, Equipment, Game Officials, Table Crew, Replay Support, and Arena Operations
12. Communication, Reporting, Film Review, and Course Assessment: Court, Equipment, Game Officials, Table Crew, Replay Support, and Arena Operations
