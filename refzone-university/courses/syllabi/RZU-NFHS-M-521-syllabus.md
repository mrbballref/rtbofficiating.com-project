# RZU-NFHS-M 521 — Transition, Press, End-of-Period, Clock, and Shot-Clock Administration

**Program:** NFHS Basketball Officiating Master’s-Level Advanced Officiating Program  
**Academic period:** Year 1 · Term 2  
**Term theme:** Elite Mechanics, Timing, Replay Policy, and Atypical Administration  
**Learning volume:** 3 RefZone graduate credit-equivalent units · 45 engagement hours  
**Prerequisite:** Successful completion of RZU-NFHS-M 512 and all applicable graduate competency gates  
**Credential status:** Professional graduate degree-level pathway; not represented as an accredited degree or transferable graduate credit  
**Publication status:** Complete graduate instructional draft pending qualified rules, mechanics, curriculum, copyright, accessibility, assessment, video, and program review  

A three-unit graduate course in the NFHS Master’s-Level Advanced Officiating pathway focused on transition, press, end-of-period, clock, and shot-clock administration. The course combines current-source seminars, original complex cases, advanced film and mechanics analysis, professional communication, applied performance, and oral defense.

## Graduate learning outcomes

- Analyze and defend the advanced competency domain of transition, press, end-of-period, clock, and shot-clock administration using current NFHS authority.
- Resolve complex fact patterns through legal status, chronology, definitions, exceptions, mechanics ownership, and complete administration.
- Distinguish legal, illegal, borderline, and correct no-call or no-correction outcomes under normal-speed conditions.
- Produce graduate-level written analysis, film evidence, professional communication, and oral defense.
- Demonstrate advanced leadership, evaluation, or applied officiating behavior appropriate to the course domain.
- Identify source limitations, local-policy requirements, copyright restrictions, and human-review gates.

## Fifteen-week graduate sequence

- **Week 1:** Transition Phases and Crew Conversion
- **Week 2:** Backcourt Press Coverage and Visible Counts
- **Week 3:** Traps, Run-and-Jump Actions, and Multiple Matchups
- **Week 4:** Long Passes, Deep Receivers, and Boundary Coverage
- **Week 5:** Fast Breaks, Secondary Defenders, and Late Arrivals
- **Week 6:** Last-Shot Responsibility and End-of-Period Crew Planning
- **Week 7:** Try, Tap, Release, Horn, and Period-End Status · midterm rules memorandum workshop
- **Week 8:** The Current Minimum 0.3-Second Timing Standard · written and oral midterm examination
- **Week 9:** Clock Starts, Stops, Delays, and Malfunctions
- **Week 10:** Definite Information and Official Count Reconciliation
- **Week 11:** Timing Corrections and Table Communication
- **Week 12:** State-Authorized Shot Clock: Jurisdiction Verification
- **Week 13:** Shot-Clock Starts, Stops, Resets, and Corrections Where Adopted
- **Week 14:** Late-Game Timeout, Substitution, and Resumption Administration · final project presentation
- **Week 15:** End-of-Game Administration Simulation and Oral Defense · oral defense and remediation conference

### 1. Transition Phases and Crew Conversion

- Write a 500–750 word graduate memorandum on transition phases and crew conversion with a source map and a clearly labeled RefZone instructional analysis section.
