# RZU-NJCM-B 121 — Free Throws, Bonus Administration, Violations, and Correctable Errors

**Program:** NJCAA Men’s Basketball Officiating Bachelor’s-Level Professional Foundations Program  
**Academic period:** Year 2 · Term 3  
**Term theme:** Violations, Free Throws, Fouls, and Penalties  
**Learning volume:** 5 RefZone credit-equivalent units · 75 engagement hours  
**Prerequisite:** Successful completion of RZU-NJCM-B 113 and all applicable competency gates  
**Credential status:** Professional degree-level pathway; not an accredited degree or transferable college credit  
**Rules basis:** 2025-26 NCAA Men’s Rules Book and Casebook, current corrections/interpretations, 2026-27 approved rulings and points of emphasis, and verified NJCAA procedures  

A five-unit academic course in the NJCAA Men Bachelor’s-Level Professional Foundations pathway focused on free throws, bonus administration, violations, and correctable errors. The course combines original RefZone instruction, official-source study, approved-ruling analysis, film review, communication practice, and applied performance.

## Learning outcomes

- Explain and apply free throws, bonus administration, violations, and correctable errors using current NCAA men’s authority and verified NJCAA procedures.
- Identify legal status, primary coverage, required visual evidence, exceptions, and administration before ruling.
- Create and solve original legal, illegal, borderline, atypical, and correct no-call scenarios.
- Use correct mechanics, signals, reporting, communication, clock, shot-clock, replay, possession, penalty, and resumption procedures.
- Analyze performance at normal speed, use slow motion only for facts, and defend conclusions with source and film evidence.
- Complete the performance task and remediation requirements at the approved competency standard.

- **Week 1:** Free-Throw Awards and Identifying the Correct Shooter

- **Week 4:** Lane-Space Alignment and Occupancy Order

- **Week 6:** Lane Players, Players Outside the Arc, and Early Entry
- **Week 7:** Lane Players, Players Outside the Arc, and Early Entry
- **Week 8:** Disconcertion, Delayed Violations, and Multiple Violations
- **Week 9:** Technical, Flagrant, and Contact Dead-Ball Free Throws
- **Week 10:** Substitution Restrictions During Free-Throw Sequences
- **Week 11:** Wrong Shooter, Wrong Number of Free Throws, and Correctable Errors
- **Week 12:** Wrong Shooter, Wrong Number of Free Throws, and Correctable Errors
- **Week 13:** Wrong Basket, Score Corrections, and Point-of-Interruption Questions
- **Week 14:** Rebounding Coverage and Transition After the Final Free Throw
- **Week 15:** Integrated Rule 8 Free-Throw and Correctable-Error Examination
