# RZU-NJCW-M 501 — Advanced NCAA and NJCAA Women’s Rules Interpretation Seminar

**Program:** NJCAA Women’s Basketball Officiating Master’s-Level Advanced Officiating Program  
**Academic period:** Year 1 · Term 1  
**Term theme:** Advanced Rules Interpretation, Complex Cases, and Judgment  
**Learning volume:** 3 RefZone graduate credit-equivalent units · 45 engagement hours  
**Prerequisite:** Completed NJCAA Women’s Bachelor’s-Level Professional Foundations pathway or verified equivalent competency  
**Credential status:** Professional graduate degree-level pathway; not an accredited degree or transferable graduate credit  
**Rules basis:** Current NCAA women’s playing rules, approved rulings, interpretations, replay and challenge guidance, authorized mechanics, 2026-27 approved rulings and points of emphasis, and verified NJCAA procedures  

A graduate-level NJCAA women’s basketball officiating course in advanced ncaa and njcaa women’s rules interpretation seminar, integrating current NCAA women’s playing rules, approved rulings, NJCAA procedures, advanced mechanics, normal-speed film analysis, written rules work, practical performance, and oral defense.

## Graduate learning outcomes

- Analyze advanced ncaa and njcaa women’s rules interpretation seminar with current NCAA women’s and NJCAA authority.
- Resolve advanced legal-status, mechanics, administration, and communication problems without cross-ruleset contamination.
- Defend conclusions through original written analysis, normal-speed film, case-play work, and oral examination.
- Translate graduate study into measurable crew, evaluator, leadership, or game-performance behavior.

## Fifteen-week sequence

- **Week 1:** Graduate Orientation: Authority, Evidence, and Professional Accountability
- **Week 2:** NCAA Rulebook, Casebook, Interpretations, and NJCAA Procedure Hierarchy
- **Week 3:** Definitions as Operative Elements of a Ruling
- **Week 4:** Reading Conditions, Exceptions, Notes, Penalties, and Approved Rulings Together
- **Week 5:** Reconciling Related Provisions Across Rules 1 Through 11
- **Week 6:** Effective Dates, Corrections, Future Rulings, and Superseded Authority
- **Week 7:** NJCAA Division, Region, Conference, Tournament, and Assignor Modifications
- **Week 8:** NCAA Women’s and Other Rulesets: Preventing Cross-Code Contamination
- **Week 9:** Act of Shooting, Try Status, and Current Rules-Cycle Interpretation
- **Week 10:** Team Control, Player Control, Loose Ball, and Possession Status
- **Week 11:** Restricted-Area, Secondary-Defender, and Legal-Guarding Interpretation
- **Week 12:** Writing an Advanced Rules Memorandum in Original Language
- **Week 13:** Source-Conflict Laboratory and Human-Review Gate
- **Week 14:** 2025-26 and 2026-27 Rules-Cycle Changes, Interpretations, and Current Emphasis
- **Week 15:** Advanced Interpretation Comprehensive Examination and Oral Defense

## Required graduate submissions

- Current-source graduate memorandum
- Original legal, illegal, borderline, and correct no-call or no-correction case set
- Normal-speed film analysis with angle limitations
- Mechanics, replay, communication, evaluation, or leadership artifact appropriate to the course
- Applied performance goal and verification evidence
- Final oral defense and remediation record

## Grading

- Graduate source memoranda and case analysis: 20%
- Film, mechanics, replay, or communication laboratories: 20%
- Applied performance and professional artifacts: 25%
- Midterm and comprehensive examination: 20%
- Final portfolio and oral defense: 15%

## Completion standard

Minimum 85 percent overall, completion of every mandatory competency gate, and successful oral defense or re-demonstration.
