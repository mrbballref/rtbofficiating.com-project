## Course Description

A three-unit doctoral course in the NJCAA Men’s PhD-Level Research, Leadership, and Elite Officiating pathway focused on governance, ethics, integrity, and sports law. The course integrates current NCAA men’s playing-rules authority and NJCAA administrative authority, doctoral scholarship, original research or policy work, ethics, equity, professional application, peer review, and oral defense.

## Learning Outcomes

- Critically synthesize the doctoral domain of governance, ethics, integrity, and sports law using current NCAA men’s playing-rules authority and NJCAA administrative authority and validated research evidence.
- Design and defend an appropriate research, policy, analytic, leadership, curriculum, technology, or evaluation method.
- Evaluate alternative explanations, uncertainty, ethics, equity, privacy, copyright, governance, and implementation risk.
- Produce original doctoral writing, evidence visualizations, peer review, revision, and oral defense.
- Translate findings into a responsible professional or institutional contribution.
- Maintain complete source versioning, research records, human-review gates, and academic integrity.

5. Conflicts of Interest and Disclosure Duties

7. Confidentiality, Privacy, and Records Stewardship
8. Due Process in Discipline, Evaluation, and Assignment Decisions
9. Negligence, Duty of Care, and Reasonable Professional Conduct
10. Safety Governance and Emergency Action Responsibilities
11. Discrimination, Harassment, Retaliation, and Protected Reporting
12. Social Media, Public Speech, and Institutional Reputation
13. Contract, Compensation, Classification, and Policy Awareness
14. Ethics Case Conference and Governance Memorandum
15. Integrity Audit and Oral Policy Defense

## Source and Copyright Controls

- Official NCAA men’s and NJCAA publications and current official updates are reference authorities; student-facing instruction is original RefZone University language.
- No protected rulebook text, casebook language, mechanics diagrams, game footage, logos, or identifiable participant data may be reproduced without authorization.
- Research literature, legal and policy sources, technology claims, and institutional procedures require source-by-source validation.
- Any uncertainty, source conflict, inaccessible authority, ethics concern, or jurisdiction-specific requirement remains blocked for human review.
