# RZU-NBA-B 120 — Professional Communication, Respect for the Game, Player and Coach Management, and Crew Unity

**Program:** NBA Basketball Officiating Bachelor’s-Level Professional Foundations Program
**Units:** 5 · **Hours:** 75
**Prerequisite:** RZU-NBA-B 119
**Status:** Complete instructional production draft. Qualified NBA rules, mechanics, replay, challenge, game operations, referee development, curriculum, copyright, accessibility, video, assessment, research, legal, safeguarding, and administrator review remain required before public publication.

A 5-unit bachelor’s-level professional foundations pathway course focused on professional communication, respect for the game, player and coach management, and crew unity, combining original RefZone instruction, official NBA source study, normal-speed film or evidence analysis, communication, assessment, and applied professional performance.

## Learning outcomes
- Select and cite the controlling NBA-specific authority, internal regulations, officiating manual, official interpretation or technical clarification, assignment directive, and underlying NBA provision.
- Apply the course concepts using complete legal-status or evidence sequences.
- Create and solve original legal, illegal, borderline, atypical, and disciplined no-action scenarios.
- Use accurate mechanics, administration, communication, research, or policy methods.
- Defend conclusions at normal speed or against complete evidence and respond to qualified review.
- Complete the course performance task and remediation requirements.

## Lessons
1. Respect for the Game principles and consistent professional presence
2. Preventive communication with players and captains
3. Brief, calm, rules-based communication with head coaches
4. Assistant-coach and bench conduct boundaries
5. Crew eye contact, factual help, correction, support, and private accountability
6. Table, replay, security, medical, arena, supervisor, and postgame communication
7. Source Map, Definitions, and Competency Targets: Professional Communication, Respect for the Game, Player and Coach Management, and Crew Unity
8. Legal Status and Required Conditions: Professional Communication, Respect for the Game, Player and Coach Management, and Crew Unity
9. Legal, Illegal, Borderline, and Correct No-Call Laboratory: Professional Communication, Respect for the Game, Player and Coach Management, and Crew Unity
10. Mechanics, Primary Coverage, and Visual Evidence: Professional Communication, Respect for the Game, Player and Coach Management, and Crew Unity
11. Penalty, Clock, Shot Clock, Possession, Replay, and Resumption: Professional Communication, Respect for the Game, Player and Coach Management, and Crew Unity
12. Communication, Reporting, Film Review, and Course Assessment: Professional Communication, Respect for the Game, Player and Coach Management, and Crew Unity
