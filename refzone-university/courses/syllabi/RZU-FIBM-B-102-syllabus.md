**Program:** FIBA Men’s Basketball Officiating Bachelor’s-Level Professional Foundations Program

**Units:** 5 RefZone credit-equivalent units
**Engagement hours:** 75
**Prerequisite:** Successful completion of prior-term requirements and Professional Identity, Ethics, Integrity, and the FIBA Men’s Officiating Environment
**Credential status:** Professional degree-level pathway; not represented as an accredited academic degree, FIBA licence, assignment credential, or transferable college credit

## Course description
A 5-unit undergraduate-style course focused on source authority, fiba foundation, FIBA adaptations, and effective-date control. It combines original RefZone instruction, official-source study, event-specific rules-code selection, film or evidence analysis, communication, assessment, and applied performance.

## Learning outcomes
- Select and cite the controlling FIBA competition-specific authority, internal regulations, officiating manual, official interpretation or technical clarification, assignment directive, and underlying FIBA provision.
- Apply the course concepts using complete legal-status or evidence sequences.
- Create and solve original legal, illegal, borderline, atypical, and disciplined no-action scenarios.
- Use accurate mechanics, administration, communication, research, or policy methods.
- Defend conclusions at normal speed or against complete evidence and respond to qualified review.
- Complete the course performance task and remediation requirements.
