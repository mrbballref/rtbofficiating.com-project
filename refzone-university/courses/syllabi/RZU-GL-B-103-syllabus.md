# RZU-GL-B 103 — Court, Equipment, Crew Designations, Table Crew, Replay Support, and Game Operations

**Program:** NBA G League Basketball Officiating Bachelor’s-Level Professional Foundations Program
**Units:** 5 · **Hours:** 75
**Prerequisite:** RZU-GL-B 102
**Status:** Complete instructional production draft. Qualified NBA/G League rules, experimental rules, mechanics, replay, game operations, referee development, curriculum, copyright, accessibility, video, assessment, research, legal, and administrator review remain required before public publication.

A 5-unit bachelor’s-level professional foundations pathway course focused on court, equipment, crew designations, table crew, replay support, and game operations, combining original RefZone instruction, official-source study, film or evidence analysis, communication, assessment, and applied professional performance.

## Learning outcomes
- Select and cite the controlling NBA G League-specific authority, internal regulations, officiating manual, official interpretation or technical clarification, assignment directive, and underlying NBA G League provision.
- Apply the course concepts using complete legal-status or evidence sequences.
- Create and solve original legal, illegal, borderline, atypical, and disciplined no-action scenarios.
- Use accurate mechanics, administration, communication, research, or policy methods.
- Defend conclusions at normal speed or against complete evidence and respond to qualified review.
- Complete the course performance task and remediation requirements.
