## Course description

A 3-unit graduate-style course focused on advanced shot clock, end-of-game, tv timing, and table administration. It combines original RefZone instruction, official-source study, event-specific rules-code selection, film or evidence analysis, communication, assessment, and applied performance.

## Learning outcomes

- Select and cite the controlling FIBA Official Basketball Rules, interpretations, FIBA Europe club regulations, and EuroLeague Women regulations, bylaws, authorized FIBA mechanics guidance, technical and assignment clarification, assignment directive, and underlying FIBA provision.
- Apply the course concepts using complete legal-status or evidence sequences.
- Create and solve original legal, illegal, borderline, atypical, and disciplined no-action scenarios.
- Use accurate mechanics, administration, communication, research, or policy methods.
- Defend conclusions at normal speed or against complete evidence and respond to qualified review.
- Complete the course performance task and remediation requirements.
