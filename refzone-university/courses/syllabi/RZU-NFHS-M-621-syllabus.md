# RZU-NFHS-M 621 — Advanced Applied Officiating Practicum

**Program:** NFHS Basketball Officiating Master’s-Level Advanced Officiating Program  
**Academic period:** Year 2 · Term 4  
**Term theme:** Film Analytics, Graduate Practicum, Comprehensive Examination, and Capstone  
**Learning volume:** 3 RefZone graduate credit-equivalent units · 45 engagement hours  
**Prerequisite:** Successful completion of all prior NFHS Master’s courses; approved practicum placement or simulation plan  
**Credential status:** Professional graduate degree-level pathway; not represented as an accredited degree or transferable graduate credit  
**Publication status:** Complete graduate instructional draft pending qualified rules, mechanics, curriculum, copyright, accessibility, assessment, video, and program review  

A three-unit graduate course in the NFHS Master’s-Level Advanced Officiating pathway focused on advanced applied officiating practicum. The course combines current-source seminars, original complex cases, advanced film and mechanics analysis, professional communication, applied performance, and oral defense.

## Graduate learning outcomes

- Analyze and defend the advanced competency domain of advanced applied officiating practicum using current NFHS authority.
- Resolve complex fact patterns through legal status, chronology, definitions, exceptions, mechanics ownership, and complete administration.
- Distinguish legal, illegal, borderline, and correct no-call or no-correction outcomes under normal-speed conditions.
- Produce graduate-level written analysis, film evidence, professional communication, and oral defense.
- Demonstrate advanced leadership, evaluation, or applied officiating behavior appropriate to the course domain.
- Identify source limitations, local-policy requirements, copyright restrictions, and human-review gates.

## Fifteen-week graduate sequence

- **Week 1:** Practicum Authorization, Scope, Safety, and Confidentiality
- **Week 2:** Individual Performance Plan and Baseline Evaluation
- **Week 3:** Assignment Preparation and Partner Communication
- **Week 4:** Pregame Leadership and Site Administration
- **Week 5:** First-Half Mechanics and Judgment Observation
- **Week 6:** Halftime Self-Correction and Crew Adjustment
- **Week 7:** Second-Half Consistency and End-Game Management · midterm rules memorandum workshop
- **Week 8:** Postgame Crew Review and Supervisor Contact · written and oral midterm examination
- **Week 9:** Complete-Game Film Self-Evaluation
- **Week 10:** Rules and Penalty Error Audit
- **Week 11:** Mechanics and Coverage Error Audit
- **Week 12:** Communication and Game-Management Audit
- **Week 13:** Incident-Report and Administrative Writing Exercise
- **Week 14:** Remediation, Re-Demonstration, and Evaluator Conference · final project presentation
- **Week 15:** Graduate Practicum Portfolio Review · oral defense and remediation conference

## Lesson requirements

### 1. Practicum Authorization, Scope, Safety, and Confidentiality

This graduate lesson develops practicum authorization, scope, safety, and confidentiality within Advanced Applied Officiating Practicum. The learner must integrate current source authority, advanced visual evidence, ruleset-specific mechanics, complete administration, professional communication, film defense, and measurable applied performance.

**Graduate evidence required**

- Read the assigned current source and record version, effective date, definitions, conditions, exceptions, penalty, and related mechanics without copying protected wording.
- Write a 500–750 word graduate memorandum on practicum authorization, scope, safety, and confidentiality with a source map and a clearly labeled RefZone instructional analysis section.
- Create an original four-play comparison: legal, illegal, borderline, and correct no-call or no-correction.
- Tag two film clips at normal speed, identify angle limitations, and defend the ruling and mechanics with current authority.
- Record a three-minute oral defense and a ten-second game-floor communication.
- Submit one measurable applied-performance goal and the evidence that will verify improvement.

## Grading

- Graduate source seminar and lesson checks: 10%
- Complex case-play or rules memorandum: 20%
- Advanced film, mechanics, or analytics laboratory: 20%
- Midterm written and oral examination: 15%
- Applied communication, leadership, evaluation, or practicum performance: 15%
- Final graduate project and oral defense: 20%

## Required submissions

- Approved practicum agreement, safety plan, and confidentiality acknowledgment
- Baseline evaluation and individual performance plan
- Pregame, table, and crew-chief performance evidence
- Complete-game film and self-evaluation
- Rules, mechanics, and communication audit
- Evaluator conference and remediation evidence
- Verified graduate practicum portfolio

## Completion requirements

- Earn at least 85 percent for the graduate course.
- Pass every mandatory rules, penalty, safety, ethics, source-control, and professional-conduct competency.
- Complete all required written, case, film, communication, leadership, evaluation, or practicum submissions.
- Correct any superseded-source, ruleset-transfer, mechanics, or administration error identified during review.
- Pass the final graduate project and oral defense or complete assigned remediation and re-demonstration.

## Source, copyright, and academic-integrity standard

Official NFHS publications and current state, league, tournament, and assigning-authority policies control accuracy. RefZone University course text, scenarios, assessments, diagrams, dialogue, film prompts, and video scripts are original. Protected publication text, case language, mechanics diagrams, footage, logos, and likenesses are not reproduced without authorization. Any source conflict, missing local policy, or disputed ruling remains draft until qualified human review.
