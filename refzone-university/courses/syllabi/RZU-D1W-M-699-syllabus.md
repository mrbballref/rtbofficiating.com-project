# RZU-D1W-M 699 — Master’s Capstone, Comprehensive Examination, and Oral Defense

**Program:** NCAA Division I Women’s Basketball Officiating Master’s-Level Advanced Officiating Program  
**Academic period:** Year 2 · Term 4  
**Term theme:** Graduate Practicum, Capstone, Comprehensive Examination, and Oral Defense  
**Learning volume:** 3 RefZone graduate credit-equivalent units · 45 engagement hours  
**Prerequisite:** RZU-D1W-M 621  
**Credential status:** Professional graduate degree-level pathway; not an accredited degree or transferable graduate credit  
**Rules basis:** Current NCAA women’s playing rules for the 2025-26 and 2026-27 cycle, approved rulings, interpretations, replay and challenge guidance, authorized mechanics, and verified NCAA Division I Women’s basketball modifications and administrative procedures  

A graduate-level NCAA Division I Women’s basketball officiating course in master’s capstone, comprehensive examination, and oral defense, integrating current NCAA women’s playing rules, approved rulings, NCAA Division I conference and championship procedures, advanced mechanics, normal-speed film analysis, written rules work, practical performance, and oral defense.

## Graduate learning outcomes

- Analyze master’s capstone, comprehensive examination, and oral defense with current NCAA women’s playing-rules and Division I administrative authority.
- Resolve advanced legal-status, mechanics, administration, and communication problems without cross-ruleset contamination.
- Defend conclusions through original written analysis, normal-speed film, case-play work, and oral examination.
- Translate graduate study into measurable crew, evaluator, leadership, or game-performance behavior.

## Fifteen-week sequence

- **Week 1:** Capstone Orientation and Graduate Competency Audit
- **Week 2:** Advanced Rules Interpretation Portfolio Review
- **Week 3:** Complex Case-Play and Penalty-Sequencing Examination
- **Week 4:** Contact Judgment and Correct No-Call Film Defense
- **Week 5:** Three-Person Mechanics and Coverage Examination
- **Week 6:** Clock, Shot Clock, Media Timeout, and End-Game Examination
- **Week 7:** Replay, Coach’s Challenge, and Atypical-Play Examination
- **Week 8:** Game-Management and Conflict-Communication Defense
- **Week 9:** Crew-Chief Leadership and Crew Performance Defense
- **Week 10:** Evaluator, Observer, and Instructor Demonstration
- **Week 11:** Complete-Game Film and Performance-Analytics Defense
- **Week 12:** Applied Practicum Evidence and Remediation Audit
- **Week 13:** Professional Contribution Project
- **Week 14:** Master’s Comprehensive Examination
- **Week 15:** Final Oral Defense and Performance Portfolio Review

## Required graduate submissions

- Current-source graduate memorandum
- Original legal, illegal, borderline, and correct no-call or no-correction case set
- Normal-speed film analysis with angle limitations
- Mechanics, replay, communication, evaluation, or leadership artifact appropriate to the course
- Applied performance goal and verification evidence
- Final oral defense and remediation record

## Grading

- Graduate source memoranda and case analysis: 20%
- Film, mechanics, replay, or communication laboratories: 20%
- Applied performance and professional artifacts: 25%
- Midterm and comprehensive examination: 20%
- Final portfolio and oral defense: 15%

## Completion standard

Minimum 85 percent overall, completion of every mandatory competency gate, and successful oral defense or re-demonstration.
