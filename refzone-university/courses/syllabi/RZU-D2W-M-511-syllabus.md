# RZU-D2W-M 511 — Advanced Contact Judgment and Correct No-Call Philosophy

**Program:** NCAA Division II Women’s Basketball Officiating Master’s-Level Advanced Officiating Program  
**Academic period:** Year 1 · Term 1  
**Term theme:** Advanced Rules Interpretation, Complex Cases, and Judgment  
**Learning volume:** 3 RefZone graduate credit-equivalent units · 45 engagement hours  
**Prerequisite:** RZU-D2W-M 502  
**Credential status:** Professional graduate degree-level pathway; not an accredited degree or transferable graduate credit  
**Rules basis:** Current NCAA women’s playing rules for the 2025-26 and 2026-27 cycle, approved rulings, interpretations, replay and challenge guidance, authorized mechanics, and verified NCAA Division II women’s basketball modifications and administrative procedures  

A graduate-level NCAA Division II women’s basketball officiating course in advanced contact judgment and correct no-call philosophy, integrating current NCAA women’s playing rules, approved rulings, NCAA Division II conference and championship procedures, advanced mechanics, normal-speed film analysis, written rules work, practical performance, and oral defense.

## Graduate learning outcomes

- Analyze advanced contact judgment and correct no-call philosophy with current NCAA women’s playing-rules and Division II administrative authority.
- Resolve advanced legal-status, mechanics, administration, and communication problems without cross-ruleset contamination.
- Defend conclusions through original written analysis, normal-speed film, case-play work, and oral examination.
- Translate graduate study into measurable crew, evaluator, leadership, or game-performance behavior.

## Fifteen-week sequence

- **Week 1:** Graduate Contact-Judgment Framework for NCAA Women’s Basketball
- **Week 2:** Legal Status Before Contact and the Complete-Action Principle
- **Week 3:** Incidental, Marginal, Illegal, Unnecessary, and Excessive Contact
- **Week 4:** Ball-Handler Freedom of Movement and Defender Legality
- **Week 5:** Push-Offs, Ward-Offs, Hooks, Arm Bars, and Offensive Displacement
- **Week 6:** Post Play Physicality, Freedom of Movement, and Consistent Enforcement
- **Week 7:** Screening: Time, Distance, Movement, and Extended Body Parts
- **Week 8:** Cutters, Denial, Rerouting, and Off-Ball Freedom of Movement
- **Week 9:** Rebounding, Boxing Out, Landing Space, and Airborne Players
- **Week 10:** Shooters, Act of Shooting, Closeouts, Landing, and Body Contact
- **Week 11:** Block-Charge, Restricted Area, and Secondary Defenders
- **Week 12:** Hard Fouls, Head Contact, Flagrant Criteria, and Upgrade Decisions
- **Week 13:** Patient Whistles, Secondary Help, Double Whistles, and Correct Ownership
- **Week 14:** Correct No-Call Defense, Consistency, and Bias Control
- **Week 15:** Advanced Contact Film Defense Examination

## Required graduate submissions

- Current-source graduate memorandum
- Original legal, illegal, borderline, and correct no-call or no-correction case set
- Normal-speed film analysis with angle limitations
- Mechanics, replay, communication, evaluation, or leadership artifact appropriate to the course

- Film, mechanics, replay, or communication laboratories: 20%

Minimum 85 percent overall, completion of every mandatory competency gate, and successful oral defense or re-demonstration.
