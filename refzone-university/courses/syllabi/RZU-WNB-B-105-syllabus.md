# RZU-WNB-B 105 — Scoring, Four-Quarter Timing, Mandatory and Team Timeouts, Reset Timeouts, and Overtime

**Program:** WNBA Basketball Officiating Bachelor’s-Level Professional Foundations Program
**Units:** 5 · **Hours:** 75
**Prerequisite:** RZU-WNB-B 104
**Status:** Complete instructional production draft. Qualified WNBA rules, mechanics, replay, challenge, game operations, referee development, curriculum, copyright, accessibility, video, assessment, research, legal, safeguarding, and administrator review remain required before public publication.

A 5-unit bachelor’s-level professional foundations pathway course focused on scoring, four-quarter timing, mandatory and team timeouts, reset timeouts, and overtime, combining original RefZone instruction, official WNBA source study, normal-speed film or evidence analysis, communication, assessment, and applied professional performance.

## Learning outcomes
- Select and cite the controlling WNBA-specific authority, internal regulations, officiating manual, official interpretation or technical clarification, assignment directive, and underlying WNBA provision.
- Apply the course concepts using complete legal-status or evidence sequences.
- Create and solve original legal, illegal, borderline, atypical, and disciplined no-action scenarios.
- Use accurate mechanics, administration, communication, research, or policy methods.
- Defend conclusions at normal speed or against complete evidence and respond to qualified review.
- Complete the course performance task and remediation requirements.

## Lessons
1. Rule 5 field-goal and free-throw scoring, two-point and three-point value
2. Ten-minute periods, five-minute overtime, game-clock operation, and timing signals
3. End-of-period attempts, expiration of time, horn, red light, and timing corrections
4. Five regulation timeouts, overtime timeouts, mandatory-timeout structure, and limits
5. Reset Timeout purpose, eligibility, substitutions, advancement, and resumption
6. Timeout requests, mandatory lengths, time-in, official suspension, and administration
7. Source Map, Definitions, and Competency Targets: Scoring, Four-Quarter Timing, Mandatory and Team Timeouts, Reset Timeouts, and Overtime
8. Legal Status and Required Conditions: Scoring, Four-Quarter Timing, Mandatory and Team Timeouts, Reset Timeouts, and Overtime
9. Legal, Illegal, Borderline, and Correct No-Call Laboratory: Scoring, Four-Quarter Timing, Mandatory and Team Timeouts, Reset Timeouts, and Overtime
10. Mechanics, Primary Coverage, and Visual Evidence: Scoring, Four-Quarter Timing, Mandatory and Team Timeouts, Reset Timeouts, and Overtime
11. Penalty, Clock, Shot Clock, Possession, Replay, and Resumption: Scoring, Four-Quarter Timing, Mandatory and Team Timeouts, Reset Timeouts, and Overtime
12. Communication, Reporting, Film Review, and Course Assessment: Scoring, Four-Quarter Timing, Mandatory and Team Timeouts, Reset Timeouts, and Overtime
