# RZU-FIBW-M 532 — Advanced Applied FIBA Women’s Officiating Practicum

**Program:** FIBA Women’s Basketball Officiating Master’s-Level Advanced Officiating Program
**Academic sequence:** Year 2 · Term 4
**Units:** 3 RefZone credit-equivalent units
**Engagement hours:** 45
**Prerequisite:** Successful completion of prior-term requirements and FIBA Crew-Chief Leadership and Crew Performance
**Credential status:** Professional degree-level pathway; not represented as an accredited academic degree, FIBA licence, assignment credential, or transferable college credit
**Review status:** Complete instructional production draft. Qualified FIBA rules, OBRI, basketball equipment, mechanics, IRS, licensing, competition administration, commissioner, table-official, integrity, safeguarding, curriculum, copyright, accessibility, video, assessment, research, and program-administrator review remain required before public publication.

## Course description
A 3-unit graduate-style course focused on advanced applied FIBA women’s officiating practicum. It combines original RefZone instruction, official-source study, event-specific rules-code selection, film or evidence analysis, communication, assessment, and applied performance.

## Learning outcomes
- Select and cite the controlling FIBA competition-specific authority, internal regulations, officiating manual, official interpretation or technical clarification, assignment directive, and underlying FIBA provision.
- Apply the course concepts using complete legal-status or evidence sequences.
- Create and solve original legal, illegal, borderline, atypical, and disciplined no-action scenarios.
- Use accurate mechanics, administration, communication, research, or policy methods.
- Defend conclusions at normal speed or against complete evidence and respond to qualified review.
- Complete the course performance task and remediation requirements.

## Completion requirements
- Earn at least 80 percent for the academic course.
- Pass source, rules-code, effective-date, safety, ethics, and administration gates.

- Correct any wrong-source, superseded-source, or future-effective-rule error identified during review.
- Pass the final project and oral defense or complete assigned remediation and re-demonstration.
