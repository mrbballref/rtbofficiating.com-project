- Every Rule 1-14 topic, exception, penalty, and Comments-on-the-Rules topic assigned to the pathway is represented in the coverage matrix.
- No NBA, G League, NCAA, NFHS, or FIBA provision is imported without explicit WNBA authority.
- The 2027 Replay Center is labeled future-effective and not taught as the current 2026 workflow.
- Reset Timeout, mandatory timeout, late-game advancement, replay, and Coach’s Challenge provisions are internally consistent.
- Crew designations are distinguished from Lead, Trail, and Center positions.
- Every lesson uses original wording and original scenarios.
- Video production uses generic unbranded courts, uniforms, players, and officials unless separately licensed.
- Completion does not promise assignment, employment, advancement, licence, certification, accredited degree, or transferable credit.
