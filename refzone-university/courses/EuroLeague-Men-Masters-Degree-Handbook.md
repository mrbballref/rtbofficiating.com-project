# EuroLeague Men Master’s-Level Degree Handbook

**Program:** EuroLeague Men’s Basketball Officiating Master’s-Level Advanced Officiating Program
**Program code:** RZU-ELM-M
**Rules cycle:** 2026-27 transition cycle
**Courses:** 12
**Lessons:** 180
**Engagement hours:** 540

## Credential status

Professional degree-level pathway; not represented as an accredited academic degree or transferable college credit

## Program purpose

A complete 12-course professional master’s-level EuroLeague men’s officiating program covering EuroLeague adapted rules, bylaws, current and future-effective FIBA rules, casebook, mechanics, judgment, replay, communication, leadership, film, research, and professional practice.

## Source-control requirement

Every assignment begins with the EuroLeague season, bylaws, adapted rules, officiating manual, technical clarification, assignment directive, source version, and effective date. FIBA 2026 rules are future effective until October 1, 2026 and require confirmed EuroLeague implementation. Domestic-league or other competition procedures never override EuroLeague authority.

## Completion requirements

- Complete every required course and assigned prerequisite.
- Pass rules-code, source-version, effective-date, safety, ethics, timing, replay, and administration gates.
- Complete required film, mechanics, event, communication, leadership, research, or practicum work.
