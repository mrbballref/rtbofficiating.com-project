## Source-Control Rules

- No unofficial summary overrides a current official NCAA men’s playing-rules authority and NAIA administrative authority.
- No future-effective provision is taught as current before its effective date.
- No legal, ethics, research, statistical, technology, or equity claim is published without qualified source validation.
- All sources require version, date, license, access, evidentiary role, reviewer, approval, and next-review metadata.
- Source conflicts, inaccessible controlling pages, disputed interpretations, and unapproved research activity remain blocked.
- Course language teaches and paraphrases; it does not reproduce protected publications.
