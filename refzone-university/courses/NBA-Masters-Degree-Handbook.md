# RefZone University — NBA Masters Program Handbook

**Program:** NBA Basketball Officiating Master’s-Level Advanced Officiating Program
**Credential status:** Professional degree-level pathway; not represented as an accredited academic degree, transferable college credit, NBA licence, assignment qualification, employment credential, or advancement guarantee
**Season/source status:** 2025-26 NBA Official Playing Rules current published authority · 2026-27 playing rules and operations pending official publication
**Structure:** Two-year recommended sequence · four terms · 12 courses · 36 RefZone graduate credit-equivalent units
**Total engagement hours:** 540

A complete professional master’s-level advanced officiating pathway for NBA officiating, covering the 2025-26 NBA Rules 1-14, Comments on the Rules, mechanics, judgment, replay, Coach’s Challenge, communication, crew-chief leadership, arena operations, film, evaluation, research, and professional NBA basketball practice.
