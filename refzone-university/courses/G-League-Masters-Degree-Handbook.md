# RefZone University — NBA G League Masters Program Handbook

**Program:** NBA G League Basketball Officiating Master’s-Level Advanced Officiating Program
**Credential status:** Professional degree-level pathway; not represented as an accredited academic degree, NBA or NBA G League employment credential, assignment qualification, or transferable college credit
**Season/source status:** 2025-26 published playing authority · 2026-27 league operations current · 2026-27 playing-rule modifications pending official publication
**Structure:** Two-year recommended sequence · four terms · 12 courses · 36 RefZone graduate credit-equivalent units
**Total engagement hours:** 540

A complete professional master’s-level advanced officiating pathway for NBA G League officiating, covering NBA Rules 1-14, current G League experimental rules, mechanics, judgment, replay, challenges, communication, crew-chief leadership, game operations, film, evaluation, research, and development-league professional practice.
