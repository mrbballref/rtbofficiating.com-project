# RefZone University — NCAA Division II Women’s Basketball Officiating Master’s-Level Advanced Officiating Program

## Credential status
Professional graduate degree-level pathway. This program is not represented as an accredited master’s degree or as transferable graduate credit unless documented authority or an authorized university partnership is recorded in the CMS.

## Program at a glance
- Recommended length: 2 years
- Academic terms: 4
- Required courses: 12
- RefZone graduate credit-equivalent units: 36
- Total engagement hours: 540
- Graduate lessons: 180
- Required video-production packages: 180
- Required applied practicum: 1
- Culminating requirements: comprehensive examination, capstone, portfolio, and oral defense

## Admission prerequisite
Completed NCAA Division II Women’s Bachelor’s-Level Professional Foundations pathway or verified equivalent competency. Placement cannot be based on self-reported experience alone.

## Program purpose
Develop advanced NCAA women’s rules interpretation, complex penalty administration, collegiate contact judgment, three-person mechanics, clock and shot-clock administration, replay and coaches’ challenges, game management, crew-chief leadership, evaluator foundations, film analytics, applied performance, and defensible professional communication within the NCAA Division II women’s environment.

## Four-term curriculum
### Year 1 — Term 1: Advanced Rules Interpretation, Complex Cases, and Judgment
1. RZU-D2W-M 501 — Advanced NCAA Women’s Rules Interpretation and Division II Administration Seminar
2. RZU-D2W-M 502 — Complex Case Plays, Penalty Sequencing, and Correctable Errors
3. RZU-D2W-M 511 — Advanced Contact Judgment and Correct No-Call Philosophy

### Year 1 — Term 2: Advanced Mechanics, Timing, and Replay
4. RZU-D2W-M 512 — Advanced Three-Person Mechanics and Coverage Extensions
5. RZU-D2W-M 521 — Transition, Press, End-of-Game, Clock, and Shot-Clock Administration
6. RZU-D2W-M 522 — Replay, Coach’s Challenges, Appeals, and Atypical Plays

### Year 2 — Term 3: Leadership, Evaluation, and Analytics
7. RZU-D2W-M 601 — Advanced Game Management and Conflict Communication
8. RZU-D2W-M 602 — Crew-Chief Leadership and Crew Performance
9. RZU-D2W-M 611 — Evaluator, Observer, and Instructor Foundations
10. RZU-D2W-M 612 — Graduate Film Analysis and Performance Analytics

### Year 2 — Term 4: Practicum and Graduate Defense
11. RZU-D2W-M 621 — Advanced Applied NCAA Division II Women’s Officiating Practicum
12. RZU-D2W-M 699 — Master’s Capstone, Comprehensive Examination, and Oral Defense

## Graduate assessment model
Every course requires current-source memoranda, original complex case analysis, normal-speed film defense, applied mechanics or leadership work, professional communication, assessments with rationales, and oral explanation. The final program requires a comprehensive rules-and-mechanics examination, applied performance evidence, capstone portfolio, and oral defense.

## Completion standard
- Minimum 85 percent overall in each course
- Completion of all 36 RefZone graduate credit-equivalent units
- Passing performance on every mandatory rules, penalty, safety, ethics, mechanics, replay, and administration gate
- Successful practicum evaluation
- Successful comprehensive examination
- Approved capstone portfolio and oral defense
- Completion of required remediation and re-demonstration

## Source and publication controls
NCAA women’s playing rules control playing-rule instruction. NCAA Division II conference, consortium, tournament, championship, venue, media, replay, and assigning-authority procedures must be verified separately for the season and assignment. Course language, cases, diagrams, assessments, narration, and video scripts are original RefZone University materials and do not reproduce protected publications word-for-word.
