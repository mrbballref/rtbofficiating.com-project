About Raising The Bar Officiating

We Will Serve.
And Will Be Of Service To The Game.

Raising The Bar Officiating was built on a simple belief—that excellence, integrity, and education can elevate the standard of officiating and the game itself.

We are here to develop confident officials, support communities, and inspire the next generation of leaders on and off the court.

Founder

Our Founder. Our Foundation.

Raising The Bar Officiating was founded by a veteran official, trainer, and leader who understands the game from every angle. With decades of experience and a passion for people, our founder created this platform to raise the standard of officiating through education, mentorship, and opportunity.

His mission is simple: develop officials of high character and high performance—who will serve the game with pride and professionalism.
