Production Pipeline

Every program moves through a controlled workflow from idea to archive.

01

Define the editorial purpose, audience, format, resources and rights requirements before production begins.

02

Pre-Production

Build the rundown, book guests, schedule crew, prepare graphics, secure releases and organize assets.

03

Production

04

Post & Publish

Edit, review, caption, transcribe, confirm rights, attach metadata, publish, distribute and archive.
