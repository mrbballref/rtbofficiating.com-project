Membership Help

Before you subscribe.

How are paid memberships billed?

Choose monthly or annual billing before submitting the form. Paid checkout is created server-side and completed on Stripe Checkout. The recurring subscription continues until canceled.

Does this page store my card number?

No. This website sends the selected plan and member contact information to the secure server, which creates a Stripe Checkout Session. Payment details are entered into Stripe's hosted payment experience.

Can I join for free?

Yes. Listener is the free subscription level for network updates, new episode announcements, live reminders and public programming.

Can I change or cancel a paid membership?

Yes. The payment architecture is prepared for Stripe's subscription management workflow. A production customer portal can be enabled after the live Stripe account and account-authentication flow are connected.

Are podcast-directory subscriptions the same thing?

No. Following The Jammed Up Bar! in Apple Podcasts, Spotify or another directory is separate from membership on the owned Jammed Up Bar! network. Directory links are distribution channels; this membership belongs to the network itself.

When do premium benefits begin?

Paid access should be provisioned only after the server receives verified payment/subscription confirmation from Stripe. The included webhook workflow is designed for that purpose.
