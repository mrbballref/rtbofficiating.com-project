Inside The Show

Where the hard moments get unpacked.

The Jammed Up Bar! is designed for the situations that put officials under pressure: what happened, what the crew should see, how rules and mechanics apply, how the call should be communicated, and what everyone can learn from the play.

01

Tough Calls

Break down difficult plays and decision points from the officials’ point of view.

02

Mechanics

Discuss positioning, coverage, crew responsibilities and what creates or solves a jammed-up look.

03

Judgment

Examine legal versus illegal action, game context, timing, and officiating judgment.

04

Pressure & Culture

Talk honestly about coaches, partners, supervisors, advancement, communication and the culture of the profession.
