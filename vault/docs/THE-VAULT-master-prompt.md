# THE VAULT

## MASTER ENTERPRISE SPORTS VIDEO + TV MULTIMEDIA PRODUCTION PLATFORM DEVELOPMENT PROMPT

You are responsible for designing, engineering, developing, and preparing for production deployment an enterprise sports-video, film-analysis, scouting, media-management, collaboration, distribution, and television-production platform called:

# THE VAULT

The Vault is not simply a video library.

The Vault must operate and visually present itself as a **major sports multimedia company, digital broadcast network, film-analysis operation, post-production facility, production studio, video archive, scouting operation, and cloud media platform combined into one unified ecosystem.**

The goal is to create an original platform with the depth and capability class associated with leading sports-video systems such as:

- DVSport 360
- DVSport FilmRoom
- Hudl
- Hudl Sportscode
- Hudl Assist
- Synergy Sports
- Catapult video-analysis products
- professional coaching-film systems
- professional scouting platforms
- replay systems
- broadcast production environments
- digital asset management systems
- professional nonlinear video-editing workflows
- sports analytics systems
- cloud media-management systems

These products are references for required capability categories only.

**DO NOT copy another company's source code, proprietary layouts, trademarks, protected visual assets, proprietary algorithms, trade dress, or confidential workflows.**

Create an original The Vault product that competes through functionality, usability, workflow depth, production quality, and innovation.

# 2. PRIMARY COMPETITIVE POSITION

The Vault's product strategy must center on two major differentiators.

## ADVANTAGE ONE — THE PRODUCTION STUDIO

The entire application must feel like entering a major television sports-production facility.

Do not create a generic SaaS dashboard with dark cards.

Do not create an ordinary video website.

Do not create a YouTube clone.

The Vault must feel like:

**a broadcast control room + edit suite + film room + media archive + analytics room + scouting department + digital newsroom.**

## ADVANTAGE THREE — THE COMPLETE FILM WORKFLOW

The user should never need to leave The Vault to complete the normal lifecycle of sports video.

Provide an end-to-end workflow from media acquisition to final delivery.
