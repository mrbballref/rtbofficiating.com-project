Raising The Bar Officiating School Partner

University of Arkansas at Little RockWomen’s BasketballOfficiating School

Strengthen your command of the women’s game through a focused onsite school connected to UALR Women’s Basketball. The experience combines rules application, crew mechanics, communication, game management, film review, and evaluation for officials ranging from novice to experienced.

Register for this school →

What You’ll Gain

Master the Mechanics

Refine footwork, positioning, rotations, signaling, and crew movement.

Know the Rules

Deepen rules knowledge, interpretation, and situational awareness.

Game Experience

Participate in live-game situations, practical drills, and film breakdowns.

Expert Feedback

Receive direct coaching, evaluation, and actionable development priorities.

Career Growth

Build confidence, professional habits, and readiness for the next opportunity.

Professional Standards

Strengthen accountability, communication, leadership, and game management.

UALR Women

What to Expect

  • Rules ApplicationTranslate rules and points of emphasis into consistent decisions during real game situations.
  • Crew MechanicsImprove primary coverage, rotations, transition positioning, and crew communication.
  • Game ManagementDevelop poise, awareness, conflict management, and the ability to lead the game.
  • Video & EvaluationReview possessions with clinicians and receive clear, actionable performance feedback.
  • Advancement SkillsBuild the habits, presentation, and consistency required for future collegiate opportunities.

Who Should Attend?

  • New officials seeking structured training
  • High school officials preparing for collegiate expectations
  • Experienced officials refining game management and consistency
  • Officials who value detailed evaluation and measurable growth

Why Train With UALR Women?

Detailed Instruction

Learn the reasoning behind positioning, judgment, communication, and game-control decisions.

Collegiate Environment

Train in a setting that introduces the pace and expectations of higher-level basketball.

Focused Evaluation

Receive practical feedback centered on decisions that directly affect performance.

Growth Network

Connect with clinicians and officials who support continued development after the school.
